If you're looking for a new fun game to play with your friends, look no further. MemeAssist is the newest 4 player co-op game taking over the internet, with its unique use of AI voice cloning. It's very similar to Lethal Company or Repo where you have to find supplies to fix your tram and verse monsters along the way, but with the chaotic twist of not knowing if your friend is actually your friend. It's currently free on Steam so if you haven't tried it already, tag your friends and try it out for yourself.
