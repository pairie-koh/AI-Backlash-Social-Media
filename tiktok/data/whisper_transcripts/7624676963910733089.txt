Have you seen this viral video? The one with a powerful eagle dominating a desert world while desperate Persian cats fight for survival? It's an obvious portrayal of the Iran war. The message was crafted by Chinese state media to frame the U.S. as a reckless force attacking weaker countries and fueling chaos. It's a narrative Chinese TikTokers are also pushing. Experts say it's part of a larger and very deliberate political strategy. So the image presented is one of a steady, engaged and diplomatic China in contrast to an aggressive and unpredictable United States. The takeaway is clear. Beijing presents itself as the reasonable actor in a world disrupted by Washington's ambitions. All global developments geopolitically, no matter in what region, they are being filtered through the lens of how China can use these developments or how Chinese state-affiliated media apparatus can use these developments to strengthen the narrative that the US is a neo-imperial power and that it is a discredited actor.
