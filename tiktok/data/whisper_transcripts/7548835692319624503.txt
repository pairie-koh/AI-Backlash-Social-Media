Is AI training the same as stealing? Courts are about to decide the latest clash between AI and copyright. Now, music publishers are suing Anthropic, the company behind Claude, claiming it trained its AI on pirated song lyrics. Now, even worse, they say the AI spits those lyrics back out upon request. The legal question is huge. Is AI training on copyrighted works fair use, like learning from examples, or is it outright copyright infringement?
