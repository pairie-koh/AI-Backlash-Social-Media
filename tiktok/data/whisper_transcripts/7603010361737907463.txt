You can now do this effect on your phone. See, most AI tools are only accessible on your computer. But what if you don't have one? First, you need to download and install Polo AI. Click Text to Image and create your reference image. This is the character that you want to move. And then, film yourself doing the action. For example, talking to the camera. Like what I'm doing. Next, go back to Polo AI and click Mimic Motion. Upload the reference image and the motion video. The one you just took earlier. Or you can just select Ready-Made Presets. Choose Kling 2.6 as the model and then hit Create. Honestly, great job to the app creators for making it available for everyone. You can try it out on my LinkedIn bio.
