You do not need to start an AI company to make a ton of money in AI. Honestly, the best way to make money in AI is through a promotion. Become an AI expert, figure out what are the most impactful AI tools for your company, and implement those tools. Literally every company is thinking right now they need to implement AI. They have no idea where to start. But if you can be the one who can actually figure out what are the most time-consuming processes in a company and then find the most effective AI tools that solve that problem, you will be noticed by leadership. Because leadership is spending all their time thinking about AI and banging their head against the wall. At the end of the day, like if your job can be automated by AI, it will be. So you're best off becoming the person who can build those automations and get a promotion in the process.
