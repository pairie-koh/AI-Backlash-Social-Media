I think it's so weird when people in higher ed act like AI is the death of education, especially since people in education have been panicking about new technology for literally thousands of years. Socrates thought writing things down would make people intellectually lazy because they would stop memorizing things, which sounds crazy today. But he worried that students would, I think he said, like, cease to exercise memory or something like that and only appear wise because they had information written down for them, which, like, again, seems insane, but something we cared about at that time. Then the printing press came along. people worried that books would become too accessible, that people would read without guidance, misunderstand ideas, and spread bad information, which like still literally happens today. So whatever, right? And then calculators entered schools and that was a crazy time. People thought students would never learn real math because they would rely on machines to do the thinking for them. And like, I use my calculator for nine times seven and I have a PhD. So like, who cares, right? Then the word processor replaced handwritten essays and people thought students would become sloppy writers because they would just delete or move or rewrite everything instantly instead of carefully planning on paper or a typewriter, right? And like seems crazy, but like was still a thing. Then spell check came along and teachers said students would stop learning how to spell because the computer would do it for them. They weren't wrong because I'm pretty bad at spelling myself. But then Google happened and suddenly people said students would never actually learn anything because they could just look everything up. Now we're at AI. Now people think students will stop thinking, stop writing, stop researching, stop learning entirely. But every single time, education adapts. So like, what are we scared of? We do not ban the technology forever. We learn how to use it better. The students who do well are not the ones who like refuse to touch technology. I think that should not be a thing at all. The ones who succeed are the ones who learn how to use it critically and intelligently.
