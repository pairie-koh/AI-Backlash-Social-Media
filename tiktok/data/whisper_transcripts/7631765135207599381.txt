JGPT or Claude? Which AI should you use to learn English? I tested both and here is my honest answer. Both are great but they are different. JGPT is like that friend who always has an answer. Fast, confident, lots of examples. It's great for vocabulary, translations and quick questions. Claude is more like a patient teacher. It explains things in more detail, better at giving feedback on your writing. So, which is better for English learners? For speaking practice, use ChatGPT. For writing feedback and detailed grammar explanations, use Quote. My recommendation? Use both. They are free. Ask the same question to both and compare their answers.
