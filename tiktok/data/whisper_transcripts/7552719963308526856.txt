Climate change is one of the most serious challenges in the world today. Temperatures are rising, ice is melting, and extreme weather is becoming more common. Many animals are losing their habitats, and people are facing floods or long droughts. To fight climate change, governments and individuals must work together. Using renewable energy like solar or wind power, reducing plastic waste, and protecting forests are important steps. Everyone can help by saving electricity, recycling, and using eco-friendly products. If we act now, we can still protect the Earth for future generations.
