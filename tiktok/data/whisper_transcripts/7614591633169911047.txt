Did you know your own brain is one of the biggest mysteries in science? Here are the top 5 craziest facts. Fact 1. Although it makes up only 2% of your body weight, your brain consumes 20% of all the oxygen and energy you take in. Fact 2. It contains roughly 86 billion neurons, about the same number as stars in the Milky Way galaxy. Fact 3. The brain itself cannot feel pain. This is why brain surgery is sometimes performed on patients who are wide awake. Fact 4. Your brain generates approximately 23 watts of electrical power, enough to power a small light bulb. Fact 5. Scientists now believe that most people Only use about 10% of their brain's true potential, and the remaining 90% stays inactive.
