AI is hungry. To feed it, tech giants are building their own private power plants, ditching the public grid. From Microsoft to Amazon, they're bypassing aging infrastructure to ensure 24-7 uptime for their GPUs. But experts warn, going off-grid creates a reliability nightmare. What happens when a private plant fails? Then there's the climate risk. Some are reviving old coal or gas plants just to keep the AI humming. The fix, hybrid microgrids, integrating small modular nuclear reactors with renewable storage. Collaboration is key. Tech firms must invest back into the public grid to stabilize energy for everyone.
