The global race for artificial intelligence is hitting a physical wall. We are building digital brains at an industrial scale, but the electricity required to power them is skyrocketing. Large data centers are now consuming power at rates previously unimaginable, straining regional grids across the world. We are rapidly approaching a moment where our demand for computing firepower exceeds our ability to generate the energy needed to run it. But nobody is talking about this. Silicon Valley is pouring billions into performance, yet the infrastructure underneath is failing to keep pace. The dream of limitless intelligence is colliding with the reality of finite power.
