The rise of artificial intelligence. Artificial intelligence, AI, is changing the way we live and work. From voice assistants like Siri to self-driving cars, AI is becoming more common. AI systems can learn from data and make decisions, often faster than humans. In healthcare, AI helps doctors diagnose diseases. In business, it improves customer service and predicts trends. However, AI also raises concerns about job loss and privacy. Governments and companies are now discussing ethical ways to use AI. As AI grows, learning how it works becomes more important for future jobs and education.
