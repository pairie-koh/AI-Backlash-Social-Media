Researchers at Keele University have created an AI software that can detect fake news with an astounding 99% accuracy. Their hope with developing this technology is to help stop the spread of misinformation from news sources all across the world. One of the main researchers on this project stated, the risk posed by misinformation, disinformation, or fake news to the credibility of online news platforms, particularly on social media, highlights the urgent need for innovation solutions. Now, he went on to say, we aim to enhance the capabilities of our AI solutions through further research to help nip this problem in the bud.
