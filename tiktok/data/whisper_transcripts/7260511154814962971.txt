AI can take people under control in 2050. Today, many scientists and philosophers are looking at the rise of artificial intelligence as a potential risk to humans or even humanity as a whole. Some are raising serious concerns over the encoding of social biases like racism and sexism into computer programs, but more and more thinkers are rightly speaking out about the possibility that future AI should be afraid of us. I really think we're in trouble in the AI case, because we're unlikely to find ourselves in a position where it's clearly the best explanation for what we're seeing, that the AI is conscious. We should create an AI rights movement before any of the above damage is done. AI tends to be wrapped in mystery, because of the, black box problem. We hardly even understand how our own minds work. We could design an AI bill of rights not because the AIS today need one but because, by the time they do need one, it may be too late. Digital beings may someday far outnumber the number of beings on earth today. And given the enormous amount of potential suffering, we can't afford to ignore it. That doesn't mean those working on ending global poverty or factory farming need to start working on securing AI rights. It simply means that it's a topic that should be on society's radar and deserves much more serious attention than it's currently being given.
