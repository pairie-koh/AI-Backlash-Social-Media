AI sounds like it understands you, but it actually doesn't. When we say AI understands, we're simplifying something much more complex. For humans, meaning comes from experience, context, and emotion. When you read a sentence, you connect it to real life. AI does something very different. It analyzes patterns and predicts what comes next. It doesn't understand meaning, it matches patterns that look like meaning. That's why it can sound correct, even when it misses the context. AI works with structure. Humans work with meaning.
