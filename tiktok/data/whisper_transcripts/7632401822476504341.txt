For this project, the NoWork project, I created multiple sections inside the admin panel. This is where everything gets managed by the scenes. The first key section is the overview section and this is where the main dashboard lies. It gives you a quick summary of everything that is happening in the website. You can actually see the bookings, customers, and general activities without digging into details. The second section is the bookings, and this is where all customers' bookings show up. You can see who booked, what they selected, the date, and the time that they booked their services. So instead of just guessing or checking the messages, everything is organized and in one place. The second section is the customers' panel or the customers' section, and this shows everyone who has interacted with your website. This is important because now the business can actually track and understand their customers. The fourth section is the review or revenue section. This one is powerful because it shows you how much the business is making from bookings or orders and it breaks everything down over time. So you can start to see the patterns and growth of your business. These four are the core that make the whole system work.
