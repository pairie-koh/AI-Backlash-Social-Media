AI bubble explained by cats. Kitty loves new toys, laser pointers, catnip mice, and now AI. Suddenly everything is AI. AI yarn, AI scratching posts, AI litter boxes, even AI fish. Humans see this and go, wow, the future. They start throwing treats everywhere. Money into AI startups, chips, data centers, money into anything that meows AI. Everyone thinks they've found infinite tuna. But Kitty notices something. Not everything that smells nice is tuna. That's an AI bubble. A bubble happens when hype gets way bigger than what the tech can actually do today. Does that mean AI is useless? No. Kitty knows AI is powerful when used correctly. Some companies will survive. Some will crash. Some just glued AI on the box. When reality arrives, the bubble pops. The market cries. The hype fades. But tech stays. Kitty shrugs and finds her actual food bowl. Kitty keeps building. Kitty ignores the noise and goes for real value.
