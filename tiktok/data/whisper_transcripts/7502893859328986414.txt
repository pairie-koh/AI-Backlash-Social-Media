Early humans were way more terrifying than you actually think. We had one of the highest levels of endurance compared to nearly every living being on the planet. But why was this scary? Well, humans used to practice something called persistence hunting, using our incredible stamina to chase down prey in a horrifying way. Knowing we couldn't catch the animal at full strength and speed, we would slowly follow it instead, where no matter how far the animal ran, they would turn around and keep seeing these hairless creatures constantly following them. Even after multiple hours of running, thinking they finally got away, the creatures would just keep appearing over and over. Humans would never get tired, forcing the animal to run until their body physically shuts down, where your only choice was to lay down from exhaustion and let them spear you to death.
