Everyone is talking about AI, how it will take jobs, how it will replace people. They are right. But they are asking the wrong question. The question is not, will AI replace you? The question is, what are you that cannot be replaced? A machine can process information. It cannot earn trust. It cannot inspire loyalty. It cannot look a person in the eye and make them feel seen. those things only a human can do. And in a world where everything is automated, the most valuable person in any room will be the one who has mastered the oldest skills in the world. Reading people. Building trust. Leading with wisdom. The skills I learned on the streets of Calabria are the same skills that will matter most in 2026 and beyond. Ascolta bene.
