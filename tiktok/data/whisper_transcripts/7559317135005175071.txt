What concerns me the most about AI in education is that students already have a hard time and have not learned about information literacy and understanding and checking sources. It's something, a skill that we develop over time and we learn. Then bring in the intersection of artificial intelligence and the easy access of it. They are taking what this generative AI gives them as word, word of God, basically, and not questioning it, not asking for sources. So as we see the uprise of the usage of AI in education, what we are also seeing is the decrease in understanding information literacy and the skills of understanding what peer reviewed literature is, what research is, and what a credible source is.
