AI agent versus chatbot. An AI agent and a chatbot might seem similar, but they're not the same. A chatbot mainly answers questions based on predefined responses or simple AI. An AI agent goes further. It can make decisions and take actions, not just reply. For example, a chatbot might answer a question, but an AI agent can answer, qualify the lead and book an appointment. So the difference is simple. Chatbots talk. AI agents act. That's why AI agents are becoming the next step beyond chatbots.
