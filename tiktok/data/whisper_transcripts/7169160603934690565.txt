The US is finally regulating AI. President Biden has recently announced five principles that are supposed to protect the American population from the threats of artificial intelligence. Number one, safe and effective systems. AI systems need to be effective. They need to actually do what they're supposed to do well. And to make sure that these systems work well, AI developers need to integrate and involve different stakeholders in the design process, which I love. This is how technology should be developed. by making tech people work together with experts of the domain. That's how good tech gets built. Number two, algorithmic discrimination protection. AI systems should take into account the potential biases they may have and protect minorities to try to prevent biases from arising. Number three, data privacy. People should know if their data is used to train AI systems. Number four, notice and explanation. People should know when AI systems are used to take decisions for them or when they're interacting with an AI agent. And number five, human alternatives and fallback. When it makes sense, people should always have the opportunity to talk to a human being and have a person evaluate their case instead of having AI taking a decision for them.
