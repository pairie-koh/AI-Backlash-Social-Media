A wave of AI job losses is turning into a tsunami as thousands of roles are wiped out. A new survey reveals how cynical many workers are about their future careers, but recruitment experts say businesses can turn this around. For 17 years, Jackie Duncan has made a career from her voice. I even waited to the letterbox. But the actor and voiceover artist is now among many creatives whose work is being replaced by artificial intelligence. I have definitely seen a dramatic downshift in the amount of work that I'm getting. A new survey from talent agency Randstad has found one in three Australians believe their job will disappear in the next five years because of AI. AI will certainly change roles and definitely the skills needed to perform in roles. Still job losses are ramping up. Block, the company behind Afterpay, announcing cuts to 40% of its global workforce, including in Australia. Envato, a Melbourne-based creative marketplace, slashing 200 jobs. WiseTech Global slashing nearly a third of its workforce. Investment giant Morgan Stanley looking to shed around 2,500 positions. And another 200 roles are set to go at Telstra. Randstad's report shows millennials, those aged between 30 and 45, are most worried about their jobs disappearing. And over half of those surveyed had not received training on AI tools that will reshape their jobs.
