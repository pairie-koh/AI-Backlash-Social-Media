So a lot of people are familiar with Alex Armosi, and one of the models that he tells you to run to work on delegation is a 15-minute method where you're taking a note every 15 minutes on a timer for your entire workday. The reason that you run a 15-minute test is to really see what you're doing throughout the day, what work you can delegate out. But what hasn't been brought to anyone's attention yet is this is how you do it for AI. When you're automating your work with AI, go about it the exact same way. run a timer 15 to 30 minutes and you write down what you did in the last 15 to 30 minutes every single time it goes off and then you get to see where your time's actually being used it only takes one work day it will be annoying but this one work day that you sacrifice to writing down notes about what you're doing will be the building plan to automating your work
