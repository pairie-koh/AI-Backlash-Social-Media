Why are data centers the most important buildings of the 21st century? They don't look futuristic, but data centers are the real powerhouses of the AI revolution. With $180 billion set to be invested in 2025 alone, they're expanding faster than ever. AI is hungry for power, water and storage. The more we rely on AI, the more data centers we need. Microsoft, Meta and OpenAI are racing to build bigger, more efficient centers. Some are even considering nuclear energy and underwater cooling to keep up with demand.
