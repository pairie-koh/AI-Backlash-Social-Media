We're at CES 2026 checking out Saibopow 1 and it's like having Jarvis from Iron Man on your desk. This is the world's first active tracking monitor that combines a 6 axis robot, 4K display, and AI interaction to create a next gen terminal for AI PCs. It has intelligent sight tracking that follows your head movements to reduce neck strain, with a 700mm arm reach that lets you go from full standing position to laying position.
