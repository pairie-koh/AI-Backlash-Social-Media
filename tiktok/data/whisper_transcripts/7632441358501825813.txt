The CEO of OpenAI has sent an apology letter to the community of Tumblr Ridge following February's mass shooting. The company failed to report the mass shooter's chat GPT account after it was banned for inappropriate use. Many were waiting for this letter to be sent, and today we have it. Here's a snippet of what Sam Altman wrote. Quote, I am deeply sorry that we did not alert law enforcement to the account that was banned in June. While I know words can never be enough, I believe an apology is necessary to recognize the harm and irreversible loss your community has suffered. Later on in the letter, Altman writes that it was agreed in his meetings with various officials that an apology was necessary, but that some time had to pass to allow the community to grieve. He writes that he can't imagine anything worse than losing a child. He tells the victims, their families, the community and the province that his heart is with them and that he remains committed to finding ways to prevent tragedies like this in the future. BC's premier David Eby responded to this in a post on X. Here's what he had to say was brief, but pretty powerful. His response, the apology is necessary and yet grossly insufficient for the devastation done to the families of Tumblr Ridge. We will continue to stand with Mayor Daryl Krakoa and the people of Tumblr Ridge in a difficult work ahead.
