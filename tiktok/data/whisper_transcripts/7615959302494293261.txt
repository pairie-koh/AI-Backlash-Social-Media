Artificial intelligence tools are better than ever, they're widely available, often free, and so social media is a cesspool of fakery right now. There's this fake video supposedly showing a group of US troops captured in Iran, and this fake video supposedly showing an Iranian military facility being blown up, and this fake video supposedly showing people fleeing an Iranian attack on an Israeli airport. Now in that last one there are people in suitcases moving in weird ways, a telltale sign of an AI creation, but other fakes of the Iran war are not so easy to spot. And there are dozens of them going around. The trickery isn't all AI either. There's footage of incidents from other countries being presented as being from Iran or Israel, footage of old incidents being shared as new, even clips from war video games being depicted as real clips. So what can the average person do? My top tip is just take an extra second of thinking or reading before you share one of these war videos. Ask yourself, does anything just seem off about it? Out of sync audio and video, oddly shaped objects, material that doesn't match what we know about the war. Ask yourself, has any credible media outlet or subject matter expert confirmed or debunked this clip? What about people in the comments or replies? Have they raised skeptical questions? And AI detection tools? No, they're not perfect, but they can help. Look, this stuff is hard.
