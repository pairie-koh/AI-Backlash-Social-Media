AI is just another bubble. It'll pop like .com. That completely misses the point. Even if AI investment disappeared tomorrow, the technology isn't going anywhere. When people say AI bubble, they're usually referring to a financial bubble. Money, stock prices, valuations, which granted right now are insane. But what's different is that the models are real. Their capacity for coding, math, research, that doesn't go anywhere when investors run scared. Think about it. The .com bust didn't get rid of the internet. It spelled the end of a lot of companies, but the technology kept improving. Stop thinking about AI progress in terms of share price and start thinking about it in terms of compute, data, and algorithms. All three keep advancing. So the next time someone says AI bubble, ask them if the AI gets any less capable when the stock market dips. Financial bubble, sure. Technology, not so much.
