If you've vibe-coded an app, if you've built an app using AI, you may think that once you build and test and deploy your app, you're done. But this is not true, especially if you have users that are paying a monthly fee, a subscription, if you're building a SaaS. In this case, there's much more work to be done after the app is deployed. And I want to talk about one of those, and that is supporting your app. Yes, supporting your app happens after the app is deployed, but the plan for supporting your app has to come before it's deployed. It has to come during the design and the build phase. You can add it on later and many of you will need to, but this adds some complexity. So what things do you need to be thinking about before you ever deploy? Well, think about basic logging. Think about things like performance metrics for some key metrics. Think about tracking errors within context. You see, all of these will help you see what's happening inside your app and that will help you support it. You'll also want to think about things like features. What does your roadmap look like? What do you plan to add? Many times you'll gain users not necessarily by the features that you have already, but by what you're planning to add in the coming months.
