If you ever visit Indonesia, you have to try this strange machine that scans your face. At first it looks like an ordinary photo booth, but it's actually much more high-tech. This boy stands in front of the scanner, and just a few seconds later, his face appears on a huge screen, where visitors can scan and analyze their face using AI. The crazy part is the system compares his face with millions of faces across the country using AI. When it finds a match, the photo shows up right away, and that's how the boy found someone who looks exactly like him.
