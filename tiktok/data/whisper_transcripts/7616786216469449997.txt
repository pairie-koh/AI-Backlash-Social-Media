The AI bubble might be closer to popping than we think. Of course, we still have data centers being built left and right, but there are still some signs that even the big companies are taking the hint. Think about it. We've got Mozilla doing a hard pivot away from their AI features by adding the Block AI Enhancements feature on their latest update. And sure, they still have the features accessible, but the fact that they listened to consumers and gave the option means that somebody higher up has to agree with us. But then you have companies like Microsoft that are throwing Copilot on literally everything, no matter how many angry comments we leave them, which is why you see more and more people moving to Linux. Because weirdly enough, companies kind of need customers to stay afloat. Who knew?
