Google just admitted their AI made a mistake that could have killed someone. A user asked Gemini for health advice, and the AI told them to eat rocks. Actual rocks. The response recommended adding small rocks to food for minerals. Google quickly apologized and removed the answer. But here's the wild part. This wasn't a glitch. It was the AI confidently pulling bad information from a satirical article and presenting it as medical fact. This reveals something crucial about artificial intelligence right now. These systems don't actually understand what they're saying. They're pattern-matching machines that can sound incredibly convincing while being dangerously wrong. And we're rushing to integrate them everywhere. Search engines, healthcare, education, financial advice. The controversial truth nobody wants to hear?
