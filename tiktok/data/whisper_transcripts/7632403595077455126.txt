Your phone runs a 4-bit model right now, but not everything is 4-bit. Weights are easy to quantize. They are fixed after training. You measure their distribution once, calculate a scale factor once, and you're done. W4, weights in 4 bits. Activations are a different problem entirely. Activations change with every single input. A question about cats produces completely different activation values, than a question about nuclear physics. You cannot pre-calculate their scale factor. You don't know the range until the model is already running. This is why most phones today run W4A16. Waits at 4 bits, activations stay at 16. But 16-bit activations still consume memory, still slow things down. So engineers asked, can we quantize activations too? Turns out you can. But there's a catch. And that catch changes everything.
