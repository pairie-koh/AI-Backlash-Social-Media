Jeff Bezos says the AI bubble is real, but so is the technology. When investors are pouring billions into an AI company of six people without a product and already valuing it at $20 billion, we are in a bubble. However, that doesn't mean the technology itself and the progress we're making is useless. For example, the dot-com bubble was a real financial bubble, but the technology itself was lasting. We are all on the web today. AI is going to impact every company in the world.
