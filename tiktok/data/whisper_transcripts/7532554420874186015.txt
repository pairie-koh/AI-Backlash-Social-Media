The hardest part of learning any creative skill at all is honestly just taking the first steps at actually trying to paint, trying to write something. Every single artist that you love, every single writer that you follow, whose books you care about, every single director who you admire, they all have 200 first projects that are absolute shit that they don't even want to think about, that they don't want to touch with a 10-foot pole. I have a book that I wrote in fifth grade on loose sheets of scrap paper that makes me want to tear my hair out to think about. Ugh. I don't know what social media platforms use other than TikTok, but I can tell you that on Tumblr there are tons of different writers groups that are entirely just meant as a space for new writers to talk with each other and to learn how to write better. If you want to do something in person, check out your local bookstore. Lots of them have writers' cafes and all sorts of events that might be helpful for you. This is a great first step and you should be really proud of this.
