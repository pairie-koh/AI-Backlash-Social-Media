On tonight's episode of Old Games Need Love Too, we have Power Grid. Power Grid is an economic game for 2-6 players by Rio Grande Games. In Power Grid, players compete to power as many cities as possible by managing their resources to bid on power plants, purchase fuel for those plants, and build houses to interconnect cities. At the end of each round, players will see how many cities they can power with the fuel that they have in their reserves. The more cities that you can power, the bigger the payday. Between expanding the power network, buying power plants to support them, and the fluctuation of cost of fuel, resource management is key. Play continues until one player has connected the indicated number of cities in their network. That number is determined by the number of players for the game. The player with the most interconnected cities is the winner. I first played this game many years ago, and I think it still holds up pretty well today. This has got to be the first economic game ever played, and I still would bring this out to teach people that are new to economic style games. Trying to manage your resources just enough to power the cities that you have while trying to expand is a lot of fun My favorite aspect of the game has to be the bidding on power plants It's an auction style.
