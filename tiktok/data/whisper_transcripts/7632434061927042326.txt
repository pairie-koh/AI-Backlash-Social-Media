Why do some people get treated like garbage while others get praised for doing absolutely nothing? It all comes down to the halo effect. Imagine a street pigeon landing on your balcony. You'd instantly react in disgust and shoo it away. But if it was a white dove, you'd probably smile and take a picture. They're the exact same bird, but you praise one purely because it looks beautiful. We do the exact same thing to humans. When someone is attractive, our brains project an invisible halo over them. We instantly assume they're smart, funny, and innocent, completely ignoring their massive red flags. But if someone loses the genetic lottery, life gets ten times harder. People will literally assume bad intentions and treat them like a threat just for existing. Everyone loves to preach that looks don't matter. But the brutal truth is, before you even speak, society has already decided exactly who you are.
