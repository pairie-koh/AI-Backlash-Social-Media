I think I might have found a way to eliminate those annoying AI videos from your feed. Give it a try and let me know if it works for you. Click on the three lines in the top right corner and go to Settings and Privacy. Then you'll look for Content Preferences. Then you will select Filter Keywords. Add AI to your keywords. turn on the smart filter and decide what all you want to filter those videos from. I did it tonight and I haven't seen one single AI video since. Good luck.
