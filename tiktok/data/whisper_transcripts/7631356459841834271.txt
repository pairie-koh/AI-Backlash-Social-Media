Use prompting framework when writing all your prompts. Start with a task in mind, which is what you want done, and then add a context, which is the goal and some background. Then add audience, which specifies who it is for. Then add constraints and the output format. As simple as that. Vague input equals average output. Specific input means targeted output. Every detail you add eliminates one guess the AI has to make. Now, what is the rule? Replace every vague noun with a concrete one. Just like how Priya replaced presentation to 10 minutes investor pitch and help me to write five headlines, you can increase your chance of getting better response out of your AI tools.
