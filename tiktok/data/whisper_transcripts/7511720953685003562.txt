AI has a huge energy problem, and that problem is not going away. AI data centers are going to double their energy usage by 2030. We're talking about consuming as much electricity as the entire country of Japan. Meta just signed a $20 billion deal to keep a nuclear power plant running to power their data centers until 2047. It may be all well and good to say that nuclear is the future of power for AI, but big tech is making these nuclear promises. And guess what's actually powering AI right now? Natural gas. Lots of it. Meta's betting on nuclear plants that won't be ready until the late 2030s to solve an energy crisis that's happening right now. It's like going and ordering a fire truck when your house is already burning down. The real question isn't whether nuclear can work eventually. The question is the timeline. Can we wait for eventually? And is AI going to break all of its promises in the meantime?
