All right, question of the day, is AI ruining education? And in my opinion, I feel like it just depends on what level of education we're talking about and how the AI is being used. For example, if we have kids in elementary school, even middle school, using AI on their assignments, on stuff you're supposed to actually learn and need in life, like addition, simple multiplication, then yeah, it's cooking education. But if we're talking about high school or college students using it on assignments, they don't really need and seems like busy work, then no, I don't think it's ruining it. Because at the end of the day, as long as you can still think for yourself, retain information, formulate your own thoughts, and you know how to critically think, then AI isn't ruining anything. You're just using it to make your life easier on work that you don't really need. And even when it comes to writing essays, like I can write a perfectly fine essay, but sometimes I'll still use AI just because it's less time consuming, I'll still get the grade and this essay does not determine what I'm going to be in life. So in conclusion, I just think it's a case by case basis and you can't have an absolute answer.
