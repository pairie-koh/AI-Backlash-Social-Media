Vietnam new AI law officially went into effect on March 1st, 2026. What is prohibited is AI designed for deception or manipulation. This includes a prohibition for deep fakes. Then we have a four-tier risk system. At the top, unacceptable, immediate bans on systems-threatening national security or human dignity. Then we have the other level, which is high. critical sectors, finance, healthcare, education, require registration in a national database, and pre-market assessments. Then we have Medium, which focuses on transparency and ensuring the users know that they are interacting with AI. And then we have the Low, which is subject to minimal oversight and voluntary best practices. We also have strict labeling, AI-generated audio, images, and video, must-carry machine, readable labels to distinguish them from reality and prevent public confusion. Then we have the extraterritorial reach. Foreign companies serving the Vietnamese market must comply.
