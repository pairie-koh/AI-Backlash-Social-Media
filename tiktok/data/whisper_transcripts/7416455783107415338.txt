I'm a former teacher and I can tell you what the future of education is. If you don't already know, schools are falling apart because teachers are leaving left and right because of various issues mostly related to child behavior and no support from administration. This is especially the case with substitutes. They're barely around anymore, which is making more work for teachers. Also, it's not like teachers' salaries are going up, but they won't have to because here comes AI. I'm not even talking about a very distant future. in a near future. AI will, there will be a special AI for each individual student. They will teach them all subjects at their own pace. They'll grade them and the need for a teacher is out the window. Teachers will be babysitters. Maybe in some cases they'll be tutors, but their job will just be to make sure that the kid stays with the AI and progresses the way they're supposed to. You'll see a lot more uncertified adults that are in schools because it's just a job to get by for eight hours. And that's why teachers will never have to be paid more because they can just pay these people less with no certification or experience. And schools themselves will probably start to have less kids because any parents that work from home, if they can just set their kid up with the AI, what's the point of school? I was a phys ed teacher. Now that is a different ball game and obviously sports and the socialization that comes with all three of those things, but also lunch and just being around a bunch of other kids. But without those things, if you don't care about those things that much, why would you wouldn't even need to send your kid to school? I think they might eliminate phys ed altogether, to be honest with you. And they might make it so that you have to play a sport or something like that. So then kids would just show up to play the sport. But I'm telling you right now that this is right around the corner. I'm about to have my first daughter in December and I am 100% convinced that this is how school will be when she is of school age. This is my prediction anyway. Could be wrong. Curious your thoughts.
