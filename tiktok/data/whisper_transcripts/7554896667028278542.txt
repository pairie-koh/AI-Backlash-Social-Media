America's power grid is running on a thin margin, just 15% above demand. That's the slim safety net standing between reliable power and rolling blackouts. Now here's the problem. Every new AI data center guzzles as much electricity as a small city. At the current pace, experts warn the U.S. Grid can only support around 500 more of these massive facilities before the safety buffer disappears. Yet, big tech is planning to build thousands. The question is, what happens if AI ends up overloading the very system that keeps the lights on?
