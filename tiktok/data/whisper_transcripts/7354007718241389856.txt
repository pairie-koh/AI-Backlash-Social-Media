Generous husband decided to remodel the last remaining room in the house like this. Firstly, he built a circular structure using several square tubes and welded some shorter ones in the middle to arrange 12 beds. He bought expansion screws with his pocket money to secure the beds firmly. He also got some eco-friendly solid wood panels to cover the beds with a special breathable structure that's comfy and doesn't trap heat. He brought home 12 mattresses from his aunt's place for the beds. Then, he constructed a low-brick cabinet with a flip panel on top for extra storage space. He installed switches and sockets near the beds, so the kids can easily turn off the lights without having to go downstairs. And it's also handy for reading before sleep. The cabinet has space above for storing clothes they wear, often making them easy to grab. Each child got their own cabinet to store their belongings before bed. Beds against the wall have soft covers for extra comfort, and the best part is the protective fence around the beds. Next, he used several wooden boxes to create steps, incorporating hidden light strips inside for ambiance and storage for books, ensuring no waste. He crafted a special wardrobe for the girl to hang and store her beautiful long skirts without them getting wrinkled. The wardrobe doors can be closed and the children take turns keeping watch, allowing everyone to move around freely without bumping into one another This setup ensures a peaceful sleeping environment for the kids The well girl gets the top bunk while the mischievous boy sleeps below Each child has their own, separate sitting area. He designated the two drawers by the window specifically for the girls, with the middle drawer for seasonal clothes, and the outer drawers for storing essentials like underwear and socks, making everything easy to find without clutter. A movable roller clothes hanger is placed centrally for the daughter's frequently worn clothes, making it easy to take and change outfits. This setup optimizes space usage effectively. For the boys, the two roller drawers next to the door are used to store their seasonal clothes and underwear with a middle roller for the clothes they wear often, ensuring easy access and organization for everyone. He placed square pipes against the wall, supported by several pillars, and covered them with environmentally friendly materials. The bottom section was turned into drawers for the children's study tables. Wooden boxes attached to the wall serve as seats, filled with seldom used books. With soft cushions on top, these boxes double as a sofa. He added small bookcases along the wall for all the college entrance exam prep materials, protected behind glass cabinet doors, both attractive and functional. He also borrowed two large desks from his second auntie to furnish the space, dedicating it as a learning area for all the children, complete with school supplies. This setup not only organizes the space efficiently, but also creates a conducive learning environment.
