So a technology that challenges maybe your beliefs, technology that brings context, that expands your worldview, technology that is optimized and focused on helping you understand what is real and what information really is something you can depend on and make decisions on. And right now, I think that conversation is very nascent. The tools that exist to support that are really at the base level. Our AI writing detector is a great example of that. As you're surfing the web, you can have CoolBot installed and you can just say, like, is this AI generated? And, you know, it'll say, like, yeah, this text feels pretty human. And that doesn't necessarily mean everything in it is right. But it does give you a sense of, well, if it's all AI generated, maybe I need to approach it with a little bit more skepticism, or at least it's a piece of context that I need to have.
