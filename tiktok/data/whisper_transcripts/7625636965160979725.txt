I've been hearing people say, stop using AI to save the polar bears. And I just, no. Stopping AI won't save the polar bears. Like, their homes are literally melting. The Arctic ice they live on is disappearing because of climate change. That's from humans burning fossil fuels, not because AI exists. Yeah, AI uses energy, and that can add to climate stuff if the energy comes from oil or gas. But even if AI disappeared tomorrow, climate change would still be happening. Cars, factories, oil, gas, that's the real problem.
