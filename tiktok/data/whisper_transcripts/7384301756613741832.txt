The use of AI is sucking up so much electricity that the demand is outstripping the available power supply in many parts of the world. One query to Google AI is roughly equal to talking on the phone for an hour. It also consumes about 10 times more energy than a traditional Google search. AI's environmental impact is worse than that of crypto. A single AI semiconductor chip that powers generative AI tools, specifically the H100 chip made by NVIDIA, consumes nearly eight times the power used by a typical 60-inch flat-screen TV. Even Microsoft admitted that its hunger for AI is affecting the company's goal to become carbon negative by 2030. Data centers built to run AI systems consume even more power. Bloomberg estimated that there are over 7,000 data centers built or in various stages of development globally. If these data centers ran constantly, they would collectively consume 508 terawatt hours of electricity per year. And that is greater than the total annual electricity consumption for Italy and Australia. AI is guzzling up our water resources too. For every 5 to 10 chat GPT problems, depending on level of complexity, about 500 milliliters of water is needed. By way of comparison, one single Google search needs only about half a milliliter of water. So when boomers on Facebook are praising AI art of shrimp Jesus and women with unrealistic body proportions, this is the cost.
