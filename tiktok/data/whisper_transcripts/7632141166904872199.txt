75% of all code written at Google is now AI generated. This is up from 25% in 2024 and 50% last year. At this point, I don't know a single software engineer who still writes code completely manually. Everyone uses AI in some capacity. If you're a corporate worker, more than likely it is part of your performance goals to actually incorporate AI into your workflow. Microsoft CTO said that 95% of its code would be written by AI within five years. Same story at Meta. They are requiring 65% of their employees to write over 75% of their code with AI. Five months ago, AI was pretty good, but some of the top engineers still thought it was a little sketchy. But at this point, pretty much everyone agrees. Unless you are using AI, you are definitely going to fall behind.
