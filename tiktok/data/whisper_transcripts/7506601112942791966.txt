When a former teacher named Hannah posted a farewell video on TikTok, venting her frustrations about tech in the classroom, it didn't take long for the story to go viral. Her message hit a nerve, especially when Fox News picked it up, giving her a national platform to claim that tech is ruining kids' education. But let's slow the scroll for a second. While I respect Hannah's frustrations, and frankly I share many of them, I have to challenge the narrative that AI is the villain. It's not. The real villain? Unchecked access. Lack of boundaries. The absence of responsible guidance from adults.
