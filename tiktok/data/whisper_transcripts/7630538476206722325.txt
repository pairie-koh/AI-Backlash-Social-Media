The United States is considering increasing uranium imports from Namibia to fuel nuclear plants powering AI data centers, Ambassador John Giordano said. Giordano added the U.S. could support new mining output through American companies and government financing. The U.S. imported 633 metric tons of uranium from Namibia in 2024, compared with 8,413 tons by China, according to World Integrated Trade Solution data. Namibia is the world's third-largest uranium producer after Kazakhstan and Canada, with Chinese firms dominating the sector per the World Nuclear Association. Interesting?
