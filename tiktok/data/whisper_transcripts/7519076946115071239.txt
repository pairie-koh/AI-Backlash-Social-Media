What if every time you use ChatGPT, a part of your brain goes to sleep? A recent MIT study tracked 54 students over four months with EEG scans while they wrote essays with ChatGPT, Google, or no tools. The ChatGPT group showed significantly lower brain activity, struggled to recall their own writing, and produced much less original work. Even when switching back to writing unaided, their brain engagement stayed depressed. Researchers call it metacognitive laziness, outsourcing thinking to AI, and shrinking your mental muscle. The takeaway? Sure, AI speeds things up, but at what cost to memory, creativity, and critical thinking? Use AI as a tool, not a crutch. Challenge yourself first, then let the bot polish.
