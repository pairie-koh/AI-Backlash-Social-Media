One of the data points that worry me the most about teens AI usage is that we're seeing that kids from low-income backgrounds are actually the heaviest users of AI for schoolwork and homework, yet they're the least likely to be in schools that actually have rules or some type of guidance for how to use AI tools. This is important to me because it means that the default decisions that we have in designs really do matter. It means that the risk of things like cognitive offloading or students just completely losing motivation to study or to learn because they have AI tools is disproportionately affecting a very specific group of people. So if you're someone who's building an AI product, I encourage you to read the data. It's here in my sub stack. And if you're someone who works with teachers and parents, something for them to be aware of as well.
