IBM recently announced that they are tripling entry-level hiring. And the reason why? AI. One of the largest tech companies in the world is rewriting all of its job descriptions around AI, including software developers. And their logic is straightforward. Skip entry-level talent, and you end up poaching mid-level talent at a premium from your competitors. There are two sides to the AI coin. How do we replace people with AI? Or how do we reshape roles around AI? And IBM just picked their side.
