No more copyright protection for AI artists in Malaysia! MACP released their new official copyright guidelines for AI music after an AI cover of the 1999 Cinta Tersempul Rapi controversially nabbed the number one spot in Malaysia's iTunes Top Song Chart. In the new guidelines, MACP declared that only works created by human authors will be eligible for copyright protection, meaning full and even partially AI-generated music will not count. And this also extends even to works created with AI tools. With this guideline, members must now declare that their work is 100% human-made. Filing a false declaration would result in the deregistration of the affected work, withholding of royalties and even termination of their membership.
