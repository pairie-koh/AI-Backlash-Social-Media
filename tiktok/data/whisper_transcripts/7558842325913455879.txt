An AI-generated video of Namibia's President Netumbo Nandindaitwa is stirring a storm in the Southern African country. The video, produced using President Netumbo's image and her purported voice promoting an investment scheme, has been widely circulated on social media. However, Namibia's government has described the video as fake and expressed concern over its implications. In a statement on Tuesday, October 7th, the Namibian presidency said President Netumbo has no connection whatsoever to the alleged investment program known as Namibia Money AI. The statement reads, We wish to make it clear that this video is fraudulent and has been artificially generated using AI technology deepfake to imitate the president's image and voice. According to the government, the video was malicious and the aim was to exploit the president's image and mislead the public for financial gain. It is not immediately clear who created the video and the authorities have not said whether they have arrested any individuals or identified a group in connection with the scam video The incident in Namibia raises questions about the regulation of AI content which the Namibian president highlighted in a speech at the United Nations General Assembly in New York last month. Artificial intelligence must not be a substitute for human judgment or responsibility. We therefore call for a global standard that ensures that the usage of artificial intelligence remains transparent, fair for humanity.
