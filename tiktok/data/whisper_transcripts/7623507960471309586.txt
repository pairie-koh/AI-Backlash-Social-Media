AI just broke the power grid growth model. For decades, electricity demand grew slowly and predictably. Utilities could plan for it. But AI changed everything. Data centers are scaling fast, and power demand is spiking. This isn't linear anymore, it's exponential, and the grid wasn't designed for that. It's creating pressure across the entire system, generation, transmission, and interconnection. Infrastructure isn't scaling at the same speed as demand. Some regions are already feeling the strain more than others. The system wasn't built for this kind of growth. The real question isn't how fast the ag grows, it's whether the grid can keep up.
