Scammers are using AI voice clones to trick people into thinking that they're sending money to their loved ones. This is a PSA for everyone, before you see your family for the holidays. These scammers can clone your voice from just three seconds of audio. So if there are any videos of you online, your family can be vulnerable. So out of precaution, set aside some time with your family to come up with a phrase or a question. And memorize it. Don't text it or save it in your phone.
