I hate when movies overuse plot armor so much that it ruins everything. Like, for example, the monster in the movie is terrifyingly violent, merciless, and bloodthirsty, killing hundreds of people in less than an hour. But when it comes to the main character, suddenly that demon has the brain of a toddler just goofing around until the main character eventually kills it. Ahem, stranger things. I mean, I'm not saying that the main character should die all the time. What I'm saying is that the writers are so scared to make the main character have a little boo-boo. The Demogorgon, they kill people instantly without hesitation. They're strong and terrifying. But when they're around the main cast, suddenly they miss every attack, slow down, and make Patrick look like a genius. The movie is still good, though, but the plot armor gotta go. Anyways, I'm out. Bye-bye.
