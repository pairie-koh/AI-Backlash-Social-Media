Same tech, totally different rules. For companies across industries, it's not a one-size-fits-all when it comes to AI. Because in high-risk industries like finance and healthcare, AI interacts with very personal and sensitive data and so can have a major impact on people's lives. So there's more regulation. Companies like banks have to do a risk assessment, ensure fairness, document everything and sometimes even need to be certified before using it. But in retail on the other hand, AI helps with answering basic questions, so companies only need to say I'm a bot, no big hurdles. So depending on the industry your company is working in, you either have to be highly regulated using AI or you just have to comply with some basic rules.
