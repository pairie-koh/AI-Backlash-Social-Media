If you are not using these three AI productivity hacks at work, you might be wasting hours. Anyone can use AI, but this is how you get top quality outputs in less time. One, build quality checks directly into your prompts. Ask a normal question, then follow up with, review your responses as a senior editor, identify weaknesses, and improve the outputs. This makes AI review its own answers and fix its mistakes. That's how to use AI at work effectively. Number two, make AI ask you questions first. Before starting, ask me all necessary questions to understand my goal. This will direct the AI to fill its own knowledge gaps, preventing guesswork. Number three, stop repeating requests one by one. Batch your prompts. Generate 10 variations, write each one in three different tones. This results in more options, saving time and better consistency.
