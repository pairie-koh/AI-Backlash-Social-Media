If you can't understand how AI makes decisions, that's not intelligence. That's a black box. Interpretability addresses this. Interpretability means designing models so humans can see and understand why the AI system reached a certain output. Think of an AI system that approves bank loans. Interpretability lets us trace exactly how factors like income or payment history shaped the approval. Unlike explainability, which adds an explanation after the fact, interpretability builds transparency right into the model's design. AI governance demands interpretability to prevent bias, ensure fairness, and make decisions contestable and accountable.
