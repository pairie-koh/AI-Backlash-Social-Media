Which chatbot should you use for career and job stuff? If you're using one chatbot for everything, you're doing it wrong. ChatGPT for brainstorming and career advice. Maybe you're at a crossroads and you want to change careers. Gemini for anything that needs current data. Connects to Google search natively, which the others don't do. The information is live and it's not based on training data that could be old. Clawed for a repeatable application process. Set up a project and a skill to customize your resume for specific jobs. And maybe something like the Indeed Connector and Cowork to find the right jobs that match your skill set. And then finally, perplexity for when you're interviewing. Maybe you need to do a case study as part of the interview process. Company research. Anything that involves research and a deep dive.
