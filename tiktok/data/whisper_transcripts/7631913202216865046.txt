One of the most interesting findings from this first set of polling results data is that contrary to what people first thought when AI emerged, which was that it would be the great leveller, that it would help people who have lower pay, less experience, fewer skills so far, that it would help to kind of boost them up to the median. What we're actually seeing is kind of the opposite, that it's people in the highest paid jobs with the most experience in the workplace who are already the ones who are using AI the most in their work. That's right and what we're also seeing is that American workers are racing ahead of British workers in adopting the technology in their jobs. Now part of that is because the US has a larger tech sector but we also see that among those who are experimenting with these tools, American workers are more optimistic, more positive about what it can do for their work.
