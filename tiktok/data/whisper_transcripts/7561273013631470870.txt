What if we discover that the universe has always existed? No beginning, no bang, no first spark of creation, just endless existence. For decades, science has told us the universe began 13.8 billion years ago with the Big Bang. But what if that wasn't the start, just a restart? Some theories suggest the universe could be eternal, expanding, collapsing and rebirthing itself in an infinite cycle, a cosmic heartbeat that never began and will never end. It means everything, stars, galaxies, even us, could have existed before in another version of reality. Different atoms, same laws, same story, told again and again across endless time. And if that's true, then the universe isn't a one-time miracle. It's a self-sustaining engine of creation, forever rewriting its own past. so maybe the real question isn't how it all began but whether it ever began at all.
