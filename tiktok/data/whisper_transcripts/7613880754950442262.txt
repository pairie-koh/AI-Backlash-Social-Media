The US government just banned one of the biggest AI companies in the world. It all started when Anthropic, the company that makes the clawed AI, received a massive $200 million contract from the Pentagon. They demanded unrestricted access to their AI for military purposes. But Anthropic's CEO literally said no. He drew two massive red lines. He refused to let the military use his AI for mass surveillance on Americans, and he refused to let them build fully autonomous weapons without humans in control. He basically refused to let the government build AI Terminators. So what happened? President Trump literally banned Anthropic across all federal agencies and labeled them as a risk. And while Anthropic was busy fighting against the final boss, OpenAI and Sam Altman immediately swooped in and signed the contract.
