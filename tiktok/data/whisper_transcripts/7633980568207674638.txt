I found a way to make perfectly consistent illustrations every single time. First, screenshot an illustration style you love. Next, paste it into ChatGPT. Then say, make a JSON prompt for this exact style. ChatGPT breaks it all down for you, colors, shapes, then gives you a reusable template. Next, copy that template, paste it into a new chat, and type anything. Rocket ship, alien, person at a desk. You now have a reusable template for the style you love.
