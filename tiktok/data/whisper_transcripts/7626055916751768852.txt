Microsoft has launched a new level of AI infrastructure, and the bar has shot into space. Microsoft has launched the Fairwater AI Data Center in Wisconsin, designed as a single computing system. Each rack contains 72 NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs, united into a single NVLink domain with up to 8 terabytes per second of inter-GPU bandwidth. a shared memory pool and throughput of up to 865,000 tokens per second per rack. Currently, this is the fastest cloud stack for LLMs. According to the company, the complex delivers 10 times more performance than the current leader among supercomputers. The storage is scaled for exabytes and over 2 million transactions per second, and the cooling system is a closed liquid loop with zero water consumption. Similar facilities are being built in Norway and the UK, where they are already expecting the GB300.
