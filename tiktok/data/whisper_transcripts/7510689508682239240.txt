This is the power grid and I'm going to show you how to get it. First, call the number on the screen right here. Next, wait 2 days for the letter to arrive to the player's mailbox. Next, you need to pay the $28,998 for the electricity to be installed in the player's home. When you're ready to submit the letter, just sign on the bottom by left-clicking on your mouse and dropping the letter at the mailbox outside the post office. The power grid will be installed in 5 days' time and you don't need to constantly refill the generator as a power grid powers the electricity for the home, the garage and the sugar shack. You could find the electric bill every other week in the player's mailbox. and you need to pay it before the next bill comes. If the power got cut, you might need to call the number on the screen right here and press 1 on the phone to pay the bills through the phone and the power will be restored the following day.
