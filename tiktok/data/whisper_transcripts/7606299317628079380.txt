Advanced power quality monitoring using machine learning is a modern approach to improving electrical system reliability and performance. Traditional monitoring systems detect issues like voltage sags, swells, harmonics, and frequency variations after they occur. However, machine learning algorithms analyze large amounts of real-time and historical data to identify patterns, predict disturbances, and detect anomalies before they become serious problems. Smart sensors collect waveform data from power lines, while intelligent software processes this information to provide early warnings and automated insights. This helps industries prevent equipment damage, reduce downtime, improve energy efficiency, and maintain stable operations.
