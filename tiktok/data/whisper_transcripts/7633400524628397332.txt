So AI just restored this damaged 15th century painting 66 times faster than traditional methods, and it's already being used in Italy. MIT researcher Alex Kashkin knew that art restoration is manually laborious, so he developed an AI system that digitally scans for missing areas that need restoration. For this 15th century painting, the AI identified over 57,000 color cues required for the fix. Next, it printed the colors on a thin polymer mask, which is then carefully laminated on top of the artwork, but the mask can still be removed to see the original painting and the entire process only took 3 hours. Alex's technology has amazed conservators and he's now working with Italy to restore Fresco's damage by an earthquake.
