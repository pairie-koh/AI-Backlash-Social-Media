So I'm definitely using Claude Design to design all my email campaigns. Look what I created for Nike. So head to Claude and set up a new design system. You'll have to mention your company name and blurb, some assets like your logos and fonts, and then head to the next step. Once you've completed all the steps, your design system will be generated. Claude takes all of your uploaded assets and also references your website to generate this huge style guide within your design system. Once that's all confirmed, you can start designing from simple prompts and references, like I did with this Nike email.
