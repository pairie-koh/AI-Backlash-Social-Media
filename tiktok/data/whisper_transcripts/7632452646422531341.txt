Most people are using ChatGPT completely wrong. A lot of people treat tools like ChatGPT like a search engine. They ask a question and expect one perfect answer. But large language models don't really work like that. LLMs generate responses based on patterns in language. So the best way to use tools like ChatGPT is more like a thinking partner. So try this. Open ChatGPT or your favorite LLM and paste something real that you're working on. maybe a business idea, a project plan, or a piece of writing. Then prompt it just like this. Act as a critical advisor, identify three weaknesses in this idea, and suggest specific improvements. The real power of large language models is thinking with them, not just asking them questions.
