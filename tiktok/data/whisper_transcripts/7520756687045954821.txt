I don't know about you guys, but therapy alone wasn't cutting it for me. When I get anxious, I need something in the moment. So I decided to build an app. It's kind of like ChatGPT, but for therapy. You can talk to the AI about how you're feeling, and it gives you a breakdown of what's going on in your mind. What I really like the most about this app is you can just speak to it with your voice, and the AI actually listens, understands and responds with thoughtful questions. It's super low effort and I've been using it for the past couple of months and even though I've tried a bunch of different mental health tools over the years, I'm still learning so much about myself. It's called Joyful AI Journal on the App Store and if you want, you can check it out and give me some feedback. Thanks everyone.
