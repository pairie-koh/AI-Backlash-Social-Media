If you checked out every book from every library in the world and scanned them, and then downloaded every single web page on the internet, you could delete anything that appears more than once and take out any errors. And you could feed what's left into a computer program. This program would read all that information, breaking it down into smaller pieces like letters and words. Next, it builds a map of these pieces, making billions of connections between them to see which ones appear together most often. By measuring the strength of these connections, it would learn how human language works. So, when you give it the first word in a sentence, it could guess what should come next. Each time it got a word wrong, it would be corrected millions and millions of times. And when it was good enough at guessing the next word, you could ask it to write its own texts, correcting it every time it made a mistake, until it finally gave you words that sounded just like a human.
