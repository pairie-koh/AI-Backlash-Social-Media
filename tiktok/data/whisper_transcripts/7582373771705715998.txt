Is AI-generated music really just stealing music from other artists and musicians? Opponents of platforms like Suno claim that it is. They believe that using AI to create music amounts to thievery and copyright infringement. Here's the reality, though. Suno was trained on tens of millions of songs. So even though it has the musical brainpower of millions and millions and millions of musicians and songwriters and artists, it's no different than an artist, musician or singer who spent a lifetime studying the music of the greats.
