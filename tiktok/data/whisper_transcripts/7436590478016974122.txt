Why do AI companies want AI regulation? Call me a hater, but AI companies for AI regulation is like teenagers for being grounded. It just doesn't really make sense. Last year, Sam Altman proposed in front of the Senate that AI companies should have licensing and testing requirements. I just could not for the life of me understand why he would want his company to have to jump through more hoops to release their latest GPT models. Until I came across this book, AI Snake Oil, where the authors make the point that if we have this kind of regulation, only these licensed companies would be able to make these advanced AI models, which would conveniently lock in the advantages that these companies currently have. So in other words, if the US government passes a licensing requirement for AI companies, there can't be a next billion dollar startup that comes out of nowhere and revolutionizes the industry the way that OpenAI did. And that decreased competition would be really good for OpenAI. This is not to say that we do not need any AI regulation. It's just that we should be really skeptical when AI companies are proposing their own regulation. If you want more tech and AI content without the hype, follow along.
