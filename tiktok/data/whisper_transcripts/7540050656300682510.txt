The real reason AI layoffs hurt the most isn't just losing a paycheck. It's losing your identity. When a machine takes over your role, it feels like everything that you worked for suddenly has no value. Years of experience, late nights, the pride you built around your career, it's all gone overnight. That's why people spiral after the AI layoffs. It's not just the financial hit, it's the silence, the call stop, the email stop. You wake up with nowhere to be, and it feels like the world moved on without you. But here's what you have to listen to. The value isn't gone. It's just trapped in the way you were told to work. If you adapt, if you learn how to use AI to amplify your skills, if you build new ways to deliver value that machines can't, you can carry everything you've built into the next chapter. AI is going to take jobs, but it cannot take your ability to adapt, to connect, to create, unless you let it.
