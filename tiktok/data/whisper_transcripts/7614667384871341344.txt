You won't believe how this rich man lived his life. Just watch how the story ends. Once upon a time, there was a man who was extremely wealthy, but also extremely lazy. He never did anything by himself. Every morning, a machine woke him up, lifted his mattress, and placed him on his feet. Another program machine brushed his teeth, washed his whole body, and even cleared his throat. When it was time to eat, he didn't lift a spoon. High-tech devices fed him bite by bite while he sat staring at a huge screen. Even talking felt like too much work, so he used a voice assistant to speak for him. His house had turned into a complete robotic fortress, all controlled by artificial intelligence. He no longer walked anywhere. An automatic chair carried him around even when he just wanted to go outside. Even though he could buy anything he wanted, he had completely lost the will to do anything for himself. Technology made his life comfortable, but he forgot how to make an effort, how to move, and how to enjoy doing things on his own. In the end, his wealth became a luxurious cage, one built by his own laziness.
