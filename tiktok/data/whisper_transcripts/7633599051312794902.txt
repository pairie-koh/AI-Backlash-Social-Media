I thought it was just the mirror at first, like my eyes were playing tricks on me. But it wasn't copying me. It was reacting before I moved, like it already knew what I was about to do. And then I saw it again, like the world just paused. Except me. I was the only one still moving. What? until I realized they were all looking at me. That's when I realized it didn't appear. It was already there. And then I saw them. More of me. Standing there. At first, it was just one person staring. Then another. And another. Not in the mirror this time. Down the hallway. Standing there. Completely still. Like it already knew what I was about to do. not randomly, not by accident, like they knew something I didn't. And then everything stopped. No movement, no sound, which means I'm not supposed to be. Time isn't moving the way it should. It doesn't flow anymore. It jumps. Small moments just disappear like they never happened.
