I've been using Vi by Versa lately, and this new multiple agent feature is an absolute game changer. Vi allows you to have multiple agents doing work for you at once. That means you can have one agent organizing data and another scripting out emails while all you're doing is sitting back and relaxing. You don't even have to be at your computer either, so you can be doing hobbies while it's doing your work. It's literally reduced my workday from 8 hours to about 4 hours. Now I have plenty of time to spend with my family and enjoy my hobbies. And the craziest part about all of this is that it doesn't even cost a dime. It's completely free.
