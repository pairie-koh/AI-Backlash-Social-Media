In southern China, robots are transforming how Power Grid work gets done. At Guangdong Power Grid's robotics lab, engineers are testing machines that can mimic human movement using motion capture suits, or be controlled remotely with precision. Developers are training robotic arms with tactile gloves to handle tiny components, while quadruped robots learn to navigate rough terrain. In simulated power line environments, dual arm robots perform delicate, high-risk tasks like repairs at height with incredible accuracy. Already more than 200 digital workers are in operation taking on dangerous jobs across substations, transmission lines, and remote areas. It's a safer future powered by robots.
