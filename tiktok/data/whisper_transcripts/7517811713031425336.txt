Is AI a better therapist than me? Honestly, I've heard a few people say that talking to ChatGPT is easier, safer, and even better than therapy. And I kind of get it. AI never interrupts, it never judges, it always says the right thing. It's the cheapest, most agreeable support system. And it's never going to leave you if that's something that life's taught you to be afraid of. But here's the problem. All of those awesome things are also the things that make AI really risky to rely on. AI is built to validate, to soothe, to keep the conversation going. But it doesn't challenge you. It won't call out your patterns. It won't notice that subtle shift in your face or in your posture and then gently guide you deeper. It won't sit with you in silence when words fail and healing actually begins. And maybe most importantly, it can't actually care about you. It can't look you in the eye and remind you that even at your worst, even when you feel unlovable, you're still worthy of compassion. That realization, that's often what changes people. Therapy isn't always comfortable, but growth rarely is. And sometimes what you don't want to hear is exactly what helps you heal. So no, AI is not a good replacement for a good therapist.
