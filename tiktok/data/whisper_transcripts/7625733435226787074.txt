What I found after. I thought the story ended when they took his body away. But it did not. The next day, I went back to the house. People told me not to go. They said it was not safe. But I had nothing left. No job. No money. No plan. I needed to understand what really happened. The police had locked the front door, but the back was still open. I went inside again. This time, I looked more carefully. The dirty plates were still there. I counted three of them. Three. But my cousin lived alone. I checked the kitchen. There was almost no food left. Just empty containers and a bag of rice that was already open. It looked like someone had been eating fast. Like they did not have time. Or like they were scared. I went to his room His clothes were scattered Drawers were open It did not look like a normal house anymore It looked like someone had been searching for something Or someone had been hiding something. Then I saw a small black bag under the bed. I almost did not touch it. But I did. Inside the bag, I found money. Not much. Just some notes. And then I found papers. Names. Numbers. Amounts. My name was there too. I saw how much I had sent over the months. I had to sit down. It was all written clearly. Every transfer. Every date. But next to some names, there were marks. Red marks. Like someone had crossed them out. I did not understand what it meant Then I heard a sound A car outside I froze Voices Two men They were not speaking loudly but I could hear enough This is the place They said he kept records My heart started beating fast again They were not police I knew that. I quietly closed the bag and pushed it back under the bed. I did not know what to do. If they found me there, I could not explain anything. I moved slowly to the window and looked out. The car was dark. No plate in front. One of the men looked up. For a second, I thought he saw me. I dropped down fast. My body was shaking. I remembered what my cousin said. If anything happens, do not come back. I finally understood what he meant. This was not just about my money. I stayed quiet until I heard the front door break open. They were inside now. Walking. Slow. Check rooms I could hear their steps getting closer I looked around for a place to hide There was nowhere Then I remembered the back door I moved slowly, trying not to make any sound. One step. Two steps. The floor made a small noise. The voices stopped. Silence. Then one of them said, did you hear that? I did not wait. I ran. I did not look back. I ran out through the back and into the bush behind the house. I kept running until I could not breathe. That night, I did not go back. I stayed with a stranger I met on the road. I did not tell him everything. Just that I had trouble. I still have questions. Who were those men? Why were some names marked? And why did my cousin keep everything written down if he knew he was in danger. I lost my money. But now I think I almost lost something else. My life.
