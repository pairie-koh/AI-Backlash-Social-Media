For any of you struggling with the old language that Shakespeare wrote in, a friend of mine asked me to try a modern language version. So here is Sonnet 18, the most famous sonnet, my most favourite sonnet in modern everyday English. See how this feels. Shall I compare you to a summer's day? You are more lovely, you are more beautiful Harsh winds do disturb the delicate flowers in early May And, of course, summer doesn't last long enough Sometimes the sun is too hot dimmed by clouds and beautiful things eventually become less beautiful either by the experiences of life or by the passing of time. But your eternal beauty, it will not fade nor lose any of its quality and you will never die as you will live on in this enduring poetry. As long as there are people still alive to read this poem, this sonnet will live and you will live in it.
