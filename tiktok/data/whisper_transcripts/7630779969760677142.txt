Second, algorithms time your notifications perfectly. They know exactly when to ping you to create maximum dopamine hits, making you addicted to checking your phone. Third, AI generates fake social media posts designed to trigger specific emotions, that viral video that made you angry or happy. It might have been created by AI to influence what you buy. Fourth, recommendation systems slowly shift your political beliefs. They feed you information that gradually changes your opinions without you realizing it's happening. Fifth, and most disturbing, AI creates personality profiles that predict your major life choices. It knows when you'll change jobs, end relationships, or make big purchases before you do. Your free will might be an illusion, and AI is pulling the strings.
