A lot of workers now say that AI at work makes them more confident to leave their job and find a better one. The University of Phoenix just surveyed 5,000 workers and 1,000 employers, and the gap they found between them is extraordinary. 62% of employers say their people are developing AI skills faster than the organisation can adapt. Meanwhile, 47% of those workers say their employer isn't doing enough to bring AI into work properly. So what's happening? Well, you've got your most capable people skilling up, as they should, independently, and then quietly realising they've outgrown the company. And they jump ship. Workers at companies with a clear AI growth strategy have 87% job satisfaction. When you don't have one, 72%. That 15 point gap is causing companies to lose talent.
