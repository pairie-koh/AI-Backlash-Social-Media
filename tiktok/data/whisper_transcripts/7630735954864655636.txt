Using ChatGPT makes you dumb, according to this research. Two groups of students wrote timed essays in the study. One group could only use their brains, but the second group could only use ChatGPT. And the results were quite shocking. When the brains of the brain-only group were scanned, it revealed their cognitive activity were extremely strong. They also had a higher memory recall of what they wrote. But the ChatGPT group had alarming results. They couldn't remember anything they had just written minutes ago. Their brain scan also reviewed weaker activity and shallow processing, which could lead to a decline in daily function. So use your brain whenever you can.
