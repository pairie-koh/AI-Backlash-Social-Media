AI is supposed to make work easier, right? Many companies are pushing AI and some are even requiring staff members to use it. However, employees aren't sharing their leaders' excitement. In fact, most say they feel overwhelmed, anxious and exhausted. Because AI isn't just click a button and done. It's fixing mistakes, rewriting outputs and double checking facts. So yes, it saves time, but then you lose a chunk of that time correcting it. And that's where things get tricky. There's even a name for it now. Brain fry, that mental fatigue from juggling multiple AI tools and watching for errors. And here's the part people don't say out loud. Employees are expected to learn how to use AI tools on their own time. So instead of less work, AI is quietly creating more pressure. The truth? AI isn't a magic fix. It works best when it removes boring, repetitive tasks, not when it replaces thinking entirely. Because at the end of the day, AI is only efficient because there is still a human behind it making sure it actually works.
