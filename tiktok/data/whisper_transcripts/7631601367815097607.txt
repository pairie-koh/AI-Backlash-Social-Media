Before you learn anything else about AI, learn this first. If you don't know this, AI will not be useful to you at all. AI is only as good as the instructions you give it. And these instructions are called prompts. A prompt is basically just what you say or type to the AI. And this is where most people mess up. They are too vague. They'll say something like, Help me plan a trip. To where? When? What's the season? It's the vibe. You have to be more specific and give it direction. And the easiest way to do that is to use TASK. T as in TASK. What do you need? A is AUDIENCE. Who is it for? S is STYLE. How should it feel? Formal, casual. And K is KEEP IT CONSTRAINED. Set rules, set limitations, set specifics. So instead of saying, help me plan a trip, you can say, I'm visiting Tokyo for three days. It's from me. It's my first time. I want to see all the landmarks. I want it to be fun, practical.
