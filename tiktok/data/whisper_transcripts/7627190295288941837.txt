Because in the United States and many other countries, copyright requires human authorship. So, if a song is fully generated by AI, your ability to claim full copyright protection is weak or not guaranteed. If the song is a hybrid, meaning you contributed creatively and significantly, your claim is stronger. But if you're just prompting and generating, you're in a gray area.
