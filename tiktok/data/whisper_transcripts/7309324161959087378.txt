Since using question.ai floating window function, all exams have been up. Question.ai has added a floating ball function. Open the app and click on the third item. Click on floating ball question search. Open the floating ball question search function. Close the question. A floating ball appears on the AI desktop. Open the topic that needs to be resolved. Clicking on the ball will resolve the problem immediately. Double click the floating ball to jump the photo mode. At this point, you can take a picture of the subject that needs to be taken. Press and hold for 3 seconds to close the suspension ball. The floating ball also has a hidden mode, which turns the floating ball into a small white dot when the option is turned on.
