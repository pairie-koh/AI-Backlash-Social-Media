Guardian Protection today unveiled ProActive Video Surveillance, an AI-powered monitoring service that promises to spot trouble and step in before the crime unfolds. The platform combines intelligent video analytics with 24-7 live operators inside of Guardian's UL-certified 5-Diamond-accredited monitoring center. Cameras flag suspicious motion, human specialists immediately issue audio warnings, or summon law enforcement. Company President Brian Cipolletti calls the launch a cultural shift in commercial security, adding that video is now the new standard for intrusion detection and situational awareness. The key takeaways for businesses, crime deterrence. Instant voice down challenges stop intruders in their tracks. Cost savings. Remote monitoring can trim or replace on-site guard budgets. Scalability. One cloud dashboard secures single storefronts or nationwide chains. Guardian will demo the service next week in San Antonio, offering retailers hands-on looks and same-day consultations. The Vice President of Business Sales says leveraging AI plus real people is transformative, giving clients the power to stop crime before it happens. For Guardian customers, proactive protection just became the name of the game.
