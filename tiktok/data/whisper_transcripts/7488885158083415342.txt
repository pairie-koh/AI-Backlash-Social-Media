AI and hiring is powerful, but it's not without flaws. AI systems are trained on historical data, which means they can unintentionally replicate bias. Some tools have shown preference towards certain names, schools, and career paths. That's why transparency, human review, and ethical oversight are critical. As job seekers, the best thing you can do is make your resume clear, focused, and authentic. And as recruiters, we've got to stay aware of how the tools are making decisions and who might be left out.
