He's a Pakistani-British singer-songwriter who first gained fame as a member of the popular boy band One Direction. And over the past decade, he's been vocal about his support for the Palestinian people. He's Zayn Malik. Zayn first expressed solidarity with the Palestinian people during the 2014 Israeli military assault on Gaza. He took to social media to simply post, Free Palestine to his, at the time, more than 13 million followers. His message sparked an immediate angry response from Israeli fans, with some suggesting One Direction would lose its fan base, and many others making threats on Zayn's physical safety. Despite this, Malik continued to use his platform to voice support for Palestinian rights. In 2021, a year marked by Israeli ethnic cleansing of Palestinians in Gaza and Sheikh Shira, Zayn took an even stronger stance, writing, I stand with the Palestinian people and support their resistance to colonization and protection of their human rights. My heart aches for the families who have lost loved ones. He emphasized the need for global action to protect Palestinian rights and end the violence. He posted a similar message elsewhere. And in November of 2023, Zayn Malik joined an open letter to President Joe Biden, urging him to call for an immediate ceasefire in Gaza amid the ongoing assault on Palestinians. The letter, which was written six months ago now, read in part, Children and families in Gaza have practically run out of food, water, electricity, medicine, and safe access to hospitals. The humanitarian situation has reached lethal lows, and yet all reports point to future attacks. We are motivated by an unbending will to stand for our common humanity.
