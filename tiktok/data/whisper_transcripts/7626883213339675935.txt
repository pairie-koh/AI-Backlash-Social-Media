AI lies to you all the time. They call it hallucination. You ask a straightforward question, and sometimes it gives you a confident answer that's completely wrong. The worst part? It sounds so convincing that you don't even realize that you're being misled. Everything you've said before influences what it tells you next. Even when you correct it, it apologizes and it gives you a new answer, sometimes just as wrong as the first. AI is not a source of truth. It's a pattern matching machine. It doesn't know facts. It predicts what words should come next based on its training data and your prompts. That means that it can hallucinate answers, invent sources, and fill in the gaps with plausible sounding nonsense. If you're not careful, you're going to end up making decisions based on fiction, not fact. The solution isn't to avoid AI, it's to use it wisely. Always double check important answers. Ask for sources. Use AI to brainstorm, summarize, and accelerate your work, but never as your only source of truth. The people who win in the age of AI are the ones who know how to question, verify, and think critically about every answer that they get. Don't just outsource your judgment to an algorithm. Build systems that check the work, not just accept it. The future belongs to those who can use AI as a tool, not a crutch.
