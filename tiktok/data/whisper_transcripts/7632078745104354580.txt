Meta is rolling out a new tool to help parents keep tabs on how their kids are interacting with the company's artificial intelligence. The company says parents who are supervising teen accounts in Canada, the U.S., Australia, the U.K., and Brazil can now see the topics their teens have asked Meta AI about in the last week. The tool will be available globally in the coming weeks. They're also providing conversation starters from experts to help parents talk with their kids about their experience with AI.
