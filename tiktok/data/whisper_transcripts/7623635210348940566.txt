Underneath the brown pigment in your eyes, there is blue, which means brown eyes are doing something extraordinary. They are hiding a brighter structure under a blanket of melanin, the same pigment that colours your skin, packed into the iris like smoked glass over a lamp. Strip that pigment away, and the blue foundation can appear. But that is only the beginning. Brown eyes are the original human setting. Go back far enough, and almost every human alive had them. Blue, green and hazel seem ancient, but they are the late arrivals, triggered by mutations only thousands of years ago. Even black eyes do not exist. What looks black is brown, so dark, so saturated, it almost fuses with the pupil. And in sunlight, the trick falls apart. Brown eyes suddenly burn with copper, amber, gold, even red. so brown eyes were never simple. They are ancient camouflage, a biological illusion, the oldest eyes on earth, swallowing light and hiding colour in plain sight all through human history.
