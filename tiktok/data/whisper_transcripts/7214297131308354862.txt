This is great news for artists regarding copyrights of AI-generated work. Today, the U.S. offices gave rules regarding AI-generated work. The gist of it is that you can copyright something that was made straight out of an AI generator. But if you take it through a creative process and it's edited and painted on top, then it can be copyrighted. However, you use AI to create a concept art sheet of a character, and then you 3D model that character, or even, you know, make an illustration using that character. You may be able to copyright the artwork that you made based on that concept, but the concept of the character itself is technically not copyrightable because it was generated by AI and is considered public domain. Concept artists rejoins, not a lawyer, but basically without a human touch, you can't really copyright a concept.
