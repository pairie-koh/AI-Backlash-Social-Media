Simple N8N customer support agents look cool but don't work. Here's an architecture for you that actually does. AI customer support automation is everywhere, but a recent Salesforce study showed devastating results for their effectiveness. And it gets worse. A chatbot providing wrong answers in a clinical or legal setting can basically put you in jail. Japanese scientists have now developed a simple yet powerful architecture called Lenoha. Customer requests are first classified using a lightweight embedding model. If the question is already found in the knowledge space, the answer is simply returned. No LLMs, no hallucinations. If the question is not present, then it's not a high stakes question and can safely be answered by a small LLM. Because the embedding and language model are both super lightweight, it can be ran fully locally. No sharing data with big tech and good energy efficiency. Quality wise, very close to using GPT-4O.
