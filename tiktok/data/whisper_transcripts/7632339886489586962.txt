Meta Platforms is set to lay off 8,000 workers or about 10% of the entire company by May 20 to offset their AI investments. Meta released the internal memo on Thursday, April 23, and a company spokesperson confirmed the layoffs. The company's chief people officer, Janelle Gale, writes the company was making cuts in their effort to run the company more efficiently for other investments. She says, this is not an easy trade-off and it will mean letting go of people who have made meaningful contributions to Meta during their time here. In January, Meta announced boosting capital spending of up to $135 billion to pursue superintelligence. This is the company's effort to offer deeply personalized artificial intelligence to its users. Along with Meta, Amazon, Snap, and other tech companies cut down their workforce to offset AI-related spending.
