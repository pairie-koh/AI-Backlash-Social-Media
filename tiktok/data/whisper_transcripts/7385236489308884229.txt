I don't have time to hate someone while I'm busy building myself up. I either love you or I don't give a damn. I'm not everyone's friend. I talk with everyone. I laugh with everyone. But I'm not everyone's friend. Friendship in this day and age is on the lips of many, but in the hearts of very few. It calls you friend when they need you, and enemy when you're no longer useful to them. That's why I'm not everyone's friend. Only those who know what friendship really means and who know how to respect mine.
