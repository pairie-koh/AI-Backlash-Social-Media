There once was a ship that put to sea, the name of the ship was a bully of sea. The winds blew up, her bowed up, out of below, my bully boys bow. Soon may the warman come, the brimble shooter and tee and rom. One day when the tonning is done, we'll take a leave and bore. She'd not been two weeks from shore, when there none were, a right whale bore. The captain called all hands and swore he'd take the whale in tow. Soon may the woman come to bring the sugar and tea and rum One day when it's with his land we'll take a leave and go Before the boy had hit the water The whale still came up and caught her Hands to the side are pinned and fought her Which she died to know Soon may the woman come to bring the sugar and tea and rum One day when it's with his land we'll take a leave and go
