Are you a college student or a recent grad who's worried about the job market because of AI? Well, you're not alone. Many students are scrambling to AI-proof their majors, but no one knows what majors can actually withstand AI's development. For example, a business analytics major could switch to marketing to be more valuable in the job market. That's because students feel that things like statistical analysis and coding can be automated, but building critical thinking and interpersonal skills is where humans still have an edge. About 70% of college students see AI as a threat to their job prospects, according to a 2025 poll by the Institute of Politics at the Harvard Kennedy School. While half of Gen Z adults use AI at least weekly, those same people worry about AI's impact on their intellectual abilities too. At the same time, many feel that it's important for college students to learn how to use AI because it's being adopted in technology-related fields at higher rates. All of this has led college students to think that picking a major that's AI-proof is confusing and difficult as they could be entering a job market that's very different by the time they graduate.
