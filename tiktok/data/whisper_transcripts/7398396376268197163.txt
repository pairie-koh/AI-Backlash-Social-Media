Not from the stars do I my judgment pluck, and yet me thinks I have astronomy. But not to tell of good or evil luck, of plagues, of thirsts, or seasons' quality. Nor can I fortune to brief minutes tell, pointing to each his thunder, rain, and wind, or say with princes if it shall go well, but I oft predict that I in heaven find. But from thy eyes my knowledge I derive, and constant stars in them I read such art, as truth and beauty shall together thrive, if from thyself to store thou wouldest comfort. O else of thee this I prognosticate, thy end is truth's and beauty's tomb and date.
