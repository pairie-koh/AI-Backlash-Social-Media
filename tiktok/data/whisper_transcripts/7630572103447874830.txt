Harvard researchers identified two distinct approaches when it comes to working with AI, and they called them centaurs and cyborgs. Centaurs divided tasks cleanly between human and AI work. Cyborgs, however, on the other hand, integrated AI throughout the entire workflow, using it as a thinking partner throughout the entire process. And don't get me wrong here, both approaches work, but cyborgs tend to see bigger performance gains because they're leveraging AI strengths more consistently.
