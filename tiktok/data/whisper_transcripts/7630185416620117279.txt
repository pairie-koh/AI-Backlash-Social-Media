Here are five workflows that you can automate to make your own everyday life easier. First, a morning briefing. You can set up a workflow to create a summary of everything you need to know when you wake up. Things like the latest news in your industry, trending topics, the weather, important events on your schedule. All compiled and sent to you automatically before you even get out of bed. Second, a job posting tracker. Instead of manually checking LinkedIn and Indeed and all of these sites, You can set up a workflow that monitors these job boards for jobs that meet your criteria and sends you a daily list of them. This way, you never miss a new opportunity without having to actively look for it. Third, an email assistant. This one automatically checks your emails every morning. It filters out spam and unimportant messages, flags the important ones, and could even draft replies for you. Fourth, a meeting note taker. Tools like Granola and Fathom will automatically transcribe and summarize your meetings for you so you never have to take notes during meetings again. And if you want to build your own, this is a great beginner agentic AI project. And fifth, a weekly meal planner. You can set up a workflow that takes into account your dietary and cuisine preferences, what's in your fridge, what your schedule looks like, and then automatically generates a meal plan and even a grocery list for you every week.
