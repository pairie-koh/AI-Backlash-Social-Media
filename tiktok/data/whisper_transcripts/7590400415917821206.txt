A Google AI mistake destroyed a musician's reputation overnight. Ashley MacIsaac is a Juno award-winning fiddler from Canada. He was set to perform at a First Nation concert in Nova Scotia on December 19th. The venue cancelled his show after searching his name online. Google's AI overview falsely labeled him a sex offender. The AI confused him with another Canadian man who shares his name. MacIsaac learned about this while visiting his grandmother for Christmas. He now fears what would happen if he tried to cross a border. He says he could have been detained or jailed based on false information. Google Canada responded by saying their AI overviews change frequently to show helpful information. The First Nation issued an apology and praised his artistry and commitment to reconciliation. Law firms have already reached out to take his case. MacIsaac wants to stand up because he knows he won't be the last victim. What happens when AI gets your identity wrong?
