Getty Images has largely lost its UK lawsuit against Stability AI, the maker of Stable Diffusion. The court found Stability AI infringed Getty's trademark by generating images with Getty watermarks, but dismissed broader copyright claims, ruling Stable Diffusion does not store or reproduce copyright works. Getty's secondary copyright claim was also dismissed. The decision is limited in scope and historic, leaving unresolved whether training AI on copyrighted works is infringement under UK law. Getty called the trademark ruling a win for intellectual property, while Stability AI welcomed the dismissal of copyright concerns. The case highlights gaps in UK copyright law and calls for clearer policy guidance.
