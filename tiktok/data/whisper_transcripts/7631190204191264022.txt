I got my app rejected by Apple. Here's every reason why so you don't make the same mistakes. First, they tap every single button. If anything says coming soon or does not work, rejected. Every button needs to do something. If your app has a login, you need to put demo credentials in your app review notes. They won't create an account. They just won't figure it out. They'll reject you. If you're selling anything online, subscriptions, one-time payments, anything, It has to go through Apple's in-app purchase. You cannot use Stripe. You can't link it to your website. They will catch you out. Every permission you ask for, camera location, microphone, photos, you need to explain why. If your app doesn't actually need it, remove it or get rejected. Two things that catch everyone. If you sign in with Google, you must also have sign in with Apple. and if users can create an account they need to be able to delete it inside. You also definitely need a privacy policy section. Users need to be able to see this so they can understand or you will get rejected. I learned all of this the hard way building my own apps. Go through the list before you submit. Follow me for more.
