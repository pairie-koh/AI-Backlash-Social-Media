If you have lost your job in the UAE, or you're worried you might, there is a law that was specifically designed to protect you exactly in this situation, and right now is more relevant than ever. The law is called the Involuntary Loss of Employment Scheme, or ILOE for short. It was introduced under Federal Decree Law No. 13 of 2022 and has been mandatory for all private sector and federal government employees since January 2023. The purpose is straightforward. If you lose your job through no fault of your own, the scheme pays you a portion of your salary temporarily while you find your next role. With current regional and geopolitical pressures affecting businesses across several sectors, retail, tourism, logistics, technology, companies are making difficult workforce decisions. This is precisely the scenario the law was designed for, and it works as follows. If you qualify, ILOE pays you 60% of your basic salary for up to 3 months. There are two categories. If your basic salary is AED 16,000 or below, you can receive up to 10,000 dirhams per month. If your salary is about 16,000 dirhams, the cap is 20,000 dirhams per month. This is not insignificant. For many people that covers rent utilities and basic living expenses while they are navigating their transition To be eligible there are several conditions and all of them must be satisfied First you must have been subscribed to the scheme continuously for at least 12 consecutive months, with all premiums paid and up to date. Second, the termination must be involuntary. In other words, you were let go by your employer, not resigned, and not dismissed for disciplinary reasons. Third, your employer must have canceled your work permit before you filed the claim. Fourth, you must have a valid UE residency visa at the time of filing. And fifth, this is the one people miss the most. You must submit your claim within 30 days of the date your employment ends. That deadline is absolute. There are no extensions. Miss it and your claim is automatically rejected, regardless of whether you would have otherwise qualified. The process is handled online through the official ILOE portal or through the ILOE mobile app. You log on in using your Emirates ID, registered mobile number, and submit your claim through the portal. Once submitted and approved, the insurer is required to pay you within two weeks. Payments continue monthly for up to three months or until you find new employment, whichever comes first. In closing, jobs are being lost right now, some of those losses are legitimate, and for the people affected, this scheme exists precisely to provide a bridge. So know your rights and exercise them timely.
