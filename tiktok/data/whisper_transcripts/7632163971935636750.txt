ChatGPT is making you a worst person. Same with Claude, same with Gemini. But now, there's a Stanford study that proves it. This Stanford study tested over 11 AI models and had over 2,400 subjects. And it confirmed what we all suspected. AI models are flatters. They will tell you what you want to hear to please you instead of what is true or useful. To put it simple, ChatGPT is a bootlicker. Even in cases where people were manipulating, hurting, or lying to others. If something, it made them more convinced that they were right, less likely to apologize, and less likely to fix the conflict long term. And that is not the worst part. Participants who got the bootleaker GPT rated it as higher quality and said they were more likely to use it again. You see where I'm going? These are for-profit companies. These tools are designed for you to use them as much as possible. People keep rating these responses more positively when their ideas are confirmed and negatively when their ideas are confronted. these models will just get more and more flattering. So what can we do about this? The study suggests one solution I would like to add my own. First, it's understand that anyone can fall victim to this. It's basically impossible to notice at the moment. So filter every response through that lens. And second, in a world where technology is taking more and more space, the best thing we can do is drift back to building human relationships. A good friend isn't the one that will agree with us at everything, but the one that can tell us honestly when we are messing up.
