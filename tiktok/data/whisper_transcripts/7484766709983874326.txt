The amount of water that AI uses is astonishing. According to a report by the Washington Post, if you ask ChatGPT to write you a 100-word email, it uses 519 milliliters. But wait, what does AI actually do with water? There's been tons of viral videos about AI's water consumption, but none of them explain what AI actually does with water. So I prepared some water and asked ChatGPT for a step-by-step breakdown. Step one is heat generation. Computers and data centers generate heat whenever you prompt an AI, stream Netflix, or play a game. So step two is to cool those computers down. The most common way is by spraying water onto cooling towers where it evaporates. But some data centers will use a closed-loop system like cooling tubes. Next is step three, waste. After reusing the water a few times, it's treated and returned to rivers, lakes, or the local water supply. Apparently, Microsoft, OpenAI's main investor, even has underwater data centers.
