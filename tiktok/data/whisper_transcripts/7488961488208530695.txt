A Victorian couple has been fleeced out of half a million dollars after falling for an AI investment scam. When it came to hunting down those responsible, they vanished without a trace. Jack Perrie reports. This was meant to be the land where John and Gail Young's future dream home was meant to be built before they fell for an elaborate AI scam. After seeing a pop-up ad on Google with TV personality Eddie McGuire encouraging people to invest, John fell for the trap. In March last year, he initially invested smaller sums of $10,000 to $20,000 in the promise of massive returns. Three or four months later, when I was expecting our first dividend to come through, nothing came through, and when we looked into it, all our money was gone and irretrievable. By July, he had invested half a million dollars of the pair's money that included their savings and superannuation. I know a lot more now about scamming than I did I suppose I could say I was naive I feel embarrassed about what happened because I trusted people all my life and I never gone down this road When the first return of the investment didn't arrive, all communication with the supposed broker went dark. And any paper trail or evidence of the investment was deleted. Don't get involved in any way, shape or form. I believe there's networks. I believe there are people working under the guise of a name or a person. and they're not. The couple have been married for more than 45 years. They've been through highs and lows but they say this is one of the biggest hurdles yet. The impact on your family and your entire life is just far-reaching. Sleepless nights, the unknown going forward, seeing your wife and your kids crying and upset. For now the couple hope authorities will be able to track down those responsible and by sharing their stories deter others for falling for similar scams.
