It is so easy to build with AI right now. I mean, it's kind of addicting. It only takes a few minutes to use AI to build something that looks great, but it also runs really well. Maybe you get super excited about what you build and you even share it with your team. Maybe you even share it with your customers, but then no one uses it. You probably didn't mean to, and I hate to say this, but you made some slop. And no one needs more slopware that technically works, but solves nothing. But that's usually what happens when you vibe your way through building something. And that has never been what product management is about. So why let it slide now? AI gives you the capabilities of a whole engineering team. It gives you superpowers. But as we all know, power comes with responsibility. And that responsibility piece still seriously matters. Especially if you want to build anything of value and not just like virtual trash. Product managers, you need to be strategic like you always have been. Act on vision and evidence, not impulse and assumptions. Find a problem to solve and make a plan. Then bring in AI to start building. So what do you think? Is vibe coding a bad habit or a harmless experiment?
