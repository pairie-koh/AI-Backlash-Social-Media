Have you ever wondered how the smartest AIs in the world think? They're actually using a method called chain of thought reasoning. This means when you ask it a question, the AI isn't just jumping to an answer. It's breaking down the problem into simpler steps. What's funny is this is very similar to how when we were in school, our teachers used to tell us to show our work when we were solving problems. So why does this matter? When an AI is essentially forced to show its work, its answers get way more accurate. In fact, a study by Google research found that AI's accuracy on complex reasoning tasks skyrocketed when it used this step-by-step approach. By copying how humans actually go about thinking through problems, AI isn't just coming up with answers, it's actually understanding them. And the better it gets at thinking like us, the closer it gets to being smarter than us.
