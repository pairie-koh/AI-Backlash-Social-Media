I found pumpkin gnocchi at Trader Joe's, so I asked ChatGBT to get me a recipe, and this is what it gave me. First, I cooked the Italian sausage on medium-high heat. Trader Joe's had a spicy sausage or a sweet sausage, and I opted for the sweet. Next, I buttered some sourdough bread to put in the oven on 400. To be honest, I forgot to set a timer, so I just watched the bread while everything else cooked. Next, I added the pumpkin gnocchi to the boiling water. The recipe said to cook the gnocchi until it floated to the top of the water. While that cooked, I moved on to the sauce. I mixed two tablespoons of butter with two teaspoons of minced garlic, six to eight sage leaves, and I added one third cup of half and half with one fourth cup of Parmesan cheese and brought that to us. Finally, I added some salt and pepper and red pepper flake. Once the sauce was finished and the gnocchi was drained, I mixed everything together. The sausage, the gnocchi, and the sauce blended so well together. Once plated, it was time for a taste test. it was really really good the flavors complementing each other well with the savory and the sweet and now it's time for the real critic and he loved it
