Did you know state school students in Queensland aren't allowed to use ChatGPT, but teachers are? Public and private school teachers have been given the green light to use AI to draft emails and homework, but there are some areas of teaching where AI is not supported. A new framework has been created by the unions to help guide teachers on where they should and shouldn't use AI. So for example, it would be okay for teachers to use it to draft lesson plans, draft homework questions, send emails to other teachers, or even write articles for newsletters. But it wouldn't be okay if they're using AI to cope with the teacher shortage. And there's a little murky area in between where some tasks should be done under consultation, like rolling out automated essay scoring or using plagiarism detection software. Now, industry experts are stressing that AI does not replace the role of a teacher. It simply enhances teaching and learning. And the Queensland Education Department says that chat GPT will still be blocked for state school students while they're on the network until guidelines are made.
