This is a scene from the ancient Roman site of Pompeii when Mount Vesuvius erupted in AD 79. But it's created by AI. Archaeologists and researchers digitally reconstructed the face of a man who died during the eruption. According to experts, this is a big deal because if used well, AI can translate skeletal and archaeological data into realistic human likeness. The portrait shows a man whose remains were found in a condition that suggests he was attempting to flee the city toward the coast of what is now Italy during the volcanic eruption. The AI-generated image shows what the man could have looked like. You can see him in this rendering running along a debris-covered road, holding a large bowl over his head and using it as a shield while Mount Vesuvius is erupting in the background. Archaeologists found the man holding a terracotta mortar, which they interpreted as his attempt to shield his head from falling volcanic stones. The man was also carrying an oil lamp, a small iron ring and 10 bronze coins.
