A deep dive into the Virginia Chamber Foundation's new statewide AI assessment. Well, that report finds Northern Virginia has the highest concentration of employers hiring for AI-related talent, but it also predicts NOVA could be the most impacted region. The report estimates more than 540,000 jobs in the region. About 39% are exposed to AI-driven change. And the roles most affected are not just in tech. We're talking project managers, management analysts, accountants, customer service, HR, even cybersecurity. And then there's another pressure point, of course, power. Virginia's data centers, mainly clustered here in Northern Virginia, already use a large share of the state's electricity and demand is going to keep rising. So how do we address all of these issues? Well, the report points to three big moves, AI literacy and reskilling, stronger worker transition support as they move into more AI dominant roles. and building out the energy and infrastructure needed to keep growth sustainable here in northern Virginia.
