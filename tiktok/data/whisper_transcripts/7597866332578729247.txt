Did you know that AI engineers are the fastest growing jobs right now, but 75% of professionals don't feel ready to compete? That's not a talent problem. That's a preparation problem. Companies keep saying that AI is the future, but they're not doing anything to help their people adapt. You can't chase AI titles, skip workforce planning, and then get shocked when hiring gets harder. AI is shaping work. The companies that win are not the ones that are going to just hire faster.
