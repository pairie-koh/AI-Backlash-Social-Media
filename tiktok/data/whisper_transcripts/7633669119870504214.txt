AI just rewrote the laws of physics. What if an AI looked at the universe and found rules that physicists didn't even know existed? Plasma is the fourth state of matter, not solid, liquid, or gas. It makes up 99.9% of everything visible in the universe, from solar winds to lightning bolts. Physicists at Emory University built a neural network to track tiny dust particles moving inside plasma, not to predict data, not to sort data, to discover brand new physics. The AI mapped mysterious non-reciprocal forces where one particle pulls another toward it, but that second particle pushes back, and it did it with over 99% accuracy. It also proved that decades-old assumptions in plasma theory were just wrong. This isn't a supercomputer. It runs on a regular desktop. And researchers say the same method could uncover hidden laws in living cells, bird flocks, even human crowds. AI didn't just analyze the universe. It read it differently than we ever have.
