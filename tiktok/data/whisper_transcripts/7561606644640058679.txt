The era of AI regulation begins in the U.S. California has become the first state to pass a law regulating artificial intelligence chatbots, setting a national precedent and challenging the stance of the White House. Governor Gavin Newsom signed the legislation requiring AI companies to include safeguards to protect users, especially minors. The law, spurheaded by state senator Steve Padilla, comes after several incidents linked to chatbot interactions, including suicides. With this measure, the state aims to hold companies accountable for the effects of their systems and allow citizens to take legal action if they suffer harm. At the federal level, the government still avoids strict regulation, arguing that innovation drops economic growth. However, California's move could mark the beginning of a new era in the United States, one which artificial intelligence must balance technological progress with the protection of people.
