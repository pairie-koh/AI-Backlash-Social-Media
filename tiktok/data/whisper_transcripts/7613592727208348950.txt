For eight hours tonight, you will lose control of your own mind. You won't choose your thoughts. You won't control your body. And yet your brain will begin doing some of the most extraordinary work it ever does. First, the shutdown. Roughly 90 minutes after you fall asleep, your brain flips into REM sleep, the stage where dreams explode into life. Your brain activity surges. In some regions, it becomes almost as active as when you're awake. But at the exact same moment, your brain disables most of your muscles. You are paralysed. A safety system designed to stop you from physically acting out your dreams. Then the real maintenance begins. While you sleep, a network called the glymphatic system opens up inside the brain. Spaces between brain cells expand and cerebrospinal fluid begins flowing through the tissue. think of it as a nightly pressure wash for your brain Waste products that built up during the day are flushed away including proteins linked to Alzheimer disease And while this cleaning is happening, your memories are being rewritten. Neurons replay patterns from the day, strengthening useful connections and trimming away the noise. It's as if your brain runs a night shift archive, reorganising the story of your life. But sleep has one more strange twist. Over the course of a lifetime, you'll experience around 100,000 dreams. Entire worlds created by three pounds of tissue inside your skull. Adventures, conversations, moments that feel completely real until morning deletes most of them forever. so when you fall asleep tonight nothing about it is passive your brain will be repairing itself cleaning itself reorganising itself and inventing entire universes all while you lie perfectly still in the dark
