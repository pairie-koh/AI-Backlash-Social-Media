2026 changed everything. AI is no longer just a tool, it is the attacker now. The fakes are the first problem. AI can generate realistic videos and audio to impersonate anyone. Attackers can convince people to authorize fraudulent payments and it is becoming hard to tell if the person on screen is real anymore. But it gets worse. Egentic AI systems can plan and execute entire cyber attacks on their own. The scan networks send thousands of personalized phishing emails per second, deploy malware and launch ransomware without any human involved. Organizations are now giving AI agents access to internal systems. If one agent is misconfigured or compromised, it can delete data, leak secrets, or shutdown operations in seconds. Cybersecurity is shifting towards zero trust, AI identity control, and passwordless of the indication. Because when AI becomes the attacker, traditional security is no longer enough.
