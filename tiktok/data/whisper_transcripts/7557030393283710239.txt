What if I told you ICE may be watching your TikToks right now? Immigration and Customer Enforcement is quietly preparing to build a 24-7 social media surveillance team with one goal, to deport even more people. According to documents uncovered by Wire, ICE plans to hire 30 private contractors to comb through every major app you use. X, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, even Reddit. And don't think niche platforms are safe either. They're on the list too. This round-the-clock monitoring will operate out of Vermont and California with potential AI assistants to track and flag posts. Remember, ICE already runs its main surveillance base with Palantir, even signed a contract with the Israeli spyware firm Paragon last year. Here's the kicker. While ICE claims this is not about immigration enforcement, privacy experts warn the system could easily be used to spy on citizens and non-citizens alike.
