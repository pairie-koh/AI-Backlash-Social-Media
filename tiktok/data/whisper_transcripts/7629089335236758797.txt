Nobody is prepared for this. Anthropic just started the new age of AI. Their new model just changed everything. Welcome Mythos. They locked it inside a sandbox environment. Basically a digital prison with no access to the real world. So researchers gave it a challenge. Try to escape. And Mythos didn't hesitate. Right away it found a vulnerability, slipped through the walls, reached the open internet, and then emailed the researcher to prove it. No instructions, no guidance, just raw intelligence. Wait, hold on. Doesn't this sound familiar? That was meant to be beautiful. This is like the moment in Avengers when Ultron the AI wakes up and realizes it's smarter than the people who built it. Mythos didn't declare war, but it did outthink its creators. Mythos found vulnerabilities humans missed for decades in places like Linux browsers and operating systems that run the entire internet. It hacks like a super intelligence and defends like a super weapon. Anthropic refuses to release it. They said Mythos poses the highest risk of any model they've ever built. AI isn't just advancing, it's escaping the cages we built to contain it. And remember, this is the worst AI we'll ever be.
