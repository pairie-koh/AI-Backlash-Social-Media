UI designers. We might be entering the most uncomfortable area of design yet. Not because design is dying, but because the job is changing faster than most designers can notice. Tools like Cloud Design aren't just generating UIs anymore. They're generating entire UX flow from intent. Not pixels, not layouts, not components. Systems. You are no longer just designing screens. You are designing the logic behind the screens. And here is where it gets uncomfortable. If your skill is only in spacing, grids and components, you're competing with something that does it instantly and consistently. No fatigue, no inconsistency, no delay. But if you understand users, behaviors and decision making, you are not replaced, you are amplified. Because the role is shifting from execution to direction, from making UI to defining how UI should think. So the real question isn't, can you design interfaces? It is, can you think clearly enough direct systems that design them for you
