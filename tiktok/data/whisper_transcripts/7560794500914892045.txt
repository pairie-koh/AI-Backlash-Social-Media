Those breaking news videos you're seeing online might not be news at all. They could be AI ads. Reporter Nilesh Christopher says companies are now using hyper-realistic AI to mimic TV newscasts, complete with fake headlines, fake anchors, and even fake logos, all to sell products or legal services. OpenAI's Sora app has a new feature that allows users to insert their own image into AI videos in just seconds, making it harder to tell what's real and what's not. Companies like Case Connect AI say they're just using technology to reach customers, but they're doing so without disclosing which part of their ads are AI. Online safety experts say the flood of AI stories and ads is making the digital landscape even messier.
