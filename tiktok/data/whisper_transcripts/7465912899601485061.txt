Artificial intelligence is poised to dramatically reshape our world in the next 5 to 10 years. By 2030, AI is projected to contribute $15.7 trillion to the global economy, boosting business productivity by up to 40%. While AI will displace some jobs, particularly in low-skilled sectors, it will also create new opportunities in fields like data science, AI training, and robotics engineering. However, this transition may exacerbate income inequality and shrink middle-class opportunities. Ethical concerns include algorithmic bias, privacy breaches, and the need for transparency in AI decision-making. To ensure responsible AI development, many organizations are adopting ethics-by-design approaches, embedding ethical principles throughout the AI lifecycle. governments and international bodies are also working on regulatory frameworks to govern AI use and protect human rights
