A U.S. resident made over $8 million creating AI-generated music and was later convicted of fraud. 54-year-old Michael Smith produced thousands of AI tracks, uploaded them to streaming platforms, and used automated bots to generate billions of streams. He was charged with wire fraud and now faces up to five years in prison, along with the confiscation of all profits made through the scheme. The case also sets a new legal precedent, one that will likely influence future laws aimed at regulating AI. Streaming platforms are already taking active steps to prevent similar schemes. ensuring that revenue from plays goes to real artists.
