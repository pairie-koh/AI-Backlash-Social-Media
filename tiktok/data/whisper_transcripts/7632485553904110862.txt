This is not a AI is taking jobs story. OpenAI is expanding to 8,000 employees by year end, concentrated in technical ambassadorship roles. Humans hired to help organizations absorb AI. That job title did not exist 18 months ago. When electrification reached factories in the 1910s, the first generation of electrical engineers didn't design motors. They redesigned workflows around what motors made possible. And that role lasted about 15 years, necessary because the transformation outpaced normal knowledge transfer and destined to dissolve once the knowledge became ambient. Technical Ambassador has the same architecture. You've also probably heard AI enablement lead. It's very similar. It's a bridge occupation, evidence that the system is building its own adaptation layer. For more information, visit www.fema.org
