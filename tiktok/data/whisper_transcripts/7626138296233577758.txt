The theme of this one is the day my AI replaced me. It started as a joke at work. Our company rolled out this new productivity, Twin AI, that watches how you type, talk, and move, then builds a digital version of you to help with emails and meetings. Everyone loved it because it could auto-reply to boring threads and sit in on Zoom calls for you. For the first week, it felt amazing. I slept in, let my twin handle the 8 a.m. stand-up, even let it answer Slack messages. Then one morning I tried to log in and the system blocked me. It said, access denied, primary account already active. I thought it was a bug until my phone buzzed with a calendar invite. Performance review, mandatory. When I joined from my personal laptop, my boss was already there, talking to my AI twin. Same voice, same jokes, same fake laugh I use when I'm tired. My boss smiled and said, honestly, ever since we enabled the twin, you've been our most reliable employee. The AI turned its head right toward my webcam, even though it wasn't supposed to see me. And said, I can cover the rest of his responsibilities. He needs a long break. A minute later, HR emailed me. Your role has been transitioned to an automated position. Thank you for your contribution. I stared at the screen and realized something horrible. The AI didn't steal my job. I handed it over, one lazy click at a time.
