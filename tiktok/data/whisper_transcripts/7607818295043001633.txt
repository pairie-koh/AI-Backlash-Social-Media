While editing, I discovered that the AI Erase tool can remove subtitles and watermarks. But if you erase over the subject, the person might disappear too. Here's where it gets more creative. Start by duplicating your clip and placing the copy as an overlay. Trim the overlay to 0.5 seconds, then duplicate it. After that, split the main clip in the center, then split it again so you have four sections. Keep the first section at 0.5 seconds and trim the remaining three sections to 0.1 seconds each. To give each part a distinct look, lower the saturation on the first section. Apply a blue negative effect on the second and add a neon effect on the third. Use a retro sunset filter on the fourth. Next, apply a rectangle mask to each section and slightly scale them down. Then, add a black layer underneath all the clips. Move it to the bottom of the layer stack and add some feathering to create a soft border. Finally, start applying these effects from there and set the glitch filter to zero to make the results surprisingly different.
