Taylor Swift is fighting back against AI. The US pop star has applied to trademark an image of herself as well as her voice amid growing concerns of AI-generated content. The pop superstar has lodged three trademark applications in the US, one using a photo of herself on stage during the Ares tour and another two trademarking her classic phrase, which she recorded for Spotify and Amazon Music to promote her album The Life of the Show Girl last autumn. It follows a growing trend of celebrities trying to tackle AI through such measures, including Matthew McConaughey, who became the first celebrity to use trademark rules to attempt to protect his voice and image from AI misuse. It is reported that by trademarking her voice, Swift could potentially challenge not only identical reproductions, but also imitations that are confusingly similar, a key standard in trademark law.
