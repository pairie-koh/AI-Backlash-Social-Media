How to use AI and not rot your brain. The truth is AI isn't going anywhere and AI is also not the problem. It's how we use it. Most people use it as a replacement for thinking, but you and me, we are going to use it as a thinking partner because we want to be hot and educated instead of having our brains fried, right? I partnered with Claude AI to show you three anti-brainrot ways to use AI. First, use AI as your sparing partner. Instead of write me a marketing strategy, try, I'm trying to solve a problem. What angles haven't I considered? Ask for perspectives, push back, have AI ask you questions. That's what keeps your brain engaged. Second, use AI as your early stage team. When I started my clothing brand and when I started my social media, I had zero resources. No manager, no lawyer, no budget. So when brands started reaching out to me, I used AI. I got this brand to offer. Help me understand what's fair and how to counter offer. It gave me a chance to get to where I am now. AI doesn't replace the team that you will build eventually, but it gives you a fighting chance to even get there. Three, use AI to learn deeper. Don't just ask AI to summarize something and then call it done. Help me understand the intersection of x and y. What patterns am I missing? What questions should I be asking? Yes, AI can rot your brain if you let it think for you, but there's never been a better time to solve a problem if you use the right tools.
