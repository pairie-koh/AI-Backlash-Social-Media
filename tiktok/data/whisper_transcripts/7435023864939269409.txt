Forget everything you know about a calendar app. This one is magic. Okay, so tomorrow, for example, I was planning on going to the studio for four hours, doing a Pilate session, and then working on social media content in the evening. But then plot twist, I get a gig last minute. So that means that not only do I have to do the gig, but I also have to plan music for the gig. And guess what? I have no time in my calendar. But Reclaim is gonna sort everything out for me. You input the calendar imposter, as I like to say. So in my case, it would be the gig and prepare music for the gig. Reclaim then reshuffles everything onto different parts of your diary where you have free time. This means that you can get everything done and not be stressed out. Amazing. And as a bonus, you can mark certain things as priority and you can also mark things with deadlines so the wrong things don't get reshuffled to the wrong places. Love it.
