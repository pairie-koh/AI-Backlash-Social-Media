Your next job interview might be decided by an AI robot. No human interviewer, just a machine. More than 90% of employers are now using AI in the hiring process. And it's not just screening your CV. AI can now score your answers, sift applications, and even carry out interviews. No feedback, no eye contact, no idea how you're coming across. And it's already putting people off. Around 40% of candidates say they've quit or considered quitting an application because of AI. So why is this happening? Job applications have exploded up 286% in one year. But jobs? They've nearly halved. And in some cases, the role you're applying for isn't even real. Recruiters say they simply don't have enough time to read every CV anymore. But here's the twist. AI might actually be fueling the problem. Almost all employers say they've seen a rise in AI-generated scripts. We know AI is taking jobs away, but now it might be deciding if you're hired or fired.
