They told us that our son cheated on a paper, which is not what happened. Jennifer and Dale Harris say Hingham High School teachers, administrators, and the school district have unfairly punished their son after he used AI on a history paper. They say the high school handbook never mentioned AI until this incident with their son, only adding this language this year. They basically punished him for a rule that doesn't exist. In their lawsuit, they say he only used it as a tool to research, not to write the paper. But the school saw it differently and punished him with detention. His grades suffered. He was not allowed into the National Honor Society. And now he's at risk of getting into his top choice schools. In my lay opinion, they violated the civil rights. They treated him and punished him more severely than other students. I'd also like them to put in place an AI policy that makes sense, that the teachers understand, that they can then articulate to the students
