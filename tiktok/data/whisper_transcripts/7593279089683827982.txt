You won't believe this. Researchers used AI to simulate the evolution of predators and prey. What happened next looked disturbingly, like real life. Red AI survived by hunting blue AI. If it failed to hunt before its energy ran out, it died. Blue AI relied on plants to survive and reproduce. Both sides had lifespans, and their bodies stayed on the map after death. At first, plants were scarce. Blue AI had to spread out to find food and was hunted relentlessly. But as blue AI collapsed, red AI ran out of prey and began starving in large numbers. The remaining blue AI recovered as plants grew back. They multiplied, and every red AI attack was met with group resistance. Only seven Red AI survived. They stopped hunting and switched to low-energy wandering, feeding on bodies instead. When plants could no longer support Blue AI, they migrated. Red AI followed, feeding on those that fell behind. Migration failed. Blue AI split into smaller groups. Red AI returned to hunting, this time together. Too efficient. Blue AI crashed again. Red AI overhunted and starved once more. In the end, Blue AI learned to hide behind plants. Red AI learned to ambush, and even fed on their own dead just to survive.
