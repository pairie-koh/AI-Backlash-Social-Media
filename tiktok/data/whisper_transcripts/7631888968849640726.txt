What if the most dangerous AI ever built wasn't made by a rogue hacker, but quietly developed inside one of the most trusted AI companies in the world? That's exactly what just happened. Anthropic, the company famous for building safe AI, just revealed a model called Mythos, and it can automate cyber attacks, not defend against them, actually execute them. The news was so serious that Anthropics CEO Dario Amode walked straight into the White House to brief officials in person Think about that for a second The same company that built its entire reputation on AI safety just handed the government a demonstration of an AI that could theoretically be weaponized But here's where it gets interesting Amode didn't hide it He went public precisely because transparency might be the only real defense we have. The surprising part is this might actually be the most responsible thing any AI company has ever done. Sometimes the safest move is showing the world exactly what you built before someone else builds it worse.
