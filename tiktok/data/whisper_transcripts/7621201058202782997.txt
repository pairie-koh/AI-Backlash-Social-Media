The power grid wasn't built for AI. Our demand is exploding. But power grids are old. They were built decades ago for predictable use. Now massive data centers are plugging in at scale. And electricity demand is rising fast. Some regions are already struggling to keep up. The real issue isn't AI. It's infrastructure. If grids can't expand fast enough, AI growth could slow down.
