Our story begins on a peaceful earth, with vast oceans, bright polar ice, and early civilizations living simply, using very little energy. By the 18th and 19th centuries, industrialization starts spreading, with smokestacks rising near cities and the first machines changing how people live and work. In the early 20th century, cars, factories, and airplanes multiply, pollution grows, and polar ice begins to noticeably shrink as cities expand rapidly. By the late 20th century, global transport networks and industry create thick haze and heat-trapping gases, while ice caps retreat and coastal cities face growing flood risks. Now in the 21st century, massive energy use and pollution intensify climate change impacts, with shrinking ice, extreme weather, and a choice ahead. What future will we create?
