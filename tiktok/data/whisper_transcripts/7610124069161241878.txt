In space, Earth's gravity is the same. Not gone. Not switched off. Almost exactly what you feel right now, standing there. Up where astronauts orbit, gravity is still pulling at about 90% strength. If you could magically stop all sideways motion for even a second, you wouldn't float. You'd fall. Straight back to Earth. So why does everything look weightless? Because astronauts aren't escaping gravity. They're trapped inside it. The space station is constantly falling toward Earth, right now, every single moment. But it's also moving sideways insanely fast, about 8 kilometers every second. As it falls, the planet curves away beneath it. Fall forward, miss the ground, fall again. That loop is orbit. It's the same feeling as an elevator with the cables cut. Gravity is still pulling you down. You just never hit the floor. So space isn't zero gravity. It's endless freefall. Earth never lets go.
