AI is actually making our jobs way harder. A survey of 2,000 workers found that almost one in four say tools like ChatGPT have actually increased their workload. Why? Because when tasks get done, bosses just expect more. In fact, 23% say AI has added to their workload, and 26% say it's increasing pressure at work. Experts call it the AI productivity paradox. The faster you work, the more work appears. Researchers say instead of saving time, people are filling those spare hours with extra projects and new responsibilities. And there's another worry. One in three people fear that AI could replace their job within five years. So while AI is making companies more productive, many employees say the reality is working faster and never really switching off.
