Here's something that changed how I see AI in the writing space. I am kind of embarrassed to admit that I used to use ChatGPT as a sounding board. At the time, I didn't have any friends who read fantasy or wrote fantasy, and I needed someone to validate that my ideas were good. I'd like to make it clear that I never took ideas from AI. I just asked if my ideas were good. I was seeking approval, not advice. But regardless, the more I learned about AI, the less I used it. Here are the three biggest reasons I stopped using AI as a sounding board or at all in the writing space. First, AI is trained by inputting information. Several authors have come out saying that AI has stolen their work and proposed it as like a novel idea. No pun intended. It isn't a person who can come up with ideas on its own. It takes from others. And this brings me to my second point. Nothing can replace a real person. Human emotion and voice are what makes books and art in general so special. For my current work in progress, I hired a developmental editor and she has been amazing. Way better than any AI could have been. And last but not least, AI is bad for the environment. I'm not going to pretend that I know how it works, but I do know that there is a lot of water involved in cooling the servers. I now know that AI has no place in the writing space and I'm going to continue to advocate for keeping it out of the arts in general.
