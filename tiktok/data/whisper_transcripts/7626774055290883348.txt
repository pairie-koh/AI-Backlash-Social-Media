AI facts. Meta has been caught for spying on all their customers' private content recorded from their AI glasses. Despite Meta's AI Ray-Ban glasses being advertised as designed for privacy controlled by you, a Swedish newspaper discovered that there were Meta workers in Kenya manually reviewing personal photos and videos captured from the glasses. Some of these sensitive footage include nudity and using the toilet. But Meta argues that deep within their terms of service, it does state that customers' content may be reviewed by a human to categorize them to train the company's AI. Although Mena claims all faces are blurred as part of the process, Kenyan workers have said that the blurring does sometimes fail.
