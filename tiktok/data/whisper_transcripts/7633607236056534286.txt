It's a system with a wild name, Huncho Dialectic User Modeling. And this is where things get really futuristic. This is not about remembering your favorite color. This is a deep reasoning engine that is actively trying to build a mental model of how you think by studying your patterns, your goals, and even the way you talk to it over time. And this is all powered by a team of three little agents working together inside the system. You have the driver, which is like a note taker, just pulling out the explicit facts. Then you have the dialectic, which is like a detective looking for the hidden patterns and connections. And finally, you have the dreamer, which works in the background, kind of like a subconscious, piecing it all together into a unified model of you.
