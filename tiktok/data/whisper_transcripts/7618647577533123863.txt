Artists are having their songs stolen through AI right now. I have shared my music online for years, both released and unreleased songs. But now, people are stealing songs from real artists, stealing their vocals, their lyrics and uploading them to AI tools and then releasing them through a cheap distributor to collect royalties on something they did not create. this just happened to someone close to me. To avoid this happening to me I have decided to remove all my unreleased songs and all my music from any free pages I've put them on. If you want to hear my work some of my songs will still be available but only the official releases and you will be able to find it on Spotify, YouTube, SoundCloud, Deezer, Apple Music, iTunes. I'd rather protect my art than feed thieves.
