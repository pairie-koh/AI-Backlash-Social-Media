There's a lot of AI doomerism nowadays, and I just would like to give people a little bit of comfort in this very uncertain time. It's easy to get caught up with new models coming out every few months that are exponentially better than the ones prior. and it's easy to see that and feel like there's no hope for either software engineers or any other really technical field. And talking to a lot of people my age and a little bit older than me, this is a very real concern that a lot of us have. But at times like this, I like to just kind of take a step back and really analyze where I'm at and where I could be going. Because AI is going to take its course. I have no control over that. But what I can control will determine where I fall in, you know, the inevitable reality that AI is going to be integrated into almost every product. So I guess what this rant really was about is just focus on what you can control. I have no control over how companies lay off people or how they decide to spend billions of dollars on data centers and potential impacts of that. I have no control over that. What I do have control over is my attitude and what I'm doing day to day. So I think that's what a lot of people need to just start doing. And eventually things will straighten themselves out.
