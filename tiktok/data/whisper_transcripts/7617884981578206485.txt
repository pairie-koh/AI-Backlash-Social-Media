But as CTV's BC Bureau Chief Andrew Johnson reports, the number of job hunters that are cutting corners with AI is creating new headaches for hiring managers trying to figure out who they really are dealing with. You can't trust the documentation you're receiving from candidates the same way anymore. Recruitment agencies say resumes are all starting to look the same. And your perfectly crafted cover letter?
