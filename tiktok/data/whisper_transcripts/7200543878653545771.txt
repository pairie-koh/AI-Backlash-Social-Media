Is the chief AI officer the next role that every company is going to be hiring for? This article in Fast Company by Assembly AI says that companies not in tech need to hire for this new role as soon as possible. And this isn't just for tech startups. Big incumbents actually have access to way more data than new startups, so they're in an even better position to build intelligent AI models. The issue is they'll need really strong technical talent and a strategy that executives are bought into if they want to succeed. And this is where the chief AI officer comes in. Here are two examples from companies you know. For example, at Bank of America, AI could be used to detect fraud in new ways. Just think about how much data your bank has on you. They can intimately learn your daily habits like where you get coffee each morning and catch any outliers before you even do. Another example is Walmart. Your shopping experience could become insanely personal. Walmart can use AI to surface online recommendations from in-store purchases or predict what you want next. The use cases are endless, so we'll be following along closely.
