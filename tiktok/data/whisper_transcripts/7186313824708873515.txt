Should AI-generated content be considered copyright infringement? Here are five reasons why it shouldn't. First is that copyright law doesn't protect IFMAS, ideas, facts, functions, methods, and styles. And the purpose of using AI is not to access the protected parts of work, but instead leverage and generate from the unprotected parts. Second is the use of public data sets which have been scraped from the web does encourage public scrutiny on what type of data is going into and fed into these models. Third is a question of practicality. Is it even possible for all of the different data points that go into these models to be licensed, especially when we move into more real-time learning where you have systems that are learning from user input? Fourth is that the output of these systems do require human input and authorship in the process. And five is that AI is completely capable of making unique and original pieces of work that haven't existed before.
