Now for some important news, an event that could change how we think about earning money. Today in Ottawa, Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney unveiled a new government investment platform created to improve the financial well-being of citizens. Key points of this program were announced during a press conference. Mr Carney, what exactly is this new government platform for citizens? It's a special investment system backed by the government and designed for the average citizen to earn money. The platform operates using AI and is capable of independent trading on global financial markets. The platform minimizes risks and ensures a stable income. Is this another national wealth fund? Not at all. This isn't a fund where citizens receive minimal payouts. The program is designed so that citizens can safely grow their savings, and the government provides guarantees and conditions for passive income. What is the minimum investment amount and what income can citizens expect? After consulting with the Bank of Canada, we've set the minimum amount at $355. This is the optimal amount for the system to begin trading and immediately generate income for the user.
