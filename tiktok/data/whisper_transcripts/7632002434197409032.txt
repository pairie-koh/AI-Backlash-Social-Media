Every image or video reference has four layers you must break down before you prompt anything. Subject describes the person or object, their age, skin tone, hair, pose and expression. Outfit go piece by piece, clothing type, color, fabric, fit and every accessory. environment, where is the set, indoor or outdoor, time of day, background details, the overall mood, camera, how is the shot framed, close up or wide, the angle, and whether the lighting is soft, harsh or natural.
