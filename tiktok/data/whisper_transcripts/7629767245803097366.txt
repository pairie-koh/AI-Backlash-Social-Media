Claude Mythos, the latest AI model from Anthropic, is causing a big stir in the world of cybersecurity. According to the company, it's the equivalent of one of the best hackers in the world, able to find critical flaws, known as bugs, in all sorts of software and systems that are vital for modern life. The fear is, if this model gets into the wrong hands, or misbehaves, or goes rogue, it might lead to major data breaches or cyber attacks across many different industries. And it's the financial sector that's particularly concerned. However, a lot of this hype and fear has come from Anthropic itself. Critics argue this is part of a broader marketing strategy used by big tech to build excitement about the next AI models. Cyber security world has not been able to assess just how good Mythos is yet. But the UK's AI Security Institute has been given access to run some initial tests. It concluded that, yes, this is a powerful model. But it also said that it only really works in undefended environments. Where you've got good cybersecurity, Mythos should be caught and stopped. Those researchers are advising people and companies not to panic. They say, make sure you're getting the basics of cybersecurity right, because most successful hacks don't need super AI. They can use simple things like phishing emails.
