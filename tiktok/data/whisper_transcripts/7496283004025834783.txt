Until you are a mother, no one understands what it takes. The late nights, early mornings, sleepless days, not eating, because you're so busy trying to tend to your kid. Whether you have help or not, a mother's job is never done. No one understands the mental strength you have to have when it comes to being a mom. Not being able to rest your mind because it's constantly going all day and night. No one understands that postpartum is real and it never ends. It creates insecurities, anxiety, depression. No one understands that even with tears in your eyes, you never have time to stop and really take care of yourself. Because being a mom is a hundred jobs in one. To all the moms that are struggling, trying to find peace, and going through the motions, Just remember, your babies need you. And no matter what, you are Superwoman.
