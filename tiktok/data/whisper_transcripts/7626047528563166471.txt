There is a pill that makes you immortal, you will live forever. But everyone you love will age and die while you remain. Do you take it? Yes, unlimited time to experience every- Yes, the chance to witness all of human history- Yes, you can always find new people to love. Yes, living forever beats dying every time. No. What's the point of living forever? If the people who make life worth living are gone, you wouldn't be living forever. you'd be watching forever. Every person you love becomes a countdown. Every goodbye becomes the last one. That's not immortality. That's the longest loneliness ever invented.
