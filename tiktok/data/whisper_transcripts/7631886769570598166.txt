What is AI sabotage? And are employees turning on bots to help save their jobs? A study found that one in three people were deliberately sabotaging artificial intelligence in the workplace because they're scared it's taking their jobs. So how are people sabotaging AI? Well, it's not a case of taking a hammer to your desktop or asking ChatGPT to delete itself. Instead, AI sabotage can range from tampering with AI-produced content to make it seem worse at its job to feeding AI bots poisoned or corrupted files to cause something called total model collapse. And it seems people are right to worry. Some company bosses admitted they were considering laying off staff that can't or won't use AI, while others reckoned that artificial intelligence bots could take hundreds of millions of jobs in the next five years worldwide.
