The entrepreneur who turned AI into a billion-dollar workforce. In 2024, Matthew Gallagher started Medvi from his house. No office, no team, just $20,000 and AI running everything from code, marketing, customer service, and even decision-making. It looked like a simple telehealth company, selling weight-loss drugs in a crowded market. But it wasn't about healthcare, it was about leverage. He didn't build infrastructure, he layered on top of it, outsourcing prescriptions, logistics, and compliance while owning the ads. Hundreds of customers became thousands, and now Medvi is tracking towards a valuation of $1.8 billion with just two employees. But innovation brings tension, regular scrutiny, and questions around AI oversight. Still though, the shift is clear. This isn't a story about AI. It's a new startup model, because now one person with software can do what used to take a company.
