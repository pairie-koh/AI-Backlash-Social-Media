If you've been staring at a loading screen or your Zoom calls have been lagging lately, it's not just your router acting up. Two major subsea cables in the Red Sea near Jeddah have been damaged. These cables, specifically the SMW4 and IMEWE systems, are the literal backbone of the internet connecting the Middle East to Europe and Asia. The impact is being felt across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and as far as Pakistan and India. The International Cable Protection Committee says the most likely culprit is commercial shipping activity. It sounds minor, but a single ship dragging its anchor in these shallow choke points can snap these fiber optic lines like a piece of string. Fixing this isn't as simple as sending a technician out like you usually would. Specialized repair ships have to be dispatched between the technical complexity and the regional tensions. it can take weeks or even months to get back to full speed.
