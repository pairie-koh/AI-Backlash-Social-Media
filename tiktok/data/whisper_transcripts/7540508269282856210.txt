Did you know your everyday devices could be secretly sending data overseas? Since 2023, Lithuania's National Cyber Security Center and EU agencies have warned that some Chinese-made smartphones and AI-powered cameras, like Xiaomi, Huawei, Hikvision, or Dahua, may send sensitive data to servers in China. These AI-equipped devices can analyze your behavior, track device IDs, and even detect specific search words. Purpose? National security, political monitoring, and AI-driven analysis. This doesn't mean overseas users are directly punished, but it highlights the privacy risks of AI devices. Stay aware, because the AI in your devices can do more than you think.
