We uncovered a frightening number of sexualized AI-generated videos that use likenesses of underage girls hosted on Instagram, tallying over 130 million views across just 144 videos. Over two months, we discovered an algorithmic track that starts with sexualized AI-generated adult characters, transitions to AI-generated videos that show underage girls' likenesses in sexual contexts, then devolves entirely into showing real underage girls, often posted by their parents, in a feed full of AI-generated and real sexual content. But what was most startling was how easy it was to surface that content and how reliably it led to real videos. The full investigation is on riddance, which we partially launched for this. The research for this was done in January, but I couldn't talk about it on video because I'm not going to show it, and I struggle getting my words out about it. But there you can read about the AI video format that we tracked, how we conducted this investigation, and why it'll probably get worse without system-wide interventions outside of just Instagram. The videos and accounts we found have been reported to Instagram, and they remain up almost entirely. We're waiting for further comments from Meta on the piece.
