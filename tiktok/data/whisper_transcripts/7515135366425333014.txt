Disney and Universal just sued Midjourney. It's the biggest AI copyright lawsuit ever. Here's what happened. These studios claim Midjourney trained its AI model on thousands of their copyrighted images without permission. We're talking about Mickey Mouse, Star Wars, Marvel characters, Shrek. Basically, everything you love. Now users can type, draw me Spider-Man and get images that look almost identical to Disney's original artwork. The studios are calling Midjourney a bottomless pit of plagiarism. But here's why this matters. This isn't just about one company. Every AI tool that generates images, videos or text could be next. We're talking about a potential complete reshaping of how AI companies can train their models. The question is, will this kill AI creativity or finally force fair compensation for artists?
