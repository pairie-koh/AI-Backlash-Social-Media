Everyone's talking about Vibe Coding like it's a shortcut. It's not. But it is genuinely changing how we build. Vibe Coding means using AI to generate code from normal language. You describe what you want to build, and AI does the build for you. It definitely lowers the barrier to building, but it doesn't remove the need to think carefully about what you're building. The hype says you can ship anything overnight. What it doesn't mention is authentication, access control, data privacy, edge cases. I mean, AI can generate a form in seconds, but who can see the data? Who can edit it? What happens when something breaks? And that's honestly where most Vibe-coded projects fell apart. Not in the building, but in the thinking before it. When I build with AI, whether it's using tools like software for the front end or automations in the back, security and structure come first. Vibe-coding done right means AI handles the speed and you handle the judgment. That's a partnership, not a replacement. If you're building with AI right now, or thinking about it, just one reminder to everyone, build responsibly. Move fast, but know what you're moving forward. Follow along. I share what actually works and what to watch out for.
