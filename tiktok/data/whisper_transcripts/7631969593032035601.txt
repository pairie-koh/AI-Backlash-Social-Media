AI video still can't do hands. Here's why. When AI generates video, it's not thinking, it's predicting what should come next based on patterns it learned from millions of videos. Most of the time, this works great. But sometimes, hands are chaos. They move fast, they overlap, fingers bend in weird directions. They're constantly occluded by objects, faces, other hands. The AI learned patterns, but hands have too many exceptions, too many variations frame to frame. So when the model tries to keep your hands consistent across 30 frames, it panics. Adds a finger, removes a finger, turns your thumb into, well, something else. Physics takes a vacation. Same with text, reflections, and anything that needs to stay perfectly consistent while moving. It's getting better, but for now, try to keep hands out of the frame when you can. A link to the full video is in the description.
