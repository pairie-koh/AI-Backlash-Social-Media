The AI bubble is about to burst because the hyperscalers, as they're called, have used up all their own cash and are now borrowing money. They are effectively net debtors at a time when they're spending huge volumes of money on data centres without having any guarantee that they're going to earn profits from that. and it's that worry that actually there isn't money to be made in that business which is gradually dawning on investors and that's going to be why they are going to pull away. In fact, there's already signs of people withdrawing from the AI sector because they fear that this is a bubble. It seems to be utterly inevitable. The question is only when.
