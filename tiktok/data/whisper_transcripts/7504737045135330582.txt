The UK have just done the biggest U-turn on their AI regulations. Originally, they allowed AI firms to use copyrighted materials without the artist's consent. There was like a little tiny checkbox that they had to tick if they wanted to opt out. And there was massive backlash to this. Loads of famous artists like Elton John and Ed Sheeran or something created this silent track of protest, essentially. So in response to this, the House of Lords have now passed an amendment that says AI developers have to disclose whether they've used copyrighted materials in the training of their models. This is huge. It might seem small, but it's huge. This is such a big step towards transparency and tighter regulations for AI companies. It's not just a free-for-all anymore. They can't just take whatever they want.
