People that work at companies that are reducing headcount with AI, what are you guys doing about this? Last place I worked at was doing it and I felt like my slack was going to get turned off at any second. There is a solution coming down the road, but it's tough to be patient. If I were you, I'd actively be looking for a new job and I'd be trying to figure out ways to make money on my own. I think this initial craze was centered around deep loading really large organizations that truly did need to reduce headcount. It somehow turned into every midsize company thinking they should do the same thing and not caring what heads will roll. I think this is a race to the bottom and these companies will not exist long term. The companies that win will be investing in humans and upskilling their current job descriptions through AI. You see Notion and Ramp already doing this. And I can pretty much guarantee anyone watching this video is not working at a company that's as large or successful as those two. So, you know, just be patient. Find companies that are leading with that counterpoint of humans first through AI instead of just AI or no AI at all. I think that's the way of the future. And in the meantime, I would start getting super familiar with how you can use AI in your free time. That way you'll be more of a ideal candidate when the time comes and the rest of the organization start taking the same approach.
