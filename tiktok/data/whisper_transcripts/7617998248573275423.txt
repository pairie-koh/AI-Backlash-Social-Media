They're not building more data centers because too many people are using AI and they just can't keep up. That's not why. Talking to chat or inferencing does take some power, but not $500 billion worth. Those massive data centers are overkill for the inferencing that we do on apps on our phone. So they're not building this just for inferencing. They're building it for training. Because these AI companies have discovered when they're training a new AI, If they just use more computing power to train it, the AI starts learning new things. Kind of unpredictably. It's like, okay, when we train it with 10 GPUs, it learns how to talk like a human. When we use 100, it learns how to code. When we use 1000, it learns how to speak different languages. So what happens when we use a million or a billion or a trillion? To oversimplify it, they're building up computing power so they can keep spinning the what will the AI learn wheel. and hopefully one day Jarvis will just pop out. Kind of like that.
