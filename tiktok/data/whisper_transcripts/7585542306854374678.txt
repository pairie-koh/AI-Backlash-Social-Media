It is entirely within their power to come back with an offer which meets our reasonable expectations on artificial intelligence protection and on royalties by the 19th of January next year. Now we have said consistently for over 18 months of negotiation that offering us protections won by our sister union SAG-AFTRA in the United States following their industrial action is a flaw and not a ceiling. Those terms are not appropriate for jurisdiction in this country and nor are they appropriate for the exponential pace of artificial intelligence development over the last two years. If producers do not offer suitable AI protections and a paradigm shift in the quality of royalties paid out to artists, the result of this ballot means the union will begin the statutory processes required to take industrial action on film, TV and on streaming productions which use our agreements.
