AI can cheaply copy styles in seconds. I joined the DTYS to show what real inspiration in art looks like instead. Hi, I'm Nat, a human artist building my own path while the industry is shifting more and more towards AI. So I'm sharing my process to show how much story and intention goes into real art. In this video I share my entry to a Draw This In Your Style challenge by one of my favorite artists, Anikeri. I've been following her for years and even own several of her prints and stickers. Her style is honestly so unique and inspiring. So when I saw her cozy cat reading among sunflowers, I immediately wanted to create my own version. I started sketching and I focused on that warm, cozy feeling, imagining the cat getting so immersed in her book that the sunflowers start growing all around her. I used soft, warm colors to really push that comforting, peaceful vibe, like a quiet moment while the kittens cause a little chaos around her. Isn't that process much more interesting than watching AI literally steal artworks of other artists? In the end I even printed it out to see how it works as a physical piece. And I'm really happy with how it turned out. I can't wait to show you what else I've been working on these days. So thank you all for checking in and for your continuous support.
