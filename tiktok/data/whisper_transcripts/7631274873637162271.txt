Missouri lawmakers are juggling discussions over a dozen different proposals to regulate AI, but some say a national order is complicating the state process. Anna Falcon has more from the Capitol. Across party lines, Missouri lawmakers can agree. AI is not a person. AI shouldn't be a mental health therapist. AI is not a person. It's a tool to be used by humans and not something to replace humans. Regulating artificial intelligence is popular with both parties, and only 16% of Missouri voters polled by YouGov and SLU believe recent AI innovations had a positive effect on society. Even so, bills proposing regulations are not moving quickly through the General Assembly. We've been working directly with the White House to ensure the bill fits within their framework while still accomplishing our goals here in Missouri. Senator Joe Nicholas' bill would say AI cannot have human rights. But when brought to the floor, it was filibustered by two fellow Republican senators who were concerned about losing broadband funding. I'm concerned.
