AI promised to solve the climate change problem. Instead, it caused a global power crisis. Every chat GPT query uses 10 times more electricity than googling something. Multiply that across 100 million weekly users and billions of daily prompts and AI data centers now eat up 2% of the world's power supply and it's about to double. The US needs dozens of new nuclear plants to power AI but data centers take 3 to 5 years to build, power plants take 5 to 10 and nuclear power plants over 10 years, but simply the demand is growing faster than the supply can be built. Microsoft reopened Three Mile Island, the first US reactor restart in history, with other big tech giants like Amazon and Google investing hundreds of millions into nuclear power rates. The flow of money is shifting. The next big players aren't going to be the ones developing power-hungry AI, but the ones who can feed the fast-growing energy appetite.
