This happened in September, but if you're curious about AI copyright, this is a story that you should know. The U.S. Copyright Office approved copyright registration for a comic book that was using text-to-image AI software to develop all of the comics. The creator was required to demonstrate substantial human involvement in the creation process in order to get approved. Also interestingly, the U.S. Copyright Office has declared that AI itself cannot be the owner of a copyright.
