We should all pursue the dreams that keep us up at night and the dreams that wake us up in the morning. On the journey to success, every struggle will be worth it. Every denial will be worth it. Every failure will be worth it. But to get there, you must never give up. Courage doesn't always roar. Sometimes courage is the quiet voice at the end of the day saying, I will try again tomorrow. A failure is not always a mistake. It may simply be the best one can do under the circumstances. The real mistake is to stop trying. Never give up. What if I told you that 10 years from now, your life would be exactly the same? I doubt you'd be happy. So why are you so afraid of change?
