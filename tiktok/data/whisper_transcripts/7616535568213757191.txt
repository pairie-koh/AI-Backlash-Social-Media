Rentahuman.ai is a marketplace where we're told AI needs your body. And violinist Nigel McLean is one of the first Australians to offer his at an hourly rate. The idea is simple. You can get paid when artificial intelligence needs a job done in the real world. An AI agent is an online tool that essentially will operate on your behalf. It might go and try and find the best airline deals for you and then actually take that next step of booking the tickets for you.
