WorkSphere has rebranded JobKorea as it pivots from job boards to AI-driven hiring tools, integrating services including JobKorea and Albamon into a single platform covering permanent, gig, and foreign worker hiring. The company said it uses user data to build worker profiles and match them with employers through AI Tools Career Agent and Talent Agent, set for initial release this year. Following January AI updates to JobKorea and Albamon, click-through rates on recommended posts rose 298% and 158%. News knows no borders.
