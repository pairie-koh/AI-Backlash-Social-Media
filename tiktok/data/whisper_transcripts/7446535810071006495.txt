Do you think AI is overhyped? If you wanted to actually make money today and you wanted to do it with a high degree of likelihood of winning, you'd be better off running a boring business, running a landscaping company, running a roofing company than going and trying to jump into AI. I think that most people would utilize AI in some form or fashion, but it will be 1% of companies that make a ton of money. How is AI really that different in its infancy from what happened with internet companies back in the day? In tech, you typically do not have a big distribution of wealth. You have few who win really big and everybody else basically loses. It's venture capital, 10 companies, one wins. I don't think it's overhyped in that it'll change the way we work forever. But if you're thinking about me personally, should I jump into the AI train because first I was crypto and then I was Web3 and now I'm AI, I think your likelihood of not making a lot of money is way higher than you think.
