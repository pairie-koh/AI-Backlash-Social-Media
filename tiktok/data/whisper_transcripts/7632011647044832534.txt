Here's an economics concept every AI user needs to know. When a technological process increases the efficiency of a resource, in this case intelligence, the falling cost actually increases demand so much so that the total consumption will increase rather than decrease. So the per unit cost of delivering electricity actually goes down, but then we start to use it more. For example, using agents. This increased demand means that often the efficiencies are offset and price will actually go up. This means that even though AI per token is getting cheaper and cheaper, we're using more and more on a daily basis and causing the price to be driven up because there is a limited supply of GPUs, RAM and electricity. This is why the Anthropic servers keep collapsing because they cannot deal with the increased demand. Their servers are basically overloaded, unable to give the compute required to deal with the massive demand they're getting.
