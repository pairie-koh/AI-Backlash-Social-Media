The AI boom isn't just using more electricity, it's quietly draining our water supplies too. Data centers powering artificial intelligence need to stay cool. To do that, many use millions of gallons of fresh water every day, sometimes as much as an entire town. That water is often drawn from local systems, the same ones residents rely on for drinking and sanitation. As AI demand grows, some communities have already reported rising water bills, tighter restrictions, or shortages during droughts. And because many facilities run 24-7, that strain never really stops. AI is reshaping the future, but it's also reshaping our resources.
