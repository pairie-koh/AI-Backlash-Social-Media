If you're spending hours answering the same questions by your customers over and over again, you need this app. It turns your website and your FAQs into an AI chatbot. The chatbot can then answer for you to all common questions your customers have. You can also design the chatbot to match your brand and even see the conversations your customers are having. The best part, though, if the AI can't answer a specific question, you can just answer yourself on your phone.
