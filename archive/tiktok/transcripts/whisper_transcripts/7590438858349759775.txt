If you're applying to jobs and not getting interviews, you're not using AI the right way. This is exactly how I'd use AI as a recruiter to land interviews much faster. This is day six of getting hired. AI won't get you hired on its own, but it will help you get in the door. Here's how to use it strategically. First, I'd use AI before I ever hit apply. I'd paste a job description in and ask it, what are they actually looking for here? Then I'd make sure my resume is clearly matching those skills. Not copying, not lying, just making my experience easier to understand for the recruiter and the hiring team. Next, I'd use AI to clean up my resume bullets. Recruiter skim fast. If your impact isn't obvious in one line, it gets missed. AI helps you say the same thing, just clearer. Then I'd use it to help with outreach. Not the awkward, just following up messages. I'd ask AI to help me a short, specific note to the recruiter or hiring manager that actually explains why I'm interested. I'd also use AI to practice interview questions out loud. Even just running through answers once makes a huge difference in how confident you sound. And finally, I'd use AI to research the company fast. I'd look up what they do, what they care about, and what success in the role looks like. That alone helps me stand out in interviews. Using AI like this didn't just help me get interviews, it helped me feel calmer going into them. When your brain knows what to expect, your nervous system feels safe.
