How to hire with Robot in 2023 Step 1. Introduction to Robot After exchanging pleasantries, AI Robot will explain that she is here to interview you for a specific role. Step 2. Job-related questions Robot will ask you questions related to the job you are applying for. For example, if you are applying for a sales role, she might ask you to name the top three skills a salesperson should possess. Step 3. The future of hiring Robot will explain how the future of hiring is already here, with many companies using automation and artificial intelligence to find the best talent. She might also ask how you feel about being analyzed by an algorithm. Step 4. Concerns and criticisms Robot will acknowledge concerns about the potential for bias in AI and machine learning tools. She might ask for your thoughts on issues such as fairness, transparency, and accuracy. Step 5 – AI Assessment Robot may use an AI assessment tool like HireVue to analyze your responses.
