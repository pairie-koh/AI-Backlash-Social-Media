Here are three things I've learned about building apps with AI after building so many. One, AI is fast and that's the problem. You build things in a weekend that should have taken you a week or months of thinking and planning. Number two, nobody cares about your stack. They care about the pain or the problem you're solving for users. Number three, the app was never the business, the distribution, the marketing. That's the business. But remember, start with the who, not the what.
