EU's new AI regulations are a nightmare for startup founders. On August 2nd, phase two of the AI Act went live. From now on, general-purpose AI providers face fines of up to 35 million euros or 7% of global turnover if they don't comply. For big tech, that's just another cost of doing business. For startups, it's brutal. Meeting these rules means pulling engineers off product to write documentation, spending scarce runway on lawyers, and delaying launches just to keep regulators satisfied. All the cash that should go into building now gets burned on compliance. The result is a tilted playing field. Giants can absorb the overhead and even use it as a moat, while smaller players risk being slowed down or pushed out entirely. Innovation in Europe isn't being killed by a lack of ideas. It's being buried under regulation. And it's only a matter of time before this becomes a global crisis.
