In part one, my sleeping fiancé called me at 2 a.m. Then I opened the recordings and heard the truth. The voice wasn't random. He had cloned his own voice to send me a warning. But why? Then I played the next file and heard him rehearsing something. He walked in and caught me listening. Frozen. Then finally admitted it. He'd been using AI voice tools to investigate someone impersonating him online. Someone scamming people using his face and voice. And the 2 a.m. call? It was meant as a test to prove how real the clone sounded. But he panicked and never told me. Then he showed me something worse. Another voice clone existed. Not made by him. Made by someone else. Using my voice. Suddenly, this wasn't about trust. It was about someone building fake versions of us. We canceled the wedding planning for a week and went to the police cyber unit. Sometimes the scariest lies. don't come from people anymore. They come from copies. What would you have done?
