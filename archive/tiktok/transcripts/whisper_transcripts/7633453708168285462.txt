There is one chatbot that effectively doesn't let you have any data privacy. Do you want to guess which one it is? Like Clawda and ChatGPT, Gemini lets you opt out of using your data to train its models. But if you do, your conversations disappear in 72 hours. You effectively lose the memory feature. You basically have to choose between sharing your data to train its models or keeping your chat history. Which to me is like giving up all of the functionality. If you use Gemini, just keep that in mind. If you need to share more private info, then use Gemma locally on your phone.
