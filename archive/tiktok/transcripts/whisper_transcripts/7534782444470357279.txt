AI, artificial intelligence, is an amazing tool, but it should never replace your own inner clarity. Think of it as your research assistant, right? You're helping you brainstorm and explore options, but the decision-making process, that's all you. A 2019 study in psychological science found that self-reflection strengthens our decision-making abilities. It helps us find clarity, not just from external information, but from understanding how we feel and what we value. So AI can offer data and insights, but you have to trust your gut when it comes to important decisions. So basically you use AI for the what, but you let your emotions and instincts guide the why.
