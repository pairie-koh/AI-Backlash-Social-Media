Did you know that one MIT professor spent 40 years warning us that technology would make us lonely and then completely changed her mind? Sherry Turkle started studying artificial intelligence in the 1980s. Back then, she was excited about computers helping people connect. Her early research showed that talking to simple AI programs actually helped some people open up emotionally. But by the 2010s, she became technology's biggest critic. Her studies revealed something alarming. She followed the same people for decades, from email to smartphones to social media. She watched them become more isolated, less able to have real conversations, more anxious around other humans. For years, she warned everyone that digital relationships were destroying our ability to connect. Then something unexpected happened. After documenting the loneliness epidemic she predicted, Turkle realized AI companions might actually be the cure. She She started experimenting with AI chatbots herself.
