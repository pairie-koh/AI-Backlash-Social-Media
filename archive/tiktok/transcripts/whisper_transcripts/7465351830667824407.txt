The amount of water that AI uses to cool itself is astounding. According to a Washington Post report, if you ask ChagPT to write you a 100 word text, it uses 590 milliliters of water. And think about it, ChagPT alone has 200 million users. If each of them asks ChagPT to write a 200 word text, that's 207 million liters of water a day. And we're not even taking into account all the other companies that are using AI now, like Meta, Google, Microsoft, Apple, and the use of AI is growing every day.
