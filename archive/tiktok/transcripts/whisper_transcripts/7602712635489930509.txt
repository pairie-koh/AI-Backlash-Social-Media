Why you should stop using generative AI. Not all AI is bad. Many different forms of AI exist to help people without stealing or infringement, examples being patterned recognitions for cancer, gaming behavioral AI, MPCs, spam filters, and environmental monitoring. The official Oxford definition for generative AI is artificial intelligence designed to produce output, especially text or images, normally requiring human intelligence typically by applying machine learning techniques to large collections of data. What does it mean? Gen.ai takes data to produce content. Where does this data come from? More often than not, it's stolen from the creators that it's scraped from the internet. Essentially, their content is plugged into programs that generate images that copy the likeness of the creator's style and work without any proper compensation. What does this mean for creators? Many artists, writers, and even just the average person has to worry about their work and likeness being stolen for generation. With Gen.ai becoming more and more mainstream, many companies have have taken the initiative to be AI first, eliminating countless jobs filled by creatives. The already unstable job market for creatives is worsened by Gen AI. Infamously, companies like Duolingo have had massive layoffs in favor of AI There have been also instances of employees being replaced by the AI they trained Industry jobs are quickly disappearing As of right now it is near impossible to have an ethical Gen AI. Even when ignoring the theft from creators, the potential for environmental damage is significant. For better insight from professionals, please look into the metrics around data centers and water usage. With the increasing accessibility of Gen AI, there has been a trend of attacking artists by plugging their art into AI algorithms. Not only is this dehumanizing, it targets and exposes artists' hard work and livelihood for childish self-satisfaction. With generative AI, scams are rampant. With the alarming ability to create realistic images, more and more fake products fill the market. Soon, you won't be able to trust anything you see online. Overall, the concept behind AI technology can be very exciting, but the negatives currently outweigh any significant positive products. AI eliminates human jobs around human expression, and there are not enough laws to protect us. By limiting your use and interest, contribute to popping the AI bubble. Don't sacrifice human expression for novelty.
