Your AI isn't lying to you on purpose. It's literally hallucinating. And nobody talks about why. ChatGPT and Claude predict the next word based on probability. They don't actually know facts. They pattern match from training data. So when they hit uncertain territory, they don't say, I don't know. Instead, they confidently generate the most statistically likely next word. It sounds real. It feels authoritative. But it's a fabrication. Think of it like your brain filling in gaps in your vision without telling you. The AI has no internal fact-checker, no database to reference, just statistical inference. This is why AI can cite fake papers, invent quotes from real people, and describe events that never happened, all while sounding completely certain.
