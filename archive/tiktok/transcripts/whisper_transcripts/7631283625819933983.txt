Water hungry data centers are drawing scrutiny as drought deepens across the southeast. It's the worst the area is seen in 25 years and water restrictions just kicked in in Raleigh. So there are limits to how much you can water your lawn. But what about data centers using millions of gallons a day? 36 different states are proposing or have laws to increase oversight over that water use and North Carolina isn't one of them. Many facilities don't publicly disclose how much water they use, but industry estimates show a single hyperscale data center can use up to a billion gallons a year and 2.7 million gallons a day in peak heat. That's about 180 swimming pools worth of water or 159,000 showers. And unlike most water use, some of that is lost to evaporation and doesn't go back into the local river basin.
