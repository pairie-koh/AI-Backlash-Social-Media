In case you guys didn't know, TikTok is now feeding your content into AI. Yes, you heard me correctly. TikTok is allowing AI to use and remix your content. However, there is an option to prevent this. So, if you post art, music, or just don't want any of your videos in here being stolen to train AI, here's a tutorial on how to find and turn off this setting. First, open up one of your videos and press the three dots in the bottom right corner. Next, you're going to want to scroll through the options until you see privacy settings. Tap that button and you should see the option to turn off allowing AI to remix your videos. If you don't see this, then the update hasn't been implemented for you. Something to note is that you'll have to do this manually for all of your videos. There isn't an option to automatically turn off AI remixing for all videos yet, at least from what I've seen so far.
