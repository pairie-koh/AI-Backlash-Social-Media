Everybody is experimenting with AI right now, but I learned something the hard way. I have been experimenting in the AI space for over a year now. I've built automated workflows, vibe coding, testing tools, just really trying to get an understanding of how it all works. And then I did what I thought other people doing, which is bringing in a third party to scale, because in theory, that's the entrepreneurial thing to do, right? But here's what no one really tells you. AI is moving so fast that it's very easy to get comfortable. When everything is automated and the output looks so good, you stop asking questions and looking closely and you just trust the system. And that's where things can go very, very wrong. Because AI doesn't have judgment, it doesn't have values, and it doesn't know the difference between inspiration and replication. And that part is on you. It will always be on you. So I still use AI. I'm really excited about the technology. I'm just using it a lot differently these days. So a lot more intentional, hands-on, everything in-house because the technology is exciting and it's incredible, but the responsibility that will always be human.
