New AI cameras that can catch drivers breaking the law are being rolled out from today, with artificial intelligence able to capture six separate driver offenses. It's claimed that the AI cameras can capture drivers speeding, using their phone while driving, failing to wear a seatbelt, failing to stop at a red light, driving without a valid MOT, and driving without insurance. The software works by capturing footage of passing vehicles, which is then run through AI. Two precise photos are taken, one taken at an angle that captures as whether or not the driver has a phone to their ear and if their seatbelt is being worn, the second is taken from a deeper angle which can capture if the driver is messaging on their device. The software is capable of capturing images during the day or at night in almost any weather conditions. The state of the art cameras are set to be deployed in the Greater Manchester area from September 3rd as part of a wider national trial. Greater Manchester isn't the first UK region to try out the tech as a similar trial in Cornwall last year saw 300 Brits caught out within just three days.
