I got suspended for using ChatGPT on one of my college essays, and this is what you should do to avoid making the same mistakes as me. What you guys don't realize is that when you copy and then paste into Google Docs, and then press Ctrl H, what you'll notice is that your text spacing is completely off, which is how AI checkers like turn it in from your school, flag your work, which is exactly how I got caught. It's okay, because I got you guys. What you want to do is open this tool called Cluely, click ask a question, and then just paste the same prompt you had on ChatGPT. Now copy the response, paste it. As you can see, if we press Ctrl H and then space again, the spacing issue is completely fixed and we don't need to worry about getting flagged by AI checkers. The best thing about Cluely is that you can move it around and have it as an overlay around whatever you're doing and ask it questions whenever you want without having to switch tabs to ChatGPT every single time.
