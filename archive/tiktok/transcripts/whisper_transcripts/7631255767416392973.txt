It's questionable to use a person's likeness and voice without permission, right? Right? If you answer no, that's exactly why we need the No Fakes Act. If passed, the No Fakes Act will create a federal right to protect the image, voice, and likeness of an artist during their life and up to 70 years after. That means AI produced music of an artist without their consent or using AI to create potentially compromising deepfakes would have major repercussions. That's something the Grammys can stand behind.
