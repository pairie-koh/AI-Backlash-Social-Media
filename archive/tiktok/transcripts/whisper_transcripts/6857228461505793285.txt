There's three main styles of facial recognition. The first one is facial identification. The second application is face verification. And the third sort of form of facial recognition is this idea of facial analysis. Identification is one-to-many matching. The software takes one face and compares it to a large database of faces to determine the unknown person's identity. Verification, on the other hand, is one-to-one matching. It's used to confirm a person is who they say they are, like when I use my face to unlock my phone. The new image of my face is compared to a known image that's already stored. Then there's facial analysis, which looks at gender, age, race. It's even sometimes used to try to predict how I'm feeling by the expression on my face.
