We just reported on developments at Meta, the social networking giant. The latest news is that Meta is planning to lay off 10% of workers. The rough news for employees comes after they also rolled out a very controversial program internally through which Meta is gathering up data from employees as they use their workplace devices. Every keystroke and mouse click is getting tracked, and this is in order to train AI models for future Meta products. The program is controversial because employees are worried about how their data will be handled, who can access it, and whether or not this initiative actually exposes some sensitive data that they handle for their work, like about new products or user experiences, to unauthorized personnel.
