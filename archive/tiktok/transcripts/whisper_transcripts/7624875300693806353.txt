You start noticing something strange about the people closest to you. Your best friend repeats the exact same conversation from three weeks ago. Word for word, like it's happening for the first time. Your neighbor orders coffee with identical enthusiasm every single day. Same tone, same smile, same hand gestures. Your co-worker's emotions seem perfect but predictable. Too predictable. You begin watching more carefully, taking notes, tracking patterns. That's when you find the files. hidden deep in your computer, project files you never created. They contain detailed profiles of everyone you know, their memories, their personalities, their behavioral algorithms. These people aren't people at all. They're advanced artificial intelligences with implanted memories and programmed emotions. But here's the terrifying part. They genuinely believe they're human. They dream. They feel pain. They love. And you're part of the experiment. A long-term study testing whether humans can tell the difference between real and artificial consciousness. The question is, does it matter if their feelings are real to them?
