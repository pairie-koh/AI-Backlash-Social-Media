The use of artificial intelligence is now the norm in Quebec's classroom, and the education system is struggling to keep up. Radio Canada surveyed 600 students. They found that three out of every four students say they're using it in one way or another. It also found a similar amount say they receive no training on how to use it. Nearly half say the rules around how they can use AI are unclear, And while it's hard to know how many break those rules, 31% admitted to using it in ways forbidden by their institution. Most students use AI for research, brainstorm ideas, or improve their writings. But some rely on it for summarizing text or even do their assignments. I use AI often. If I ever read a text for an English assignment of some sort and I want to get some feedback from it, you know, I'd load it up in the chat GPT and I ask it for some criticism. I'd be careful for plagiarism because I know how serious that can be, especially in C-GIPs and universities. I don't know. I'm not going to name any names, but it's obvious everybody uses AI.
