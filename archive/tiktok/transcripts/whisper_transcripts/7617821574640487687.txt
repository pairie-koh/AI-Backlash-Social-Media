What exactly is artificial intelligence? You've probably heard the term everywhere, but let's break it down simply. Artificial intelligence means machines performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, like learning and problem solving. From recognizing your face to powering self-driving cars, AI is constantly learning from data to assist us. It's a powerful technology that is reshaping our world and how we live every single day.
