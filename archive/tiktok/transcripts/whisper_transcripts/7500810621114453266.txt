Long ago, Earth was full of life, glowing oceans, skies that whispered peace. But slowly, the balance broke. Flames rose, storms grew, ice melted, and the planet began to tremble. Rivers dried, forests died, the air turned still. The world grew quiet and sad. A girl knelt in the silence, holding the last flower, crying for what had been lost. The seas filled with poison. Machines roared louder than the wind. Even the birds forgot how to sing. Far away, the cold lands melted. The ice cracked, and the penguins stopped moving. But still, she hoped. She placed her hand on the ground, and the earth answered just a little. A single patch of green. A few steps of life. Beyond her circle, the world remained gray, waiting.
