We get asked why we don't allow AI art on teaser, and here's why. It's not innovation, it's theft. People say AI makes art more accessible, but most AI art doesn't come from nowhere. It's trained on real artists' work, downloaded, scraped, used without consent. This isn't inspiration, it's exploitation. When platforms flood with AI content, they push actual artists out of their own space, while someone else profits off of their style. We don't stand behind that. We don't reward shortcuts built on stolen effort. It erases the human in art. Art isn't just a final image, it's a process. Years of practice, self-doubt, growth, emotion, every stroke, every detail comes from a human expression. AI skips all of that. AI doesn't ask permission. It doesn't credit anyone. It mimics, then moves on. It's not just unauthentic, it's unethical. At Teaser, we don't believe in replacing artists. We exist to celebrate and support the creators behind the work, to give them a platform to show off their true art. Support independent artists today at teaser.com.
