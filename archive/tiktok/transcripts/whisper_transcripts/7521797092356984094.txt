AI is killing Google search traffic and Google is paying the price. Here's how they're trying to fix it. With AI Answers showing up instantly, people are clicking fewer links and the publishers are losing traffic fast. To fix the damage, Google just launched something called the Offer Wall. It's a new tool that gives users access to premium content like news articles or how-tos in exchange for simple actions. Signing up for a survey, signing up for a newsletter, or watching an ad. It's Google's way of saying, sorry we nuked your traffic. Here's a new revenue stream. Will this be enough? Critics say it's just a band-aid, not a real solution to how AI is reshaping the web.
