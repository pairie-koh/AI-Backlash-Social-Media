China's electricity consumption reached a major milestone in 2025, surpassing 10 trillion kilowatt hours for the first time. It's more than double that of the United States and higher than the combined total of the EU, Russia, India and Japan. Meanwhile, the rapid expansion of artificial intelligence is fueling a sharp rise in demand for computing power, and with that, a growing appetite for electricity. So how can China's power supply keep pace with the fast-growing computing infrastructure? I believe we need action on several fronts. First, power planning and computing infrastructure should be coordinated at the national level, so they are planned and built together. We should also strengthen technologies that ensure reliable power supply for data centers. Another priority is to promote projects in Western China that combine green electricity, energy storage and computing clusters, so clean energy can be used locally. use locally. And finally, we need more cross-disciplinary talent who understand both power systems and digital technology. For Deputy He Guanghua, the message is clear.
