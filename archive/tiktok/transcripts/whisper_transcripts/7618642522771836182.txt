An ad for an AI tool that implies you can remove women's clothes has been banned. This YouTube advert for a video and image editing tool has been taken down for looking like it could digitally remove a person's clothing. It's for software PixVideo AI Video Maker and here you can see the before and after of the young woman which removes part of her clothing to show her exposed skin. Following complaints of the Advertising Standards Authority which regulates these ads of the video sexualizing and objectifying women, it has been banned. The ASA said it was irresponsible, included a harmful stereotype, and was likely to cause serious offense. The company, which owns the software, says its terms prohibited the creation of nude or sexually explicit images, and that the app believed the support nor was designed to make such content. However, they accepted the concerns around the ad's presentation and voluntarily suspended all advertising while they investigate.
