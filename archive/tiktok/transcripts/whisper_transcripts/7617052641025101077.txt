Your use of AI is killing the planet. In 2025, the carbon footprint of AI was equivalent to the entire city of New York, between 33 million and 80 million tons of carbon dioxide. AI data centers that are being built today are expected to each consume as much electricity as 2 million households. Because electricity in the U.S. is mostly powered by fossil fuels, data centers emit a lot of greenhouse gases. AI data centers also used as much water in 2025 as the total amount of bottled water people drink globally in an entire year. This is due to their evaporative cooling systems. Although some of the water can be recycled, 80% of it evaporates. So the next time you use AI to write an email, think about the impact on the environment first.
