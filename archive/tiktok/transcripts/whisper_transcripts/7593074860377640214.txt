AI might be killing the planet. Data centers worldwide already account for 3.7% of global greenhouse gas emissions, and their total electricity consumption could reach 1,000 terawatt hours a year in 2026, about the same as Japan's annual demand. In 2025, AI-related data centers emitted 75 million tons of CO2, a footprint comparable to New York City. AI also strains water resources to cool its vast centers. By 2027, AI data centers could consume up to 6.6 billion cubic meters of water, equivalent to two-thirds of England's annual water use. With AI demand growing exponentially, big tech is investing billions in clean energy to power its data centers. Google, Microsoft and Meta have agreed multi-billion dollar nuclear and renewable energy deals.
