AI could double global data center electricity use. This decade, training large AI models requires massive compute clusters. One hyperscale data center can use as much power as a small city. Analysts project global data center. Electricity demand could more than double by 2030, driven largely by generative AI workloads. Even companies buying renewable energy credits still rely on local grids that may be powered by gas or coal at peak hours. AI may be digital, but its footprint is physical. The next energy race isn't oil, it's compute. Can clean power scale fast enough?
