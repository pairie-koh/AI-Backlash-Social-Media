We're about to see an AI company pay out the biggest copyright settlement in all of history, over a billion dollars, but is it enough? Quick recap, last year a group of authors sued AI company Anthropic for training their AI model on pirated copies of their books. They settled this weekend agreeing to pay out $3,000 for each of the 500,000 books stolen, totaling around $1.5 billion in total payout, the biggest copyright recovery in all of history. This might seem like a big win for creators, but I'm not so sure that it is, because remember just last week Anthropic raised another funding round of $13 billion at a $183 billion evaluation, meaning that the fine they're paying for creating a product built off of stolen data is less than 1% of what that product is now worth. If someone can steal $100 million and the fine if they get caught is having to pay a million back, they're going to do it every time.
