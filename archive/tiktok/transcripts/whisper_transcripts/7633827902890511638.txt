You don't need to use Figma to get decent UI designs when vibe coding. Let's say this is your vibe-coded project, but you now want to add new sections to this project. Don't just vaguely describe what you want because you'll waste credits on generic results. If you go to this website, add a screenshot of your current UI. Then just describe what you want to add. It then uses all the best coding models to generate multiple variants for your project. But here's the best part. Each model generates their unique version. So all you have to do is pick the one you like best, copy the design prompt, and paste it into your builder. Now you get the best design for your project, without burning through all your credits.
