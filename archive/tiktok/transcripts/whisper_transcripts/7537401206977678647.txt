Your use of AI is killing the planet. Asking chat GPT to write one email is the equivalent of pouring out an entire water bottle or powering 14 light bulbs for one hour. AI runs on servers kept in hot data centers that need water to cool the equipment. It also requires a lot of energy to run these servers. One chat GPT request requires nearly 10 times as much energy as a Google search. By 2027, AI could use the amount of electricity that Argentina uses in a year. Fossil fuels are the main source of energy used to power AI. Google's greenhouse gas emissions have risen by 48% in the last five years due to AI energy demand. So next time you use AI to write an email because you're too lazy or generate an image for a meme, think about the impact on the environment first.
