The developer behind Dead by Daylight, Behavior Interactive, has announced that they have laid off an undisclosed amount of employees. This report comes from GameDeveloper.com, who reached out to Behavior Interactive on a statement, and here's what they have to say. A portion of Behavior Interactive's business has traditionally focused on external development partnerships in recent months. Demand for mobile and casual external development projects have declined. as we conclude our remaining engagements and do not foresee comparable opportunities in the near term we have made the decision to part ways with some colleagues those decisions are never easy and we are grateful for the contributions of the infected employees who have helped shaped behavior and support our partners over the years hopefully behavior interactive is able to help out the people that they laid off, particularly just because as it is now, it is harder to find a job in the gaming industry. According to Jason Schreier of Bloomberg, it can take up to a year to find a new job in the industry right now. It's been wild. So hopefully these people get some help.
