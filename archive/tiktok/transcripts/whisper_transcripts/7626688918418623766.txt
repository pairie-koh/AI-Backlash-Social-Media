What if the AI you trust most is actually lying to you and you'd never even know it? Researchers at the Rochester Institute of Technology just dropped a bombshell. They built a tool specifically designed to test whether AI chatbots actually tell the truth. And what they found should make everyone stop and think. They ran major AI models through rigorous fact-checking scenarios, and some of the most popular chatbots out there, including Gemini and DeepSeek, failed in ways that were genuinely alarming. We're not talking about small mistakes or typos. We're talking about confidently stated false information delivered with the same calm, authoritative tone as accurate facts. But here's where it gets really interesting. Most users have absolutely no way to tell the difference. The AI doesn't hesitate, doesn't stutter, doesn't add a disclaimer. It just sounds completely certain. The surprising part is this tool might be the wake-up call the entire industry needs. Because if we can't trust AI to be honest, we need to build systems that hold it accountable. Stay curious out there.
