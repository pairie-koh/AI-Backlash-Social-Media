A 52-year-old man has been accused of orchestrating a massive AI music fraud scheme, earning him over $10 million in streaming royalties. Michael Smith used AI to generate hundreds and thousands of tracks. He uploaded these tracks to Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon Music, and then programmed his army of bots to play them repeatedly. By doing this, he managed to generate billions of streams and millions of dollars. In an effort to outsmart the fraud detection algorithm, Smith generated so many tracks that no one track would create suspicion. At one point telling his co-conspirators they needed a ton of songs fast. But the FBI got involved and Smith's scheme came crashing down. They charged him with wire fraud and money laundering and if found guilty, Smith can expect to be spending decades in prison.
