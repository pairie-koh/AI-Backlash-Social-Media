Someone on this app asked, why is it that brands don't understand we don't want AI? The answer is very simple. The companies and brands that are pushing AI love the sales pitch that they were given, which is, here's a machine that can replace all your workers so you don't have to pay anyone ever again. Fine printed that sales pitch, also told those companies, by the way, unless we can get the general public interested in this tool as well, there won't be enough money for us to run it and replace them like we want to. Generative AI is not a tool that is designed to help the general public. It is a tool that is designed to hurt them so that the business owners and capitalists can get exceedingly rich. But the problem is it doesn't work unless the general public also likes this product, which they know is not designed to help them. Companies in charge of the generative AI industry desperately want to make it the next big thing, but they're overlooking that for it to be the next big thing, it has to actually have a reasonable use to the consumer that is not already taken care of by something else. For example, when the internet launched, it was something that quite literally every human in history has dreamed of having access to all of the information and instantaneous communication with anyone in the world at the push of a button When home computers became the next big thing in the 80s and 90s it made sense because it provided you a digital space to do all the work that you wanted without having to make actual space for the physical minutia that those tasks would otherwise take up Smartphones became the next big thing. It filled the one gap that everyone wanted filled with the internet, that it wasn't mobile. Smartphones made the internet mobile, made information mobile, and made communication more mobile. Printing press removed the need for physical scribes and introduce the ability to distribute information at a mass volume level. The problem with generative AI is it doesn't fill a gap. It just approximates what you want to fill the gap, and there are other tools that already do a better job of it. AI can guess at the fact that you want to look up, but it's quicker to just go through a search engine and read the information yourself. AI can approximate the image that you want it to generate, but again, it's easier and more precise to just do it yourself with Photoshop or ZBrush. AI can try to generate art that's to your taste, but taste is so specific that it can only guess at what you want, and it's easier and more accurate to just do it yourself. You have corporations and brands who are desperate for you to use AI because otherwise they can't use it, but they don't know how to make it appealing to you because there's nothing it provides that you can't already do better without it.
