ChatGPT just got a lot smarter and your life is about to get a lot more productive. OpenAI has launched Tasks, a revolutionary feature that lets you schedule ChatGPT to perform tasks in the future. Need a reminder for an important call? Done. Want to get a daily news briefing every morning? Easy. Here's how to set it up. Open ChatGPT, click your profile icon and select Tasks. Then select Try Your Own. This will switch you over to GPT 4.0 with scheduled tasks. Now just type in your tasks, like send me a crypto market report every night. and ChatGPT will automatically create one for you. You can even change the task name, instructions or schedule by clicking the three dots. When a task is due, you'll get a notification with the completed information. This is currently available to Pro and Plus subscribers, but will be available to all users soon. So, what will you use tasks for?
