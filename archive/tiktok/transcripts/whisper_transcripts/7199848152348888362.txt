If you're a software engineer, your job is in danger within the next seven years. We all know that ChatGPT can be used to generate code, but did you know that OpenAI is training ChatGPT to completely replace software engineers? A new report just came out that OpenAI has hired hundreds of contractors to train ChatGPT with the goal of making basic coding practices obsolete. Instead, developers can move past the point of mundane coding and focus their efforts on more complex tasks. While OpenAI has already released Codex, a program that converts natural language into code, recent hiring indicates further advancements in this technology. Amazon software engineers are already using ChatGPT to aid in coding efforts, and it's only a matter of time before manual coding is a job of the past.
