Most people have no idea how much pressure is building on the power grid right now. Here is what is actually happening. Every AI task uses up to 1,000 times more electricity than a basic web search. 1,000 times. And millions of people are running AI searches every single day. U.S. data centers already consume about 4% of all electricity in the country. By 2030, that number could more than double. The strain is already showing up. In 2024, a voltage fluctuation in northern Virginia disconnected 60 data centers at once. The grid had to make emergency adjustments to prevent a wider outage. Experts at Gartner now predict that 40% of AI data centers could face power shortages by 2027. And here is the part most people are not thinking about. As electricity demand keeps climbing, the grid gets stretched thinner. Aging infrastructure was simply not built for this level of demand. When the grid gets overloaded, urban areas feel it first. apartments, condos, dense neighborhoods with no backup power and no warning. The question is not if outages will happen.
