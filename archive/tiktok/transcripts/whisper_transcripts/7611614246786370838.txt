What happens if you never die? A1. Nothing changes. You feel normal. Year 1. You notice something strange. You don't get sick. Year 10. Friends begin to aid. You don't. Year 30. Wrinkles appear on everyone else. Your face stays the same. Year 50. The first funeral hits hard. Then another. Then dozen. Year 80. Everyone you grew up with, gone. You're still here. Year 120. You stop telling people you're real age. No one would believe you. Year 200, languages evolve, slang feels foreign, you start feeling out of place. Year 500, technology feels like magic, you've watched civilization upgrade itself again and again. Year 1000, you've had hundreds of names, hundreds of lives, none of them permanent. Millions of years pass, oceans evaporate, the planet cracks, you drift through space. No air, no sound, but you can't die. Stars burn out, galaxies fade, the universe grows cold, and you're still awake.
