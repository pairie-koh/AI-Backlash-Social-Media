Everyone keeps saying AI is coming for your job, but in the UAE, it might actually be creating way more of them. UAE officials say they will need to add around a million workers by 2030 as AI, digital transformation, and economic growth speed up. That's a 12% jump in the workforce. And tech roles alone are expected to grow even faster, with demand up 54% by the end of the decade. That growth rate surpasses the US, UK, and India. And this isn't just about coders. We're talking jobs in operations, retail, education, healthcare, finance, cloud, cybersecurity, and data too. And at a time of regional uncertainty, the fact that the UAE is still planning for more jobs, not fewer, says a lot about where it thinks growth is headed. So if you're looking for your next move, the UAE might be one market worth watching.
