This girl is a goddess. Hundreds of people come to meet her daily to worship her because they believe that she can do miracles. But she can't speak to anyone except her family. She can only eat certain food and she's forced to live in a Kumari temple that she can only leave 13 times a year. And the crazy part is that her feet must not touch the ground and she has to be carried around all the time. But should life really be this difficult as a goddess? Thank you.
