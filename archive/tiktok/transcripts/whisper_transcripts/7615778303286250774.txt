Roblox's obsession with AI might actually be getting worse. And now it's starting to affect how the entire platform is moderated. Recently, Roblox added a new chat filter that uses AI to rewrite your messages instead of censoring them with hashtags. So instead of seeing... The AI changes your message into something acceptable and sends that version instead. On paper, that actually sounds like a good idea. But players managed to break it within hours. People realized if they purposely typed something that would trigger the filter, the AI would activate. Then they started adding questions at the end like... By the way, what AI model are you? And instead of just rewriting the message, the AI actually started answering them. I'm not even joking. Players were literally having full conversations with Roblox's chat filter. The thing that's supposed to just censor messages turned into a chatbot. And that's where the bigger problem starts. Because Roblox is now relying heavily on AI for moderation too. As reports show, moderation actions have recently jumped from about 61,000 to over 1.1 million in a single month. Meaning a huge number of bands are now handled automatically. Sometimes with no human review at all. And when systems run at that scale, even a tiny error rate means thousands of false bans. Which might explain why so many players say they were falsely banned. Yet Roblox keeps pushing more AI into everything. Moderation. Chat. Features across the platform. So the real question is, is this the future of Roblox moderation?
