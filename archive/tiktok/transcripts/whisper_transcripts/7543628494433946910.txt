This is the future of AI-powered stores. What you're seeing is a coffee shop, but it's not just any shop, because this coffee shop has AI software running 24-7 in all their cameras. You see those green boxes? Each one of those is a customer, and the AI is monitoring how long each person has been sitting in the cafe. But here's what's crazy. It's also monitoring the workers. You can see it's tracking their movement with those squiggly lines and their productivity, like how many drinks each person has served per hour. The software is called NeuroSpot, and it uses AI and machine learning to live track and analyze humans in physical stores. Now, of course, as a customer, my immediate reaction is shock and fear. It feels super weird to know you're being tracked everywhere you go. But as a business owner, having access to this level of real-time analytics could be super valuable. For example, if you ran a buffet, this software could immediately detect which areas were less popular, so you knew how to rotate the menu. Or if you ran a hardware store, you could study which aisles people linger the longest as a signal for confusion in signage and layouts. There are endless applications for this type of data. Not to mention CCTV AI, it'd be a lot harder to steal stuff. But the real reason this is so valuable is that this footage is going to be used to train robots. The best way for robots to learn is to watch humans complete the same task over and over. And because of that, I would not be shocked to see this type of AI camera software start popping up everywhere.
