Campaigners and disability charities have slammed the accounts saying they promote negative stereotypes, they're fetishising and they're drowning out authentic disabled voices. But it's not just the presence of these profiles that's causing concern, it's how they're being made. One researcher told the BBC that while these accounts are making pictures of fake women, the data sets these AI tools are drawing on are using images of real people. People who likely didn't consent to being in the data set or for their photo to be used in this manner. After flagging the accounts Meta, Instagram's parent company, says it's investigating.
