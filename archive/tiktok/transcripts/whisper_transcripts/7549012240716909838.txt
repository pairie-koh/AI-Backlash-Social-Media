Did you know your favorite songs might have been stolen by AI? ICMP, the international music publisher group, just dropped some crazy receipts. And they say AI companies like OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft, even X, scratch copyright tracks from The Beatles, Beyonce, Mariah Carey, The Weeknd, millions of songs. And they're using it to train AI music models. That means robot brains are learning your favorite music without asking anyone. We're talking about what ICMP calls the largest IP theft in human history. IP meaning intellectual property. Songs that took artists years to craft are now being used without their permission.
