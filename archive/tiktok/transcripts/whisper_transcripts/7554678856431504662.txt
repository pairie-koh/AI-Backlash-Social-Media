Make the Internet on your phone faster. How to increase Internet speed. All you need to do is adjust two settings. First, in the main settings on your phone, go to the Connections section. Now here, select More Connection Settings and go to Provided DNS. By default, it is set to automatic. you need to tick Provide DNS Provider hostname and enter, as I have, DNS.Google and press Save. After this, go to the Chrome browser. Click the three dots in the top right corner and go to Settings. Here, go to Privacy and Security and find the setting called Use. Secure DNS Here it is also set to default. You need to select the option Choose another provider. Here click and choose Google Public DNS. After this your internet will work much faster. Enjoy.
