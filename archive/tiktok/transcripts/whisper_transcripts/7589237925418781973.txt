This one cooling method made it possible to remove every single fan from a server. As the world's data piled up, servers kept getting hotter, and nearly 40% of a data center's power was spent just on cooling. So engineers chose a radical solution, submerging servers directly into liquid, known as immersion cooling. The liquid is dielectric, meaning electricity can't flow through it, but heat can. Heat transfers far more efficiently than through air, cutting total power costs by up to 40%. With fans gone, noise and vibration disappear, extending server lifespan. The liquid also blocks oxygen, slowing oxidation and corrosion. The upfront cost is still high, but in the end, won't the servers of the future all end up underwater?
