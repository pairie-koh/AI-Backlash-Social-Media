Attention smartphone users, on a hidden service marketplace on the cyber criminal forums, a seller calling themselves USD's Advertising Interceptor, a piece of covert spyware. Here's what it claims to do. It snatches your text messages in real time, even the so-called silent text messages that never show up on your screen. They track your exact location, log every call, and peek into installed apps. They have live listen capability to your phone calls when paired with a small plug-in hardware box. The package even ships with its own remote access Trojan, but it requires the victim's phone to be jailbroken first. Right now, no specific person or company is known to be hit. This is a tool for sale. Still, it highlights two simple safety steps. Keep your phone fully updated and never sideload unknown apps. Enable multi-factor authentication so even intercepted SMS codes can't unlock your account. Stay alert. The next malware you never see could already be listening.
