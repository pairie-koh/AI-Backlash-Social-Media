AI chatbots are struggling with news accuracy, according to BBC Research. Testing ChatGPT, CoPilot, Gemini, and Perplexity, 51% of answers had significant issues, with 19% introducing factual errors. BBC News CEO Deborah Ternis warns of potential real-world harm from AI distorted headlines. Examples include incorrect statements about NHS vaping recommendations and misquoting BBC News on Middle East events. The BBC calls for tech companies to pull back AI news summaries and work together to find solutions. OpenAI responded, stating they support publishers by helping users discover quality content through summaries and attribution. The study highlights the need for improved AI accuracy in news interpretation and summarization.
