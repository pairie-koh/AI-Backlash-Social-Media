Face value that is super impressive and kind of makes AI seem a little bit scary, but AI music is actually not as relevant as it seems right now. People think that people are just typing in make a Drake song or make a Kanye song and it's spitting stuff like that out. It's not. It's basically just a glorified vocal filter. To my knowledge, every single AI song that you hear are songs that already exist. They're using the original song, the original instrumental, and reference vocals from whoever they're trying to get to sing it and basically just filtering the voice of the original song to sound like the reference vocals from the celebrity. The quality of the AI cover is basically determined by how good the reference vocals are. Like, that's why all the Ariana covers sound good. It's because they have good reference vocals of her actually hitting the notes so it's easy to match up.
