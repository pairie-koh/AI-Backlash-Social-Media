I want to talk about banning chat GPT in schools because I've received a lot of questions about this. Yes, the AI system can write essays and do students' homework and that's already been happening. Banning chat GPT is really a step in the wrong direction. The whole purpose of education is to equip students with the skills they'll need for the economy of the future. Economy that will be largely underpinned by artificial intelligence including systems like chat GPT. Schools should be the very place that students start to learn how to use these tools. Knowledge and how we test for knowledge changes over time. It always has. The invention of the calculator meant that we tested for math knowledge in new ways, challenged students with more complicated math concepts as a result. A world where AI can write an essay, it doesn't mean that knowledge goes away. It means that we test for knowledge in new ways. For anyone who thinks that technology means students will no longer have to think, a viewpoint goes back thousands of years. This guy right here, Plato, believed that writing, a relatively new technology at the time, meant that people would no longer undergo deep thinking, that orality was far superior. Now here we are, arguing in favor of a technology that was once seen as a threat to thinking.
