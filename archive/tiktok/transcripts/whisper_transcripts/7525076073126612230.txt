My name is Nongmaeba. I'm a field marketing agent for Betway, working in the Tembisa area. I've been with Marketing to the Max for four years, and I've been proudly representing Betway for the past year. Every day I go out into the field to visit spazer shops allocated to me as part of my call cycle. At each store, I speak to the shop owner, take them through the Betway product, and make sure they understand how it works. I update them on any new campaigns, promotions or important information from the brand. And I'm always available to answer any questions they might have. After our chat, I ask for permission to put up the battery signage outside the store. It helps increase visibility and drives food traffic. Before I move on, I take a picture of the shop brand with the branding and upload it into a field marketing app. That's how we track our visits and make sure everything's done properly. It might sound simple, but it makes a big difference. I enjoy being out in the community, building relationships and helping Betway show up in all the right places.
