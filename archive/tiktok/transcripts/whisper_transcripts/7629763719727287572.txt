Here is an open world co-op game where you and your friends have to smuggle sketchy cargo across the ocean. Use tack illegal cargo while your friend gets the airplane ready to take off and others help navigate and keep the plane running. You have to be fast and be aware of storms, risky landings and police because if you get caught it's all over. And when you're not flying, repair your aircraft, upgrade it and customize it to be faster and more efficient. This is Cartel Pilots Wounded and it's coming soon to steam.
