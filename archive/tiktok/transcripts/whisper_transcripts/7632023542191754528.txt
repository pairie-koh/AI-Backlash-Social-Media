Artificial intelligence is often described as the technology that will replace humans. It writes texts, generates images, automates decisions, and seems to improve every day. Naturally, this raises an important question. What will be left for us? But if we look closer, something interesting happens. AI does not think. It does not understand. It does not create meaning on its own. It waits for instructions, for prompts, for humans to tell it what to do. The more advanced it becomes, the more precise those instructions need to be. Suddenly, the most powerful technology in the world depends on one thing, clear human thinking. So maybe the question is not whether AI will replace us, but whether we are able to clearly explain what we want. Because in the end, artificial intelligence is only as intelligent as the human behind it.
