Recent analysis shows artificial intelligence-driven data centers are rapidly reshaping global electricity demand. According to the International Energy Agency, data center electricity use could nearly double to 945 terawatt-hours by 2030, representing nearly 3% of global consumption. What's more, a separate study finds generative AI workloads consume 10 to 30 times more energy than older, task-specific systems. For investors, this means the bottleneck isn't compute.
