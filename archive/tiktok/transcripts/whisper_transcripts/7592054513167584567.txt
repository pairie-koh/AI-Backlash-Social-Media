Over the 2026 New Year's break, I built a video thumbnail app that composites people in a video and the products into a single image. I found out the hard way why models like Nano Banana and OpenAI still kind of suck at procedural image tasks. Text is descriptive. Compositing is spatial, physical, and procedural. These image models don't follow rules. They follow probabilities. Prompts are not instructions. They're suggestions. The output is non-deterministic. You don't good steps, you get vibes. They're amazing at concept creation and ideation, and anywhere variability is a feature, not a bug. Great for generating ideas, terrible at following instructions.
