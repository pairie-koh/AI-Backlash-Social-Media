Have you seen any of these viral videos of Muslims supposedly complaining about life in Australia? One woman says there's too much pork in the shops while another fumes over non-halal chicken wraps. People are furious, but there's a massive catch. While these clips are gaining millions of views, AP Fact Check can confirm that these videos are completely fake. Every single person in these videos is an AI-generated character designed to stir up cultural tension. The page behind these videos is actually operated by a user in Sri Lanka, not Australia. And if you look closely, it's easy to tell the clips are synthetic. Butcher shop signs in the background list impossible items like shikits and pork lugs. Even the interviewer's microphone is covered in garbled nonsensical text that doesn't belong to any real news outlet. These AI errors are everywhere in the other clips as well. This is a classic case of using synthetic media to bait your emotions. So always check the background for weird text or warping before you get angry. Thank you.
