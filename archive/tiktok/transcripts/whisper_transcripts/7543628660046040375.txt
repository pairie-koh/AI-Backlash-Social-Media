Did you know most AI generated images are not legally usable in your social content or advertising? Is this true? Kind of is. Here's how it goes. If you're using Midjourney to create images for product packaging, or if you're using ChatGPT to write product description for your new wellness brand, that is totally fine to use those images and use that text in connection with your product or your service or your brand. But you do not then own IP rights in those things. And this goes back to the basic fact that as of today, in August of 2025, content made using generative AI is not subject to US copyright law. And that is because there are three requirements to have copyright protection in the United States. The thing needs to be original, fixed to a tangible medium, and must have human authorship. This last requirement around human authorship is the basis that generative AI content does not enjoy copyright protection. So to be clear, if you create an ad using generative AI, your competitor can probably make the exact same ad and there might not be much you can do about it.
