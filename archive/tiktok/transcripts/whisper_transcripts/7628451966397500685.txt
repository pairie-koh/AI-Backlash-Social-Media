Vancouver-based Soma Energy raised a 7 million seed for AI-powered grid optimization. Now, I'm sure you know globally the grid is under a pretty serious crunch. What's interesting about Soma Energy is that the founders were previously at AWS working as energy managers. What they did is they essentially built a platform to help manage resources for the grid. This includes wind, solar, and battery. The idea is to optimize the grid for power producers and data centers in real time. Though the company is out of Canada, they have actually deployed 2 gigawatts of assets in the U.S. with 5 different data center customers. A 7 million seed round was led by Category Ventures, with participation from Haystack, Panash Ventures, RRE Ventures, TOVC, Uncork Capital, and Walter Kortschak participating.
