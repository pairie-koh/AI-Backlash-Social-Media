Texas is booming, but its water supply can't keep up. Key sources like the Ogallala Aquifer are shrinking, and severe droughts are intensifying the problem. By 2060, the state's total water supply is projected to decrease by a staggering 18%. Some cities are already facing critical shortages as reservoirs run dangerously low. Without serious action, Texas faces a future of water scarcity. The state is now planning for a future where every drop counts. Investing in conservation and new technologies to survive.
