I just finished three days learning from Tony Robbins and Dean Graziosi at the AI Advantage event. And one message really stood out to me. AI is not here to replace us. It's here to help us get our time back. Whether it's writing emails, creating content, or organizing ideas, AI can help us work smarter and move faster. For me, that means spending less time on busy work and more time serving my clients and being present with my family. The real advantage of AI is not just productivity, it's freedom. freedom to focus on what we do best, be more creative, and spend time doing what we love. So if you haven't started using AI yet, start small. Use it to simplify one task today, because when used the right way, AI helps us create more space for the things that matter most.
