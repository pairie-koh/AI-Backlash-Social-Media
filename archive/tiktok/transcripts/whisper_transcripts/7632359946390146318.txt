Amazon and Meta, they signed a deal spanning at least three years for Meta to deploy hundreds of thousands of AWS Graviton chips, making Meta one of the top five customers of Amazon's custom CPU. That's what Graviton is. And Graviton has actually gained adoption from Adobe, Apple, Snowflake, and even earlier this week, Anthropic. The deal is about price and efficiency, not necessarily scarcity, but it does signal a bigger shift in how AI gets built. Agentic AI doesn't just generate text. It takes actions, writes codes, coordinates tasks, and that work falls on CPUs, not necessarily GPUs. The industry standard has been one CPU for every eight GPUs. Intel's CEO just yesterday said it's right now at one CPU for every four GPUs, with Agentec AI pushing that ratio to one to one. Morgan Stanley estimates this shift could add roughly $60 billion in incremental CPU demand by 2030. Intel and AMD have hiked prices at least 10 to 15 percent this year, with lead time stretched to six months. Even Intel saying that they're going to be increasing prices in the second half. That's going to continue.
