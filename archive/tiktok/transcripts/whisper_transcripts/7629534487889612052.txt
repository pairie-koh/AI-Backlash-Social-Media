But today, lawyers were handed a warning of their own, told to be wary of over-reliance on artificial intelligence. Australia's Federal Court has issued a new practice note concerning the use of AI in the profession. Chief Justice Deborah Mortimer acknowledged that there are many benefits of the technology, including increasing efficiency, reducing costs and enhancing access to the justice system. But she pointed out it also poses a serious risk to the proper administration of justice and public confidence in the legal system. Under the new guidance, lawyers and solicitors are asked to confirm if they use AI to summarise information, make images or prepare documents. They have also been warned that AI can provide false information, which is unacceptable and will lead to serious consequences. And it's not just an Aussie problem.
