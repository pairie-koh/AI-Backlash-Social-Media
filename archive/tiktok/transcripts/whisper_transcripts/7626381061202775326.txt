Like dissecting a frog, making a baking soda volcano, mock debates, design projects. AI can't just grow hands and complete these physical tasks. And these activities just so happen to be the ones where students learn the most. If our modern school system was actually created correctly, AI wouldn't have caused nearly as much damage. Earlier, I mentioned how AI is leading to a decline in critical thinking, but there was never much critical thinking in the education process to begin with. How are students developing their critical thinking abilities by answering multiple choice questions, by being confined to a rubric when writing essays, by memorizing hundreds of terms? I think the rise of AI is going to benefit education as it's going to force modern education to finally change. At the rate that things are going right now, if schools don't adapt quickly, then the entire school system is going to crumble.
