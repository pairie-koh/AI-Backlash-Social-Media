When his idol passed away, this artist decided to honor him in a way that actually carried real emotion. And what he created turned out unbelievable. Because he grabbed a basketball covered in charcoal paint, pressed it over his face, and started painting without being able to see while using his entire body to pour everything into the artwork. And then after adding a few final highlights, with a lighter tone, he flips the canvas around. And that's when it hits you that he had been painting the whole thing upside down the entire time, revealing a stunning portrait of Kobe Bryant and his daughter. That looks absolutely incredible. So the real question is, would you actually pay $10,000 for it?
