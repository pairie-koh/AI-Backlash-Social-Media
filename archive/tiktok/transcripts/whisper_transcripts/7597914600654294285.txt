As somebody on the left, I'm worried that our sense of moral superiority is preventing us from using all the tools available to us to help ourselves and our neighbors. Now this post is specifically about AI, but it can be generalized. I'm aware of all the criticisms, the data centers, the water usage, etc. AI, horrible for the environment. However, if you can think of a use case where AI will help a family stay together out of harm's way and keep your community together, it is a no-brainer to use AI. If you're an activist who could use AI to automate certain tasks to make you more effective, free up time in your schedule, or just make your activism better, use it. That being said, be smart about it. Either host your own LLM or make sure that the service you use runs in a secure enclave with end-to-end encryption at the minimum. Remember, tools don't have morals assigned to them. It is all about how you use it. And the same goes for AI. Accomplishing goals in unprecedented times demands flexibility. At the end of the day, the only thing that matters is whether or not you achieved your objective, not how you actually did that.
