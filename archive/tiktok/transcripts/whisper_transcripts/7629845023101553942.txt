The deep fake voice scam that sounds real. It sounds like someone you trust, a boss, a friend, or a family. But it is not them. Scammers use AI tools to clone real voices from short audio clips online. Then they call you, sounding urgent and emotional, asking for money or sensitive information. Because the voice feels real, you don't question it. You react quickly. Some even mimic tone and background noise, making it more believable and harder to detect. The trick is simple. They rely on trust, not just technology. The defense is also simple. Always verify through another channel. Call back on a known number. Never act on pressure alone, even if the voice sounds familiar. Trust verification, not emotion.
