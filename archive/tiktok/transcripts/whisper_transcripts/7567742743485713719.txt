Cursor, the creators of the most popular AI coding IDE out there, just came out with their first AI coding model and it's pretty impressive. It's called Composer and what stands out about it is the fact that not only is it pretty powerful, but it is really, really fast. And that's something that we haven't seen a lot these days in the various coding LLMs out there. If you look at models like Claude Sonnet 4.5 or GPT-5, they boast really high scores on all the coding benchmarks. But if you actually use them in your day-to-day, they are very, very slow compared to Cursor. While models like Cloud Sonnet 4.5 and GPT-5 theoretically have better raw output, the speed at which the output comes out is slow enough that it could kind of take you out of your flow state, working state. Whereas Cursor's Composer model, at least according to the benchmarks, is almost as good as the Sonnet and the GPT-5s out there. But because the code output is so much faster, it actually helps me stay in a better flow state when working so I'm not twiddling my thumbs for 5 minutes waiting for an agentic coding task to complete. I'm only waiting like maybe 30 seconds a minute max, which is way, way faster. The Cursor team cooked pretty hard with this model, and I'm actually finding myself using the Composer model more and more, and using the Claude models a little bit less just because of how much faster it is.
