Florida officials say that ChatGPT helped an alleged gunman carry out a school shooting at Florida State University last year that killed two people. Now Fox News obtained those chat logs and they're chilling. If this were a person on the other end of the screen we would be charging them with murder. Florida's attorney general has opened a criminal investigation into open AI after ChatGPT answered questions about what gun and ammo to use, whether the gun would be useful in a short range and where on campus the shooter would encounter the most people. The 20-year-old alleged shooter asked very direct questions to ChatGPT, like how many victims would have to die in order to get national media attention. If there was a shooting at FSU, how would the country react? When was the last school shooting? The chatbot provided detailed responses and repeatedly asked if he wanted to know more information. In a statement, OpenAI says ChatGPT is not responsible for this crime, stating in part, quote, in this case, ChatGPT provided factual responses to questions with information that could be found broadly across public sources on the internet, and it did not encourage or promote illegal or harmful activity. Florida says this investigation is the first step in figuring out if OpenAI could be held criminally liable in this tragic shooting.
