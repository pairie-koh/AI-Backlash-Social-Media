There's a new corporate term out there and you need to pay attention to what it is. This is called DWT or dead workplace theory. Dead workplace theory is the concept that all very big strategy decisions all the way down to lower level work done from executives, C-level executives, middle managers is being done by AI. If you think about it at the highest level, they are using things like large language models, ChatGPT, Cloud Cowork, and they are intaking all the information from their employees, from vendors, from customers, and they're running it through these models. And the models are actually generating the output and helping these executives come up with a plan. So if they try to do a layoff, if they try to make a shift in their corporate strategy, it was advised by AI. No one is actually thinking for themselves anymore. If you think about the conversations that have been had from executives to other customers and things like that, how many of those emails back and forth or decisions were done by AI at this point? I would say within the last year, it's got to be up near 80%. Now, although a lot of these tech layoffs are due to AI, they're actually not. However, you start to think about the conversations being had between AIs and AIs at these companies, all it's going to take is a few more months or years of advancement before they can just talk to themselves and completely cut people out of the equation. But this is definitely a fascinating and interesting concept, and it makes you start to wonder about the future of work in this country.
