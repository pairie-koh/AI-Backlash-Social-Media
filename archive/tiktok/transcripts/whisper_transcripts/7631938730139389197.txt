How Publix used a no-AI disclaimer as a marketing weapon. Today, Publix dropped a two-minute ad featuring handmade puppets, real locations, and zero AI. A group of dog puppets bake a pie to apologize to their mailman for years of chasing him. That's it. But the no-AI badge in the credits is what made it spread. In a world where every brand is racing to automate everything, publishing, Publix positioned handmade as the premium signal. The same way organic became a badge in food, human-made is becoming a badge in content. The lesson? Sometimes the most disruptive thing you can do is just be the one brand that didn't use the tool everyone else is using.
