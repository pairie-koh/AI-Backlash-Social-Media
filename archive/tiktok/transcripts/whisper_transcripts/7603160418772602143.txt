Midjourney is an AI tool that creates images from text descriptions. You type a prompt describing a visual idea, and the AI generates images based on that input. This changes how visuals are created. Designers use Midjourney for inspiration and concept development. Marketers use it to create campaign visuals without traditional design workflows. The key skill with Midjourney is learning how to describe ideas clearly. Words become the starting point for visuals. In the future, creativity will depend less on tools and more on how well you communicate ideas.
