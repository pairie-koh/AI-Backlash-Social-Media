I think we are seeing early signs that a lot of the UX that is being used in chatbots and AI products today are not only not great, they are actually kind of creating bad experiences for users. And I would even go as far as saying creating harmful experience for users. You can read more details about what I mean in my sub stack here, but I really think there's an opportunity for UX designers to think about how to evolve the UX of AI chatbots to be ones that like one better recognize the current level of understanding and knowledge that a user currently has in using a product so products aren't just giving answers or just giving results or that they're actually helping someone think a little bit more along the way so not only are they getting the answer they're getting kind of these guided answers that build their understanding i also think there's some opportunities for us to be thinking about how to build more friction in these products in order to kind of respect the user's well-being i think we're seeing lots of signs that people are almost getting burned out through using some of these AI products because they send you in these loops of iteration and this feeling that you can start a bunch of projects without thinking about the cost of maintaining them. And I know a lot of designers have been kind of cut out of the product development process at different companies and different organizations, but I think these are in its core just human interaction problems that designers are so perfectly well positioned to solve.
