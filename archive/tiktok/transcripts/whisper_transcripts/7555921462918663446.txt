What does facial recognition technology see? First, it finds a face in the picture. It ignores the rest of the scene and just focuses on the face. Next, it maps key points that are unique to your face, like the distance between your eyes, the shape of your jaw, and where your nose and mouth sit. Think of it as a dot-to-dot pattern, which is then turned into your unique face print or a code. It then compares that code to find a match, or to check you are who you say you are. Some systems even use things like skin texture patterns, ear shape, hairline outline, 3D depth info, and infrared patterns.
