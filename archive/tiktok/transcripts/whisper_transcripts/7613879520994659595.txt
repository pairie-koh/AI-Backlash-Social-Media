The U.S. Supreme Court has made a major decision about AI-generated art. The court declined to hear a case over whether artwork created entirely by artificial intelligence can receive copyright protection. The dispute began when computer scientist Stephen Thaler tried to copyright an image called A Recent Entrance to Paradise, which he said was created by his AI system, DABIS. but the U.S. Copyright Office rejected the request, arguing that copyright law requires human authorship, a position later upheld by lower courts. By refusing to review the case, the Supreme Court leaves those rulings in place, meaning fully AI-generated artwork still cannot be copyrighted under U.S. law.
