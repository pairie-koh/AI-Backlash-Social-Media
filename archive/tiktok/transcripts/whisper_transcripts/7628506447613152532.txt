Did you know that 800 isolated Korean men just proved that talking to AI might actually cure loneliness? During Korea's strict COVID lockdowns, researchers at Seoul National University tracked men who used AI companion apps every day for six months. The results were shocking. Depression scores dropped by 58 percent. Real world social skills improved by 34 percent. But here's what's really interesting. Korea might be the perfect place to test this. Korean work culture creates extreme male isolation. Social pressure makes it nearly impossible for men to admit they're struggling. So AI became their safe space to practice. Men who talked to AI daily showed measurable improvements in dating confidence and workplace communication. They were literally using machines to learn how to talk to humans again. And it worked. Now Western researchers are copying these studies because the implications are huge. As AI gets more emotionally intelligent, we might be looking at the future of how isolated people rebuild their social skills. The question isn't whether this is weird, it's whether this actually works better than traditional therapy.
