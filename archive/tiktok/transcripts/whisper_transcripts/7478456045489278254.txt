If you ever try to hire someone, you know how annoying it is. You get tons of applications, and most people aren't even close to a fit. And sorting through all that is just a huge time suck. So we built an AI system that basically ranks every applicant for us instantly. Instead of wasting hours reading applications, this automation takes the form submission, sends it to ChatGPT, and then decides who moves forward, all without me touching a thing. First, when someone applies through a Google form, this webhook grabs their details and starts the process. Then ChatGPT will read their answers and rank them 1 to 10 based on what makes a great hire. There's no BS. It's just a simple number. So then that ranking gets sent here. If they score below a 7.4, they're out. If they pass, they move forward automatically. And finally, the system sends an email, either a rejection or an invite to our Slack group for shortlisted candidates. So that's it, really. There's no manual filtering. There's no wasted time.
