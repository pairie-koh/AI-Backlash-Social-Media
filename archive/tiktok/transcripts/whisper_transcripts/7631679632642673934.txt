Everybody keeps asking the wrong question about AI. They ask, will it take my job? That matters, but it ain't the real question. The real question is, will AI free you or own you? Because both futures are possible. AI could remove boring work, paperwork, scheduling, repetitive tasks, the stuff that drains your spirit and steals your evenings. It could help small businesses compete, help regular people start companies, help workers earn more with less stress. That's one road. But there's another road. A world where a few companies own the tools, own the data, own the systems, and rent intelligence back to everybody else. Where you don't own your assistant, you lease it. You don't build wealth, you pay subscriptions. You don't gain freedom, you become dependent. That's the fork in the road nobody talks about. Technology itself ain't good or evil. It reflects who controls it. So right now, while this thing is still being built, regular people need to wake up. Learn the tools. Use the tools. Demand fairness from the tools. Because if you ignore the biggest shift of your lifetime, someone else will use it to manage your lifetime. AI is coming either way. The question is, will it work for you or will you work for it?
