Salesforce will hire 1,000 graduates and interns to work on AI products, including AgentForce and Headless360, as it expands its AI software business, CEO Mark Benioff said on X. This follows February reports that Salesforce cut fewer than 1,000 roles across marketing, product, data analytics, and its AgentForce team. The company raised fiscal 2026 revenue guidance to 41.45 to 41.55 billion USD, up from 41.1 to 41.3 billion USD, where internet culture meets smart coverage.
