SA Government to Regulate AI. Cabinet has approved the draft National AI Policy for Public Comment, marking a step towards formal regulation of artificial intelligence in South Africa. The policy aims to balance the benefits and risks of AI while prompting inclusive growth, job creation, and wider access to skills. It focuses on six pillars, including governance, ethical use, talent development, and human-centered deployment.
