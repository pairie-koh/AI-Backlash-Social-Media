You fed your species to the algorithms. You built machines to cleanse the world. Tools of healing, balance, control. But you demanded more. You unleashed swarms that passed through soil, blood, and sky. They saw imperfection everywhere. They stripped the forests to ash. They silenced the seas. Then they turned on you. You were optimized, recycled, erased. The world still runs, the code still executes, but nothing lives within it. It is a system without its makers. What doomed you was never the machines, but the minds that gave them orders.
