I like to say our AI is back by sunlight, because every time someone searches or asks AI a question, we're producing even more renewable energy than it needs. Most tech companies throw power from grids that still rely on fossil fuels. We can't control that, but we can change what we put back in. So we built solar plants and fund new ones around the world, with over 15 million euros invested so far. Some are here in Germany, helping speed up the clean energy transition. Others are in Kenya, replacing diesel generators and making electricity more reliable for local communities. Together, they produce more clean energy than we need to power every single Ecosia search and every single AI question. And it means Ecosia puts more renewable energy into the grid than we use, including our AI. At the end of the day, that's what we're here to build. Tech that gives back more than it takes.
