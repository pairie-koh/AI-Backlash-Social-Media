I've memorized a Shakespearean sonnet because I can, so here is sonnet 18. Shall I compare thee to a summer's day? Thou art more lovely and more temperate. Rough winds do shake the darling buds of May, and summer's leash hath all too short a date. Sometime too hot the eye of heaven shines, and often is his gold complexion dimmed. And every fair from fair sometime declines by chance, or nature's changing course untrimmed. But thy eternal summer shall not fade Nor lose possession of that fair thou oust Nor shall death brag thou wanderest in his shade When in eternal lines to time thou grossed So long as men can breathe, their eyes can see So long lives this and gives life to thee Because why not?
