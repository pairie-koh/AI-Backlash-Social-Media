A new study reveals a shocking truth about Google search. In April, AI overviews cut clicks to top websites by 34.5%. Since then, the feature has rolled out worldwide, and many sites have watched their search traffic vanish. So the study was run again with new data from December. The new finding is stark. The average click-through rate for the top result has fallen by 58%. For every 100 clicks a top page used to get, Google now keeps 58. The AI answer appears right at the top so users don't need to click. The damage is worst for the first result, but every position on the page is losing clicks. This confirms a major shift. Search is becoming a zero-click experience. For creators and businesses relying on Google, the landscape has fundamentally changed.
