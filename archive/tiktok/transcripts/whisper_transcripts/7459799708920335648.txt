An internet scammer used AI Brad Pitt images to con more than €800,000 out of French interior designer. Anne handed over the money to a fake Brad Pitt who told her he needed the money for cancer treatment. The lovestruck woman was going through a rough patch with her millionaire husband and the scammer took advantage. Initially, she was contacted by an account that was claiming to be the Oscar winner's mother. The scammer claimed Angelina Jolie had frozen his accounts and needed money for hospital bills. Eventually, she transferred €830,000. It was only after seeing images of the real Pitt with his new girlfriend that she went to the police. The investigation was launched in the summer of 2024, with Anne being hospitalised with severe depression.
