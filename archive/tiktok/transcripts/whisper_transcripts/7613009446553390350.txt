What would happen if you had powers like Thor in ancient Greece? Day 1 You arrive in Athens with a hammer and the ability to control lightning. You summon one bolt from a clear sky in the middle of the Agora. No warning, no explanation, just lightning. The entire city stops. Athens has not been this quiet in 300 years, day 2. Every criminal in the city disappears overnight. The crime rate drops to zero. Even corrupt senators suddenly find their morals. Remarkable what lightning does to a person's character, day 3. Three armies marching toward Athens' turn around simultaneously. No battle, no negotiation, no treaty, they just go home. Nobody wants to be on the wrong side of the sky, day four. Greece sends its entire army after you 10,000 soldiers. Spear shields armor the whole thing, you raise the hammer. One lightning strike. The entire army sits down, not dead, just done. Nobody picks their spear back up, day five. Socrates walks up completely unbothered, says the lightning is a manifestation of your insecurity. You strike him with lightning, day six. Every king, every general, every philosopher in Greece sits in silence. No wars, no crime, no corruption, just complete stillness across the entire ancient world. You never declared yourself anything. You just stand there with a hammer. Nobody needs a second demonstration.
