What is the difference between the Cloud app and the Cloud desktop? The desktop has way more functionality. Even on the free plan, you can drag and drop your files in easily. You have features like custom styles that you can create on desktop and not on the app. On the pro plan, though, the desktop gives you access to Cloud Cowork, which is like your personal assistant, and Cloud Code, which is like a personal programmer.
