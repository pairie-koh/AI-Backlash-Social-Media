Artists need artists. My life is made better by the artists that I have in my life. We understand each other. We have respect for the effort that each of us is putting into our various work. We know how to encourage each other. I think my eccentricities maybe throw people off. But when I'm with artists, with you doing this, or at a show exhibiting art, or at a museum viewing art, I'm understood. And I need to be understood. Art doesn't have to be an isolated, lonely pursuit. Take the time now to look over some artists, perhaps here on Instagram or Facebook or wherever, encourage them. Say to them the things that you would like to be said to you.
