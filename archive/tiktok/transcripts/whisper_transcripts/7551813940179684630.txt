Google just broke their own golden goose and they had no choice for 25 years. Google's entire business model was built on a simple premise. You type in a search, you get a list of links and Google gets paid when you click on a sponsored result. That model generated over $200 billion in search revenue last year alone. But that user behavior that made Google so rich is changing fast. Our research at NP Digital tracking AI platform growth shows ChatGPT absolutely dominating the landscape. While Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, and DeepSeek have all gained some traction, ChatGPT's search interest dwarfs them all combined. Google noticed, and they realized they had two choices, fight it or own it. They chose to own it, even if it means cannibalizing their own cash cow. This represents a fundamental shift from a linked-based search to answer-based search. Instead of showing 10 blue links and hoping that a user clicks through, Google's AI provides direct answers and embeds advertising within those responses. So they're reshaping their entire business model to match how people actually want to search. The irony is that Google is disrupting themselves to prevent others from disrupting them. They're breaking their own golden goose because they see the writing on the wall.
