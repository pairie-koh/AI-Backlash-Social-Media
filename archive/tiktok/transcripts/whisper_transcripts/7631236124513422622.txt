As an ordinary person, is there anything I can do to promote the responsible use of AI? More people need to enter the conversation, and there should be a public opinion which is formed around AI. Right now, it's a bit elitist, I would say. The people who are in the field of AI, they're forming one side, and the people who know nothing about AI are forming the other side. For us to create, develop, and evolve AI in a responsible way, we need to understand it. So the best thing you can do is to start using AI today to understand it very well. Keep in mind the next 15 years, they'll be all about AI. Some jobs will be replaced, some jobs will be augmented. 95% certain your job will be changed in some way because of AI, and you should learn to use it so you can participate in the conversation. If people are educated, have information about the capabilities, they will be able to make rational and reasonable arguments. And we as a society will be able to progress AI in an ethical and responsible way. If we don't know anything about it, we wouldn't.
