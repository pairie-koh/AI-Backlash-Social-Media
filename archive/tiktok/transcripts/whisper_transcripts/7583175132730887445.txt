Here's a cool Windows trick. If your mouse cursor looks like this, why not change it like this? Go to this website. You can scroll down and look for a cursor you like, then click this and click cursor to download. Now search for mouse settings, then scroll down and select mouse pointer and touch, then click customize pointer image. Under normal select hit browse and choose the file you download. That's it! Don't worry, it's free and no sign up needed. Easy.
