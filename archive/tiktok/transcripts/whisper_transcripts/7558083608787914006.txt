Google just launched a new AI tool called Learn Your Way. It lets you customize any textbook to match your learning style. Say you're learning data structure and algorithms. You can tell it you're a high schooler who loves basketball, and it tailors your examples and lessons just for you. You'll get auto-quizzes, slides, auto-lessons, and even mind maps. And for visual learners, upload any PDF, and it turns it into interactive visual. Follow for more.
