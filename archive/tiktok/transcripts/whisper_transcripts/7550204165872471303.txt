If you use character AI, don't scroll. This is for you. I used to have an addiction to character AI. I wish I was kidding. Every day, for two hours minimum, I'd talk to a chatbot. Usually it was around four. I stopped hanging out with my friends. I didn't care about school to the point where my grades fell, and I treated everyone around me like shit. And let me tell you, this coping mechanism ruined my life. During this time, I even found a knack for writing my own bots, which I would post on a site called Janitor AI. My bots began getting popular, and I created a Google form so that people could request scenarios with certain characters. However, at one point, somebody had sent me something in a request that changed me and my bad habits. They asked me, Do you have an AO3 account? This made me realize that my work and effort was put into something so harmful to both to me and the environment, when instead, I could have been doing this healthily. I, as embarrassing as it sounds, began reading and writing fanfiction. Fanfiction is genuinely almighty. There's so many variations of A.O.'s and your favorite characters that, unlike generative A.I., contain actual soul. And hey, if you're like me and you've scavenged Tumblr and Wattpad and AO3 for the best Abby Anderson Spider-Man AU, have no fear. You can write your own. But honk, I can't write. No matter. Fanfiction isn't about being good at writing. It's about being creative and doing what you love. You don't have to post it either. You can keep it to yourself if you want. If anything, the fan fiction community is one of the most welcoming ones as of present day. Moral of the story, don't use AI as an unhealthy coping mechanism when you can spread creativity elsewhere. Honk out.
