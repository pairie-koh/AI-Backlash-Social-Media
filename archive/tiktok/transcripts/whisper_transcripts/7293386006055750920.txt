If you're an artist and you want to protect your artwork from being stolen and scraped by AI models online, there's not a tool that you can use to do that. It's called Glaze. Glaze is developed by a team of scientists at the University of Chicago in collaboration with human artists. It works like this. You're an artist, you upload a painting onto Glaze's software, and the program will shift around the painting's elements on a pixel level to confuse AI art generators. Glaze describes this process as adding something like a cloak to an artwork, and the result of this cloaking is that it disrupts an AI model's scraping process when it's scanning for artwork on the internet. Don't get too excited yet though. Using glaze doesn't mean that your artwork is now 100% bulletproof against AI. Glaze's chief scientist Ben Zhao tells Business Insider that art that has been glazed can still be fed into AI models but it just won't be 100% accurate.
