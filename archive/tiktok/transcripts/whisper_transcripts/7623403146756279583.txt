Why is AI bad for the environment? Let's break it down. Most AI apps like ChatGPT run on massive supercomputers that use up a lot of electricity. Just training ChatGPT once uses enough power to power every single home in Washington DC for over a month. One robot, one training session, that much power. And the newer versions that they're putting out in their training, they can use up to 10 times that amount of power just for one training session. And it doesn't Stop there. Every time someone uses AI to write, draw or answer a question, those computers fire up again, burning through even more power. A single AI question uses 5 to 10 times more power than a single Google search. Now multiply that by millions of people using that multiple times a day, you're looking at massive, unnecessary pollution. And then there's the matter of water.
