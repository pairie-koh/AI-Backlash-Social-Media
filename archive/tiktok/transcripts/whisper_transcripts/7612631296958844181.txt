Creativity is over. This is the biggest lie that they will tell you. The truth is convoluted. But the antidote is simple. In this world now, anyone can create. Anyone with a breath, with an imagination, anyone willing to spend countless hours chasing a vision in their head, anybody can create. But not anybody can be creative. Not everyone will think how you think, not everyone will dream what you dream, fear what you fear, or see what you see, because no algorithm lived your life. It doesn't carry your memories. It doesn't share your taste or feel your scars and it never will. So dig deep. Find what's inside because what you create is no longer a reflection of the gear that you have or the places that you go. It is simply a reflection of you.
