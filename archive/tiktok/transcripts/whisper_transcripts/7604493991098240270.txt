The AI bubble is getting closer to popping, but not for the reasons you're expecting. While many are quick to point out the big old circular investment that each AI company is getting into, the unfortunate truth is that a lot of the big money being thrown around is real. NVIDIA and others are making real cash that they have to reinvest. So they chuck it into financial dumpster fires like OpenAI. This author, however, seems to think that the real breaks will come down to two major policy issues, tariffs and immigration. The main idea is that tariffs are going to make every portion of the AI data centers too expensive to build. From steel and aluminum to the transformers and circuit breakers or even the batteries, there's a cost bomb that's incoming for these buildouts that's going to hurt more than the RAM shortage. The immigration angle is that there's not going to be enough. Making it harder to get H-1B visas and getting legal workers to question their safety in the country is going to make everything about AI deployment harder. Construction, expertise in chip production, and more specialty skills will be hard to come by. Even if all of that is domesticated, it's going to take so long to train people to do it that the bubble may pop because the USA just actually can't keep up.
