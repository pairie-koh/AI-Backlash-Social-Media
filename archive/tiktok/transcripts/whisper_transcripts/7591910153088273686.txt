Why is AI in the simplest explanation ever? AI stands for artificial intelligence. That sounds complicated, but it's not. AI is just a computer that learns from examples. If you show it lots of pictures of cats, it starts to recognize what a cat looks like. If you give it lots of words and sentences, it learns how people talk and write. So instead of following fixed rules, AI learns patterns and makes smart guesses, kind of like how humans learn just faster and with more data. Think of AI like a super fast student. The more it studies, the better it gets. That's AI. No magic. No hype. Just learning from data. Follow for more simple, real explanations of AI.
