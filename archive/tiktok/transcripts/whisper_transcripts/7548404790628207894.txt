AI companies are accused of stealing songs from your favourite artists, the Beatles, Elton John, Madonna, even Beyonce, to train their models. Billboard is calling it the largest IP theft in human history. The International Confederation of Music Publishers, or ICMP, says tech giants like Google, Microsoft, Meta, plus AI music apps like Suno and Udio, have allegedly scraped copyrighted catalogs without permission. The ICMP even claims OpenAI's chatbot admitted its jukebox app was trained on legendary artists without paying them a cent. This isn't just a theory. Velvet Sundown, a band with over a million streams on Spotify, was entirely AI generated. Music, imagery, backstory, raising debates about transparency and ethics in music.
