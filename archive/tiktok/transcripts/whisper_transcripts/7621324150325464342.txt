Think your private photos are actually private? Think again. You see, platforms like GeoSpy AI can now take a single photo and pinpoint exactly where it was taken anywhere on Earth. No GPS data, no tags, no location services. How? By analysing tiny visual clues like building shapes, road patterns or unique street markings. The AI compares your image to a massive global satellite database and finds the match in seconds. See, investigators, journalists and AI analysts can verify events, debunk misinformation and track conflicts with extreme precision, which they are already doing. But in the wrong hands, this turns every photo you post into a location beacon, which can lead to surveillance, stalking with zero consent and zero awareness. The question is, how do we regulate it and how do we keep our privacy?
