AI can steal your entire artistic identity and you wouldn't even know it. The Train Act would change that. If passed, the Train Act would empower creators with tools to prevent the use of their copyrighted work. Similar to the laws around internet piracy, the Train Act would give creators the ability to find out if their work is being used to train AI systems without consent. That's the way it should be.
