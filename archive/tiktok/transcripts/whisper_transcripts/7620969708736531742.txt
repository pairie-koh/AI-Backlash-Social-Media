People think that one day they'll check their phone and see AI bubble has popped and then all of the AI apps will disappear, the RAM prices will drop back down to normal, but that's not how it works. It's not the AI models or the GPUs or the data centers that will pop and disappear. It's the investment. Eventually investors will say this AI thing isn't actually making that much money, so let's not give them more investment money for now. And for all of the random AI products and all of the random AI apps that are surviving purely off of that investment money, it's going to be bad news. But for the big established AI companies like Google and Anthropic, the ones that can actually take a hit and survive, they're still going to be doing AI. Their financial sheet is going to look different, but the models will still exist. The data centers will keep running. We've seen bubbles before. The internet was a bubble, but when that bubble pops, the technology doesn't disappear. The companies just get separated between the ones that can survive on their own and the ones that can.
