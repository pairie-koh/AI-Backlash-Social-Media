So in short form, non-chemical, also behavioral addiction, is when a person becomes compulsively dependent on an activity or behavior because it strongly activates the brain's reward system. Chemical, also substance addiction, is when a person becomes physically and psychologically dependent on a drug or substance that alters brain chemistry. Both of these are valid types of addictions and can cause withdrawal symptoms. People get addicted to the dopamine spike.
