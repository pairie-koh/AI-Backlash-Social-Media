Wait, AI is currently almost destroying our environment. Yep, you've heard right. Training AI models requires a massive amount of electricity. A typical AI-focused data center consumes as much electricity as 100,000 households. With larger, future centers projected to consume up to 20 times that amount, which is already pretty crazy. One chat GPT search requires like 10 times the electricity a simple Google One needs, reported by the International Energy Agency. Now maybe you've heard of AI taking water? Maybe you've even thought it wasn't true, but oh it is. AI servers are large consumers of water, which is putting some countries in danger. On Earth, AI-related infrastructure may soon consume six times more water than Denmark, a country of six million. That is a huge problem since a quarter of humanity already lacks access to clean water. Also, the microchips that power AI need rare Earth elements, which are often mined in environmentally destructive ways.
