These ideas may sound weird now, but they'll all be normal in 10 to 20 years. The smart toilet will give us a detailed breakdown of our health every time that we pee. Dating apps will use AI so everyone finds the perfect person for them without swiping for hours. AI will also create chatbots of your passed away family members based on data from their actual life. Delivery companies will have robots cooking your food in an oven while on the way to your house so that it'll be ready on arrival. Future machines will be able to 3D print the exact makeup pattern that's requested by the user. Live courtside VR experiences will be a common way to watch sports without paying the extreme prices to actually be in those seats. There will be people living on the moon. Fireworks dropped from the sky by drones will dramatically change the way we experience them. Drones will also be used to fight fires by directing loud sounds to the trees below them. Pressure waves from the sounds can disrupt the air surrounding a fire, cutting off its oxygen supply, making the fire die out. Drones in general are just gonna dominate the skies and it's gonna be normal to see them everywhere.
