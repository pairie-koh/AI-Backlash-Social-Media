The independent analyst Carl Wehner published a comprehensive vendor positioning map for enterprise agentic AI plotting every major AI vendor. So this includes products like or vendors such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, AWS, SAP, Meta, etc. And he plots them across two dimensions, how much enterprises should trust the vendor's AI and how much lock-in they accept.
