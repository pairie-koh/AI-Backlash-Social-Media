AI video looks incredible right now, but watch closely and you'll see the cracks. I've been going deep on world models this week, and here's why they matter. A person walks, a car moves, a room looks cinematic, and then suddenly the model gets confused. Things disappear, faces morph, objects shift when the camera looks away. That's where object permanence comes in. basically knowing something still exists when you can't see it. And that's the problem. AI can make video, but it doesn't always understand the world behind the video. And that's what world models are trying to fix, helping AI understand what's in a scene, how things move, and what should happen next. So the future of AI video isn't just better visuals. The real leap is from making video to understanding video.
