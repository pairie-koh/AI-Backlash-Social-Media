This might look like a Dementor from Harry Potter, but it's actually a silk-covered mannequin sent to the edge of space. It's part of Mission Taroni, an art and science project created by the Dorothy Project and artistic director of Taroni, Maximilian Kanepa. According to the team, the goal is to explore our relationship with the planet, inspired by the overview effect astronauts feel looking back at Earth. The process wasn't a simple one. To make it, the team made an ultra-lightweight human cast, designed the custom silk covering, and mounted it to a carbon fiber rig. They then launched it from the Canadian countryside by balloon into the stratosphere, about 33 kilometers above the surface of Earth. Up there, the sculpture was exposed to sunlight, gravity, and wind. The result? An eerie floating silk figure above the planet. In the age of AI, it was important to the creators that every step of the process was documented, and very little of the final product was edited. While reactions online have been mixed, the creators say they're grateful for the opportunity to have made something never seen before.
