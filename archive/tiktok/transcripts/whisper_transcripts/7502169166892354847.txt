Here are three scary AI scams that look real. Number one is deepfake video calls. And how it works is scammers use AI-generated deepfake videos, for example, of a company executive, to trick employees into transferring millions of dollars. This actually happens. Number two, AI romance scams with fake celebrities. So basically scammers make AI-generated replicas of celebrities like Brad Pitt using deepfake videos and images. and then they DM victims on social media to build a romantic relationship, sending personalized messages and videos. For example, a French woman lost 850,000 euros after falling for a scammer who is posing as Brad Pitt. Number three is AI voice cloning in fake family emergencies. How this works is scammers clone a loved one's voice using AI tools trained on short social media clips or past voicemails. then they call other family members pretending to be that family member in urgent need of money. For example, they were just arrested or in an accident. To counteract this, make sure your family has an emergency keyword that you talk about in person. You don't share it online.
