Google's AI once called a black man a gorilla, and I am not joking. So in 2015, Google Photos mislabored a black man and his friend as gorillas. So why did this happen? Because AI couldn't recognize darker skin tones because the training data wasn't good enough. So this so-called bug showed us how badly things can go wrong when AI isn't trained on the right data. So Google obviously apologized, but their fix was just to block certain words like gorillas and not to actually solve the recognition problem. So what lesson can we learn from this? We learn that when the training data isn't good enough, AI can really fail. And sometimes those failures can be very catastrophic, like in facial recognition software.
