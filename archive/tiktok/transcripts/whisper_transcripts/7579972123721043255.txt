Right now, several lawsuits are being filed against Sunil and Udiol for using copyrighted music to train their models without permission. What that means to me is artists and indie labels are saying, you escaped our catalog and built your AI systems off our copyrighted songs. So while you as a user might be safe under Sunil's terms, the bigger legal picture is it's still being worked out. But as creators, we should care because it impacts how our music is used and valued. Music, the art, it's got to have a value. It's got to have a meaning. Training AI on copyrighted works without permission or compensation or credit is not innovation. They consider that theft. And honestly, I agree with them 100%. Figure this stuff out so we can do it right and make sure that the music is still valued.
