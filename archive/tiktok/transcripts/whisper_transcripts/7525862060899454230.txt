My main mission now is to warn people how dangerous AI could be. Did you know that when you became the godfather of AI? No, not really. I was quite slow to understand some of the risks. Some of the risks were always very obvious, like people would use AI to make autonomous lethal weapons. That is, things would go around deciding by themselves who to kill. Other risks, like the idea that they would one day get smarter than us and maybe we'd become irrelevant, I was slow to recognise that. Other people recognised it 20 years ago. I only recognised it a few years ago, that that was a real risk that might be coming quite soon.
