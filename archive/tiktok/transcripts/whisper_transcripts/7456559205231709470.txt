Teachers are going crazy after this Harvard student just revealed a brand new AI that thousands of students are using to pass their finals. Oh no, there's a scary new AI tool out there all my students are going to use to cheat on my assignments. What am I going to do? Look, I got news for any students out there who thinks AI is cheating. Your teachers don't really care if you use AI. We care whether you learn the material we're teaching. The problem with AI isn't that it's cheating because it does the work for you or some garbage like that. The problem with AI is students think it's doing the work for them, and it's not. Use AI all you want. Just use it in a way that demonstrates you've learned the material. For example, if AI could help you write an amazing essay in less time, that is wonderful. I don't need you to spend 20 hours writing an amazing essay if you could have done it in three. The thing is, in order to write that amazing essay, you still have to learn what an amazing essay should look like. And AI can't do that for you. You have to have learned the material. Yes, AI is a powerful tool that can help you accomplish amazing things, but like any tool, it doesn't do the work for you, it only helps you do the work better and more efficiently. Never forget that the hard part is acquiring the knowledge to know what great work looks like, and that's the part your teachers are trying to teach.
