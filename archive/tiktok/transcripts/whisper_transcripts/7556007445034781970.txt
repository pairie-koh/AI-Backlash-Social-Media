The CEO of OpenAI, an unofficial high priest of the AI cult, just randomly admitted that, yeah, this might all be a bubble. He told reporters, He even compared it to the dot-com bubble, that fun time when the Nasdaq lost almost 80% of its value because it turned out most internet companies were just PowerPoints with mascots. And honestly, he might be right. The US stock market has almost doubled in five years. Mostly because 10 giant companies promised AI would change literally everything. Altman's raised billions and is already talking about spending trillions on data centers. Which is a totally normal thing to say if you're planning to buy a small moon. So here's the real question. If this is a bubble, what did we actually buy? This is where things get weird. Most of the headlines you see about the AI boom aren't about how much value AI has created. They're just about how much the price of a few companies' stocks have gone up, which is kind of like judging the quality of a restaurant by how expensive their forks are.
