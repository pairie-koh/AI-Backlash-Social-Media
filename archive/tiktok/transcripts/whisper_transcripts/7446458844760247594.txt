Another landmark decision dealing with AI was decided in 2023 when a group of artists brought a class action suit against AI developers, alleging that these tech companies were improperly storing and using various pieces of the artist's visual artworks to train their AI models to create content without getting the artist's permission. The case is named Anderson versus Stability AI Limited. Now, there, a bunch of artists sued AI developers like Stability AI and Midjourney after claiming these companies use artist creations without permission, infringing on their intellectual property rights under the U.S. Copyright Act and the Lanham Act. Now, the judge in this lawmaking decision ruled in favor of the artists and found that these companies' AI models were built on copyrighted work stolen from the artists designed to exploit them, making the plaintiff's copyright infringement claims viable. This decision emphasizes that intellectual property rights remain with the original creators, not the AI systems, and marks a significant victory for artists, setting a precedent that AI companies must secure licenses to use copyrighted content for training.
