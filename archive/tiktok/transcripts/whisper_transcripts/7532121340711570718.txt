Google search is eating itself. Google's own AI just broke its most profitable product, search. Their AI overviews feature started giving answers directly at the top of the page. The problem? No one clicked the links anymore. Website clicks dropped by nearly 50%. A huge oops when your empire is built on those blue links. So now they're scrambling to fix it with a new feature called web guide, which just uses AI to organize the links for you. It's a massive company trying to fix a self-inflicted wound, proving even Google doesn't know how this all ends.
