Someone threw a Molotov near Sam Altman's house this week. And honestly, I'm not surprised anymore. People are furious. Marches in London. Unions banding together. Nearly 80,000 tech jobs gone last quarter and everyone's blaming AI. What's bugging me is that according to analysts that have looked at it, well, cost cutting, only about 30,000 of those cuts are really down to AI. The rest? CEOs who wanted to make those calls anyway and finally found a story shareholders would buy. So the anger is real. The job losses are real. But a lot of it is pointed at the machine when it should be pointed at the people using the machine as cover. So when you're aiming your Molotov, metaphorically, please aim carefully. The tools aren't going anywhere. Pandora's box is wide open. and that ship has sailed. So who's actually making the decision? Your job is gone and are we aiming at the right target?
