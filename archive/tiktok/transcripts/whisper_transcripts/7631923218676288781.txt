The real power of AI isn't generating finished output. It's expanding the number of ideas you can explore. Most marketers are using AI like a shortcut to the final answer. But the smartest teams, they're using it earlier in the process. Instead of asking AI to write the final ad, use it to explore different angles, new concepts, cultural references, different ways to frame the idea. That's the difference between using AI for answers and using AI for exploration. The best marketers don't create AI as a writer. They use it as an idea accelerator.
