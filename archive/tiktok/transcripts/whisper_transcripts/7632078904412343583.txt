Number one, all of this is overblown. We'll adapt. Yes, we've adapted to electricity, the industrial revolution, the Internet. But we've done so on the back of generations that did not have the training nor the time to adapt. And in the case of this revolution, time is of the essence. You may think you have time because agents are just emerging. And it's a fact. Our research shows that today, only 13% of companies have embedded agents in their workflows. But tech moves exponentially. Humans, they crawl linearly. If you don't prepare now, you'll struggle to keep up. Now, myth number two, soft skills are our sweet spot. Yes, it's lovely to believe that empathy, creativity are uniquely ours. But evidence says otherwise. More and more, humans like to interact with AI because they feel it's more empathic. And why not? I mean, AI doesn't get tired, doesn't get cranky, doesn't judge you. So this mode we thought was ours, it's shrinking. And we need to stop asking what AI can't do and focus on answering.
