Meta is now tracking employee activity and keystrokes to train its internal AI. Basically trying to train a model to replace all of the employees' jobs. The new employee tracking tool called MCI allows Meta to observe and collect data on all employee laptops. They also announced that they are cutting 10% of their workforce or 8,000 jobs starting May 20th. Employee tracking tools have always been a thing and the smarter people will know that there's tons of automations that allow you to trick these things. However, now it's not just to make sure that you're working, it's also to gather data on you so that they can replace you. It's really hard to say where all of this AI stuff is going to go, because if the end goal is to replace every corporate worker, who's going to have enough money to buy anything?
