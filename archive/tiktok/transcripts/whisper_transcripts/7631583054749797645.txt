Design is not the bottleneck anymore, slow decisions are. Claude Design just came out and it can give you multiple serious directions before most teams even finish one kickoff. The old way is slow. One rough idea turns into one safe draft and now everybody is reacting to the same weak starting point. Claude Design is better because it lets you actually explore the page faster. You can test different hero concepts, proof structures, CTA positions, section order on your website, and voice directions before the team even gets attached to one version. And that matters because better pages usually do not come from one genius idea. They come from seeing better options earlier. And once that happens, the creative conversation gets sharper. Better options early usually beat perfect opinions late.
