Leah from Itzee has found herself at the center of the latest K-pop scandal. It all started when Leah honestly admitted to fans that she frequently uses chat GPT, and not just for looking up information, but for communicating with her audience. The singer explained that she doesn't always have a deep understanding of certain topics and is afraid of giving fans the wrong info. According to her, even Instagram posts or preparations for in-person meetings go through the AI filter. I want to give you the right information, so I run my thoughts through chat GPT, she shared. Fans of the group admit they feel cheated. They had asked the members to stop using AI, but it seems their requests were ignored. People spend huge amounts of money on albums and tickets just to feel a connection with the artist, and Leah's admission essentially devalues that contact. If a social media post is written by a machine, where is the artist in it? Adding fuel to the fire is the fact that a war against open AI is currently raging in Korea. Major broadcasters are suing the company over the theft of content used to train its AI systems.
