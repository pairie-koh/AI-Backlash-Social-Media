As a football agent I see a lot of confusion. My job is not to make players rich. My job is to make players seen, heard, protected, well represented, have better opportunities and have a proper team of professionals and support them. The money? Well the money side of things will take care of itself once the player feels the confidence of performing at his peak, at his best on the pitch. It's a reflection of all these things that we do for the player and how we get the player to focus and think only about football. Have a great week guys.
