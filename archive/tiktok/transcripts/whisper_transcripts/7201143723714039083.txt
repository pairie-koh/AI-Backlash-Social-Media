Will AI like ChatGPT take away your job? Business Insider has published a list of jobs that they think are most likely to be replaced by AI. Let's look at which ones made the list. First of all, tech jobs. Coders, computer programmers, software engineers, data analysts. Then, media jobs. Advertising, content creation, technical writing, journalism. Legal industry jobs. Paralegals, legal assistants. Market research analysts. Teachers. Finance jobs. Financial analysts. Personal financial advisors. Traders. graphic designers, accountants, customer service agents. One thing that most of those jobs have in common is manual repetitive tasks. All right, maybe they're not manual, but they are repetitive and NAI can be taught to do them. So my verdict is that it will replace certain people who are doing those repetitive tasks, who are only doing them. So how do you survive an era of AI and chat GPT? Focus on building skills that an AI cannot replace. And a lot of the times they involve critical thinking, problem solving, social skills, creativity, perception and manipulation. And finally, learn how to use AI in order to complete your job more effectively. Those skills will set you apart and allow you to compete with machines. Oh my God, what are we even doing?
