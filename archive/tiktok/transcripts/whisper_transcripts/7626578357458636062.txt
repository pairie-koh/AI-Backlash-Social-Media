I've been getting sent so many AI epoxy videos lately. All of them have millions of views, of course. Meanwhile, I feel like I'm struggling for anybody to see my artwork. So I figured I'd do a series showing you what really goes into these type of projects that AI is not showing you. This project started with the search for the perfect piece of wood. It took us weeks to find, and unfortunately, it did not show up how we intended. It was anywhere from like a quarter inch to two inches deep, which is nowhere near what I need for full-size flowers. The client and I were both pretty devastated but fortunately we were able to find another slab that was beautiful and I was able to start prepping the wood I spent many hours sanding and cleaning up the pieces before laying out a rough draft in my driveway to show my client what it might look like if we went with this layout Fortunately, she loved it and so it was on to custom building a mold that gave the shape and aesthetic that we were looking for At this point, we're ready to start the floral design process and that's where we'll stop for today there's so much more work that goes into this so make sure you follow along for the rest of the series
