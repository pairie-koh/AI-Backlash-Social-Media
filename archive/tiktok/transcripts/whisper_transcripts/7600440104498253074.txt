Amazon is slashing about 16,000 jobs in the latest round of layoffs at the tech giant. Amazon says it plans to use AI to replace corporate workers. The fresh job cuts are part of a broader goal of trying about 30,000 corporate roles with layoffs expected to affect workers in Amazon Web Services, retail, prime video and human resources. The company, you may recall slashed about 14,000 jobs back in October. A senior vice president says Amazon has been reducing layers and removing bureaucracy.
