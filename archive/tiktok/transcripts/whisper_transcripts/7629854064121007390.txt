In this game, you and your friends have to smuggle drugs across a bunch of different countries. You work for the cartel as a pilot, transporting goods for their criminal empire, and you have to find different ways to sneak goods onto your plane. After you sell the goods, you can use the money to buy new planes and upgrades so you can smuggle even more drugs. Just make sure to learn how to properly fly the plane so you don't crash and lose everything. The cartel won't be very happy if you lose all their product.
