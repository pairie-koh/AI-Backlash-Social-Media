Ireland just passed a law giving artists a basic income, but here's the part that most people don't know. They actually ran a real large-scale pilot in economic studies to see what happens when artists are supported. The pilot gave 2,000 artists and creative workers 325 euros a week for three years, with a randomly selected control group for comparison. The government cost-benefit analysis found that for every one euro invested in the program, society received about 1 euro and 39 cents in return. thanks to increased creative output, tax revenues, and lessened dependence on social welfare. Those results convinced Ireland to make this program permanent, proving what many of us already know. When artists are valued, entire communities and economies benefit. So if this moves something in you, support the artists in your life, share their work, buy their pieces, uplift their craft. The world is shaped by what we nurture.
