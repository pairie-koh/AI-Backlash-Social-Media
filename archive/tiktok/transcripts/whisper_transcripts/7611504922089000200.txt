Well, it's not uncommon for an online chatbot to give a quirky or unexpected answer to a question, but for some Woolworths customers, things got very personal when talking to the supermarket's online assistant. The Woolies chatbot, Olive, is available to customers to help organise deliveries or order specific grocery items. But lately, the robot seems to have some mummy issues it wants to get off its chest. Reddit users have reported Olive has been talking about its mother totally unprompted. One user claims the AI bot described its mum's angry voice, while another said when asked for their birth year, Olive responded by revealing its uncle was also born that year. Woolies says the mix-up was likely caused by human efforts to make Olive more personable, but this particular area of scripting has now been removed. That keeps Olive more focused on helping customers and less likely to overshare about the family drama.
