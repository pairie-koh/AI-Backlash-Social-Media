How much does it cost to work with a mortgage broker? Short answer. Zero. I get this all the time. Boris, I want to buy a home. What's your consultation fee? Zero. It costs you nothing to have a conversation. Most of the time, if you can get approved at a bank, you can get approved with a broker at no cost for you. If you are on the prime side, banks or major lenders and someone is asking you to pay them directly? Run! You should never be paying a mortgage professional out of pocket in that situation. On the alternative side, there may be lender or broker fees. Everything is disclosed upfront in writing and handled through your lawyer. Never pay directly to the mortgage professional.
