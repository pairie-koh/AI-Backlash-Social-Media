My love is as a fever longing still for that which longer nurses the disease, feeding on that which does preserve the ill, the uncertain sickly appetite to please. My reason, the physician to my love, angry that his prescriptions are not kept, has left me, and I desperate now approve. Desire is death, which physic did accept. past cure I am now reason is past care and frantic mad with evermore unrest my thoughts and my discourse as madmans are at random from the truth vainly expressed for I have sworn thee fair and thought thee bright who art as black as hell as dark as night
