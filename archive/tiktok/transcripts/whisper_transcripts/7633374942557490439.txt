Whenever you're using two, three, four monitors, sometimes it can be hectic to locate where your cursor is. And so here is a hack on how to solve that problem. Come here, right click, go to settings. Once you've gone to settings, select Bluetooth and devices. Then select mouse. Under mouse, you will come and choose mouse pointer and touch. Then you will come here and choose the mouse indicator. Show a circle around the mouse when I press control then toggle that on. That helps you locate your cursor anytime you can't find it. By clicking on control it will always show a circle and you can easily identify the location of your cursor.
