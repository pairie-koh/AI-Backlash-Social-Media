Is AI making us dumb then? So when you use AI, you're engaging in less deep processing and more in cognitive offloading. Similar effects have been seen when GPS and search engines have been introduced. Alongside, you also observe lower attention span and of course, lower engagement. But that doesn't mean it's making you dumber. It's just your brain adapting, shifting from memorizing to focusing on how to access information. The main issue appears when you stop challenging what AI gives you. That could make you dumber.
