Women and girls are being targeted by AI. How? Deepfake pornography. Deepfake AI is used to turn people into other people, replacing faces and bodies to make them appear as someone else, or in this case, the same person, just naked. The AI technology is being used to turn ordinary photos of ordinary people into pornography, mainly women and girls. The problem was highlighted when a number of young girls in Spain said they received nude images of themselves that they hadn't taken. And when I say young, we're talking like 12 years old. Police say the alleged male perpetrators knew most of the victims and used photos taken from the girls' social media profiles. The scary reality is that pretty much anyone can be a potential victim of the epidemic. It's raised alarm bells internationally with calls to make this type of content less accessible, with specific blame cast toward internet providers such as Google and Microsoft. People believe they're not doing enough to stop people from accessing these crime generating sites. At the moment, the EU is debating new criminal laws, targeting online abuse, including deep fake pornography.
