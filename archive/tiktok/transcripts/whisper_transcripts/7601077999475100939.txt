After accidentally having 12 kids, this couple was in for a challenge. The dad bought six bunk beds, but the room was instantly packed to the brim. No space to breathe, let alone play. The kids had no choice but to sleep all day. So the wife called in every friend and relative for an emergency room overhaul. And together they came up with a genius plan. First, they used galvanized square steel to map out positions for 12 beds. With a few bucks borrowed from auntie, they got expansion screws to firmly fix everything to the walls, ensuring nothing wobbled. Force columns supported the bottom bunks, while the top ones were secured with steel frames. Covered in solid wood panels, these eco-friendly beds are built to last 10,000 years. Each got a soft mattress for a comfy night's sleep, with wooden guardrails to prevent accidental tumbles. Soft wall padding added extra coziness. For extra charm, they built wooden boxes with hidden light strips to stash all the kids' favorite books and literary classics. A small ceiling fan above kept the air circulating, and the kids could move freely without bumping into each other. Sleeping now felt like a daily ritual, special and organized. The left side of the room was dedicated to the girls, with two large roller drawers divided into six compartments. One side for their underwear, the other for less worn clothes. A movable roller hanger in the middle kept their everyday dresses and skirts easy to access. On the right side, the boys got the same setup. Two large drawers for underwear and neatly folded clothes, with a roller hanger for their frequently worn outfits. To top it off, they borrowed a cat cage from Auntie and got a big cat. The kids play with it every day, and apparently they're getting stronger from the exercise. Opposite the beds, a row of wall cabinets neatly stores books with closed doors to keep everything tidy. Below, desks were installed for study time, each equipped with space for school supplies and small items. The middle of the room was left open as a super spacious activity area. This new setup gives each of the 12 kids their own cozy sleeping area, plenty of storage, and a shared study and play space.
