What happens when the student becomes smarter than the teacher? That's exactly what we're facing. But this time, the student is artificial intelligence. Right now, AI can already process data at speeds the human brain can't even imagine. It reads millions of pages in seconds. It sees patterns we'll never see. It predicts, analyzes, adapts. all without fatigue, without emotions clouding its judgment. But what happens when it starts teaching itself, when AI begins improving AI? This is called recursive self-improvement, and it's not science fiction anymore. The moment AI becomes better at building AI, we enter what experts call the singularity, a point where growth becomes uncontrollable, intelligence rising exponentially beyond human understanding. Some say it will solve every problem on Earth. Others fear it may no longer need us. Because for the first time ever, we become the second most intelligent species on the planet.
