If AI replaces millions of jobs, what happens to your income? It's a question more people are starting to ask as artificial intelligence begins to reshape work. And Elon Musk says he has an answer. He calls it universal high income. As AI disrupts jobs and reshapes entire industries, traditional sources of income could become less reliable. According to Musk, the solution would be a future where people receive direct government payments, funded by the economic expansion driven by AI and robotics. This idea isn't entirely new. It builds on an existing concept known as universal basic income, which is based on regular cash payments given to people regardless of whether they work. Supporters say it could reduce poverty, expand personal freedom, and help people adapt to a rapidly changing economy. But critics argue it would be extremely costly, difficult to implement, and could weaken incentives to work in the first place. Meanwhile, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman is proposing a different approach, giving people a stake in AI-driven growth through a public wealth fund. But as the debate continues, concerns are also growing over whether the wealth created by AI will be shared broadly or concentrated in the hands of a few.
