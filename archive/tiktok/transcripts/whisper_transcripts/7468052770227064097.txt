How f***ing useful is AI? If you're tired of Google's AI-powered search results leading you astray with poor information from bad sources, here is some good news. It turns out that if you include any expletives in your search query, Google will not return an AI overview, as they are called, at the top of the results page. For instance, if you search, How large is the student body of Yale University? The search results page will return a large AI-generated blurb above the blue links. If you instead search, How large is the f***ing student body at Yale University? instead get a standard list of blue link results sans AI summary. Google's search results have never been perfect, of course, there is still a lot of poor information across the web, but AI summaries present users with a prominent blurb at the top of their search that looks authoritative when it just risks compounding the misinformation problem with more erroneous slop. We imagine Google will close the expletive loophole eventually, but in the meantime, if you are sick of AI, you now know an easy way to avoid it. Just tell Google to give you the f*** links.
