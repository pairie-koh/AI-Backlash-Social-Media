Create floor plans in seconds using AI technology. Maquette AI is a website where you can use AI to generate floor plans. You can add the number of bedrooms, bathrooms, and garages, as well as optional closets, balconies, and walk-ins. Then the AI takes care of the rest and generates four iterations that try to be the most accurate to the settings that you put in. This tool gives you the ability to generate multiple floor plans in under 30 seconds and gives you a bunch of new ideas that you can test out. Now everything isn't perfect. As you can see in this, two garages are in awkward spots and there's a bedroom in between, which makes absolutely no sense. In addition, you can also render floor plans.
