The real reason to learn AI if you're an entrepreneur is because AI now enables one person to do what you previously needed a huge team and tons of resources for. When I think about AI and the leverage I get from it and the time it saves me, I think of a very simple formula. AI leverage equals my skill times my clarity. The more skill I have in the domain, for example, coding, the further I get using AI tools, the more mileage I get, and the more clarity I have when I'm communicating back and forth with AI, the more success I will have in using AI to complete the task successfully.
