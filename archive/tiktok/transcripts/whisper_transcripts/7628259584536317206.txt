If AI creates the song, who owns it? Hi everyone, I am Al Lister, founder of Artis Music, and this changes everything for creators. The U.S. Supreme Court just sent a message. Purely AI-generated art is not protected by copyright, because under U.S. law, copyright begins with human authorship. But there's a gray zone. If a human adds real creativity, direction, editing, composition, some protection may apply. So if AI creates everything, someone else may copy it, reuse it, monetize it. For creators, this means using AI as a tool is safer than using AI as the creator. The law is saying something simple. Technology can assist creation, but humanity still owns authorship.
