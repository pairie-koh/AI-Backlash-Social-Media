This is a really important AI legal precedent. The U.S. Copyright Office has said that AI works like a suggestion box. When you input prompts, AI interprets them in its own way. This means that raw AI-created content is not copyrightable. If you tell ChatGPT, write me a story, that output that is raw is not copyrightable. In order to have a better chance of copyright, you need to heavily edit the AI outputs. And your best chance for copyright is if you're using AI as a tool for your film special effects or editing a bunch of AI clips together.
