Yes, AI is that. Recently, somebody tried to derail a conversation about AI being unethical by saying, well, I used ChadGBT last night to find me a list of synonyms for the word something. How is that unethical? And while I would normally say that's a fair question, the reason it's not is the guy knew he was trying to derail a conversation. Nobody has a problem with you using AI like a search engine. It's actually originally what it was built for. What people have a problem with is somebody taking AI, which is trained by the stolen material of other writers, and then using it to generate art and selling that for a profit because you're selling stolen material.
