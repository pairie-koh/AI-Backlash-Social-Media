This is Blue singer Eddie Dalton. He's had at least three songs in the iTunes Top 10 chart this year so far and his album, The Years Between, has reached number three on the iTunes US charts. His music is charting in countries including the UK, Australia, Germany, Canada and the Netherlands. But Eddie Dalton is completely AI generated, from the voice to the visuals. He is the work of South Carolina-based content creator Dallas Ray Little, who has responded to criticism over creating music under a fictional artist. In a statement given to Showbiz411, Little says the videos are clearly labelled as AI-generated and that he wrote all the songs himself. The lyric video for the song Another Day Old already has 1.4 million views on YouTube and the AI-generated artist has over 350,000 monthly listeners on Spotify. The story is now fuelling a wider debate about AI in music and raising questions around authorship, authenticity and creative ownership.
