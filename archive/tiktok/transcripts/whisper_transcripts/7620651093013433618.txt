The first ever AI music fraud case just dropped. A guy from North Carolina generated hundreds of thousands of songs and put them on Spotify, Apple Music, and Amazon. Then he botted billions of streams and walked away with $8 million. 52-year-old Michael Smith already pled guilty to the charges. Under the terms of his plea agreement, he's going to forfeit $8 million and face up to five years in prison. This is one of the first successful prosecutions for AI fraud, and it certainly won't be the last. Thank you.
