More big news from the world of AI and music. Per a new U.S. copyright rule, work solely created by AI cannot be copywritten. But for artists and producers using AI tools to make music, they can copyright as long as there is also sufficient human input. That makes things super clear. That means complete artist replacement is not welcomed while using AI as a tool is protected. This is great news for most musicians worried about being replaced by the robots because now it's clear AI systems cannot hold intellectual property. The thing that remains unsolved is whether AI companies can continue to use copyrighted music to train their AI models without permission or payment. Let me know what you think in the comments.
