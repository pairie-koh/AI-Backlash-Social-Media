I wasted a whole day on Microsoft Copilot so you don't have to. I've been using ChatGPT and Claude for months, but this week, a project finally forced me to try Copilot. One day in and I'm genuinely frustrated. Here's why Copilot is the worst of the three. First, it doesn't think ahead. I asked for PowerPoint automation solutions. ChatGPT didn't just give me options. It started building a brand guide without me asking. That's the kind of proactive thinking I need. CoPilot, just a basic list. No depth, no trade-offs. Nothing to help me actually make a decision. Second, the responses feel lazy. Claude writes like a consultant. ChatGPT feels polished and professional. CoPilot writes like the co-worker who's already mentally checked out. You know that one. And look, all three make mistakes. But when CoPilot's tone is already this bare minimum, I trust it even less when things go wrong.
