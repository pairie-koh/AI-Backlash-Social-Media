tutorial on this trend style. Get a clip then many images ready. Go on your audio then go on beats. Add a beat for all the beats of the audio. Go on your clip and freeze it on the first beat. Go AI image on the frozen clip. Go model then select Nano Banana Pro. Go image settings then put aspect ratio on fit and resolution on 4k. Paste this prompt which I show at end then generate. Once your images have generated split each image according to the beat then delete the right part of the image. Go effects then shake. Make the effect last under one set of beats. Go object then select all videos. Add keyframe at start then put speed and intensity on 100. Add keyframe at end then put both settings on zero. Go graphs then select ease out. Duplicate the effect and slide under next set of beats. If your image lasts over two set of beats, add a keyframe before the middle beat and then a keyframe on the middle beat. Go on second keyframe then zoom into image and rotate it a bit. Go graphs then cubic in. Duplicate any of your images then split the duplicated image and delete the right part so that the image is 0.3 seconds long. Overlay the 0.3 second image and slide at end of previous clip.
