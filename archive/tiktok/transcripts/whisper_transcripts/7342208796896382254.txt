I mean, I use this thing for aviation, which is one of its intended purposes, but to be honest, I would way rather deal with a bigger iPad in the cockpit than dealing with an iPad with no battery. And yes, yes, I could take a battery bank, but then I have this big thing I have to lug around, and then I might as well just get a bigger iPad with a bigger screen.
