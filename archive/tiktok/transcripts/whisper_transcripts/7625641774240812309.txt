Here are five disadvantages of AI for school children. 1. Over-dependence on technology. Children may rely too much on AI tools to answer questions or do tasks, reducing their ability to think critically and solve problems on their own. 2. Reduced creativity. If students let AI generate ideas, essays, or projects, they may not develop their own creativity and originality. 3. Academic dishonesty. AI can make it easier to cheat, for example. generating essays or answers, which affects learning and honesty in school. 4. Limited social skills. Spending too much time interacting with AI instead of real people can reduce communication and teamwork skills. 5. Inaccurate or misleading information. AI is not always correct. Children might trust wrong answers, leading to misunderstandings and per-learning outcomes
