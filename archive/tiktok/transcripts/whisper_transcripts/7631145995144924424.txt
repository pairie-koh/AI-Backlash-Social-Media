I thought it would be interesting to ask ChadGPT for a recipe and see how it pans out in real life. Can ChadGPT actually come up with a winning recipe? My thoughts were probably yes, after all it is genius. But pretty soon I found out why you shouldn't blindly make a ChadGPT recipe. Let's go through what went wrong. The first issue. This did not make 10 to 12 cookies. It made 3. Next up, the texture. No dramatic lava center. Just one bit of caramel that was hard to find. They were also super powdery and very crumbly, not chewy at all. Not exactly what you have in mind for a delicious cookie. And then the biggest issue, the flavour was off. The black cocoa and sesame were unbalanced and the cookie itself was not sweet at all. Which was almost surprising because the smell of the cookies was good so it was kind of deceiving. It just didn't taste very good and I truly wanted to like it but I couldn't.
