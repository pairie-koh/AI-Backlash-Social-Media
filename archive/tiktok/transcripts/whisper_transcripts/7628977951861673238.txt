This teenager is the first person to be charged in Australia for creating deep fake porn. 19-year-old William Hamish Yates was charged under a new national law, which criminalises the manipulation of sexual images. Yates had shared images of his alleged victim across multiple ex-accounts without her consent, the court heard. He didn't comment after he left the court after admitting to four offences on Wednesday. But he'll return for a hearing in April. This type of crime carries a maximum penalty of seven years in prison in the country. He was previously facing 20 Commonwealth charges, but some were withdrawn after he pleaded guilty to creating or altering sexual material without consent and distributing it, as well as using a carriage service in a harassing or offensive way.
