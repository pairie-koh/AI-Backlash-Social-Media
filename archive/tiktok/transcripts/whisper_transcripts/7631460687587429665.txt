There's a tool that makes AI mislearned, and 250,000 people downloaded it in five days. A team of computer scientists at the University of Chicago, led by Professor Ben Zhao, built a tool called Nightshade. It allows artists to add invisible perturbations to their images before uploading them online. To the human eye, nothing changes. But if those images are scraped into an AI training set, the model mislearns. The goal, as the researchers stated, was not to destroy AI, but that paying artists for the work became the only rational and economic choice.
