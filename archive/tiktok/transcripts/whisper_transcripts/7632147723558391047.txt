Indonesia is becoming more efficient but also far more carbon intensive. An Asian Productivity Organization report shows that while energy efficiency has improved sharply since 2000, the country's carbon emissions have more than doubled. At the same time, total factor productivity has declined over the long term, even as labor output improved. Renewables now make up just over a tenth of Indonesia's energy mix. But inefficiencies remain across key sectors like services and agriculture. And as urbanization accelerates and investment grows, manufacturing's share of the economy continues to shrink.
