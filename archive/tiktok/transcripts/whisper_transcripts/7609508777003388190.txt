If you ever get a phone call and there's nobody there and it's just silence, do not say a word and just hang up. Let me explain. A lot of people don't know this, but those silent calls are not accidents. Scammers use them to clone your voice. And it really doesn't take much. A few seconds of you saying hello is enough for AI to clone how you sound. And once they have that, they can call your mom, your bank, whoever, sounding exactly like you. Asking for money, authorizing payments, really whatever they need. So if you pick up the phone and it's quiet, just hang up. Don't say hello, don't say yes, don't say anything. And honestly, if I get a call from an unknown number, I just let it go to voicemail. If it's important enough, they will leave a voicemail. And one more thing, set up a safe word with your family, something only you and them would know. So if they get a call from a scammer asking for money that sounds like you, they know to ask the safe word first, and that just kills the scam right there on the spot.
