Most people won't survive AI layoffs, but here's how you can. The reason is simple. They wait. They wait for the company to train them. They wait for the government to pass laws. They wait until their job is already automated, and then they panic. By then, it's too late. The ones who survive move before they're forced. They learn the tools now, not later. They figure out how to use AI to speed up their own work so they look like an asset, and not a cost. They shift their focus to the parts of their role that AI can't touch, relationships, judgment, you know, original ideas, and they make their results visible so leadership can't ignore the value that they bring. Most people will treat AI like a threat until it replaces them. The few who survive will treat it like an ally before it does. That's the difference between being in the layoff list and being the person that they keep.
