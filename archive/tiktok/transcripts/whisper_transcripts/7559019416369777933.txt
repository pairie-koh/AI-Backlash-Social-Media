Imagine getting a call from your daughter's voice, crying, begging you to wire somebody because she's in trouble. Would you stop to question if it was really her? This is the new frontier of scams, AI deepfakes, and voice cloning. All scammers need is just a few seconds of a voice recording, and then they can generate a voice clone convincing enough to fool parents, spouses, and even business partners. They'll say, Dad, I'm in trouble. I need money right now. And your brain shuts down. Fear hijacks your logic. The psychology at play here, it's familiarity and it's a sense of urgency. You hear a trusted voice, someone you care about. They're desperate. They need immediate help. And so what do you do? You act without thinking it through.
