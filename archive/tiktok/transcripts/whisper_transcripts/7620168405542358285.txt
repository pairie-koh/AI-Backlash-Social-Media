Did you know that every time you open your overstuffed fridge, you're feeling echoes from 400 years ago? In 1609, Jamestown colonists were so hungry, they ate shoe leather and even their own family members. This starving time was so brutal that it literally changed how Americans think about food forever. That's why we have the biggest portion sizes on earth and why we panic buy groceries before storms.
