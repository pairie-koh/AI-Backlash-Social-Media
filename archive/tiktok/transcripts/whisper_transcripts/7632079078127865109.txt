Is ChatGPT wiping your memory? Scientists say, yes. Your brain on AI might be lazier than you think. New research suggests that leaning too hard on ChatGPT could decrease key brain functions by up to 55%. Here's why your digital assistant might actually be a cognitive assassin. The experiment. The soulless essay. MIT research scientist Natalia Kosmina tracked 54 students writing essays. She split them into three groups, AI users, search users, and old school, no tech at all. The shocking result, the no tech group's brains were on fire. But the AI group, their brain activity plummeted by 55%. They weren't just being efficient, they were checking out. When tested later, they couldn't even recall quotes from their own papers. The brain didn't fall asleep, but there was much less activation in areas for creativity and processing. Natalia Kosmina, MIT, the rise of cognitive surrender.
