We hear about the AI bubble all the time, but what actually is it and why should you care? Most people just think that the AI bubble just means too many AI startups and too much hype around AI. But the part about the AI bubble that's actually really concerning and that not enough people are talking about is that most companies don't actually run their own AI and are actually hyper-reliant on other companies' models. That means almost every AI startup is reliant on another AI company to function. So if the company below them changes a price, removes a feature, or shuts down access, the company above them breaks. Kind of like a giant Jenga tower where at the base we have OpenAI, Anthropic, NVIDIA, AWS, companies that everyone depends on in the ecosystem. If even one bottom block moves, the whole tower shakes. That's why this bubble is considered to be so weak. Because hundreds of AI startups aren't actually building AI themselves. No original model, no data, no infrastructure, and no mode. So what does this all mean for people like you and I? It means that the AI tools that you now use every day, your bank apps, healthcare, school tools, work tools, search engines, is built on a shaky stack that can disappear if the companies at the bottom disappear. Putting your data, daily routines, and essential systems at risk. That is why the AI bubble matters.
