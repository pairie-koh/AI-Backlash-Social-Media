DeepSeek entered the AI race with a bang last year. Now it's back with a brand new model. The Chinese startup has just unveiled its V4 Flash and V4 Pro models. Models it says are the most powerful open source AI models in the world, posing a challenge arguably to competitors ranging from anthropic to open AI. They're claiming top tier coding performance, major leaps in reasoning and more advanced agent-style capabilities, all powered by upgraded architecture and heavy optimization. There is a catch. Capacity is tight. DeepSeq says access to its V4 Pro model is limited, at least for now, because of a chip crunch. But it expects prices to drop sharply once new clusters powered by Huawei's Ascend 950 chips come online later this year. Remember when DeepSeq first sent shockwaves through the global markets. Its earlier model delivered performance in the same league as ChatGPT, but at a fraction of the cost. That pricing gap triggered panic across Silicon Valley and raised serious questions about whether the AI boom was being wildly overpriced. The backlash came quickly. US tech companies and politicians accused DeepSeq of distilling or borrowing from American models to jump ahead, turning a tech story into a geopolitical one. Now with V4, DeepSeek isn't just trying to compete, it's trying to reset the playing field. Again.
