The way consumers find agents is changing fast. A year ago, if somebody was looking for an agent in their area, they would have Googled it. Not anymore. Today, a vast majority of people are going to ChatGPT. They're going to Perplexity. They are going to Gemini. They are going to their AI of choice, and they are searching for their real estate agents or tips on how to sell their home. They are asking real estate questions, and the rate of adoption for AI search is through the roof. And they're asking whatever AI assistant is on their phone at the time. But here's the problem. If your digital presence is not built the right way, AI is not going to give those potential clients and prospects your name because you don't exist in those results yet.
