AI is already changing the workplace faster than most people expected. The jobs most likely to be affected first are repetitive and predictable roles. Tasks like data entry can often be done faster by AI systems. Simple customer support questions are already being handled by chatbots. Routine admin work, scheduling, and filing are also easy for AI to automate. Bookkeeping, proofreading, and telemarketing are also under pressure from automation. Jobs that need empathy, physical skill, or real-world judgment are harder to replace. The smartest move now is not to fear AI, but to learn how to use it. People who adapt early will have the best chance of staying ahead in the future of work.
