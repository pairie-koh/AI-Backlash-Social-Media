Is AI objective? I'm Dr. Avril Epps and this is AI Mythbusting. People love to say AI is objective. It's just math. But let's pause right there because that is mostly a myth. AI doesn't come from a magical land of neutrality. AI comes from people, histories, assumptions, blind spots, and cultural context. AI systems don't start off unbiased. AI inherits the biases of the humans who build it. Some systems recognize lighter skinned faces more accurately because they were trained mostly on lighter skinned people. That's not objectivity. It's just a representation imbalance. Just because AI isn't objective doesn't mean it has to stay unfair. We actually have really powerful tools to make it better. So the next time you hear someone say AI is objective, you can confidently say, no, it's not. It actually can't be. AI is power, but power is shaped. And we get to shape it.
