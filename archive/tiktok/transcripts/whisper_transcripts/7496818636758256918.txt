Taipei girlies, bullet journal lovers and planning freaks, here's how you can use ChatGPT to generate aesthetic journal pages in just a few seconds. At this point you probably already know that you can use ChatGPT to plan your day down to the minute, help with your time blocking and your prioritization. But did you know that you can now get it to turn those daily plans into actual bullet journal pages complete with doodles, colors and your own aesthetic vibe? Like these, I didn't draw them, I didn't use Canva. I just gave ChatGPT a single custom prompt and there we go. Custom, cute and very type A coded. After I got my schedule from ChatGPT I just prompted it to generate an image of a bullet journal page and asked it to customize it with a theme like kawaii for example, hand-drawn doodles or a specific color scheme if you want. You can also customize the title at the top or ask it to add a date. The heck I wish I had years ago when I was spending hours hand lettering just to maybe get myself to stick to a schedule.
